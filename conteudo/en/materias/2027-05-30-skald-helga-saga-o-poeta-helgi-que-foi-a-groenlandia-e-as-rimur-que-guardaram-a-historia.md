Skáld-Helga saga, the "saga of the poet Helgi", is a lost saga of Icelanders, set in Iceland, Norway and Greenland, between about 1000 and 1050.

## The hero

The protagonist is Helgi Þórðarson, a poet. He travels to Greenland and becomes a prominent figure at Brattahlíð.

## A text that survived in poems

The content of the saga first appears in the Skáld-Helga rímur, narrative poems of the 14th century. The rímur were rewritten as prose in the modern period, and the saga is also known through an account by Gísli Konráðsson, of about 1820. So what we have is a saga reconstructed from late texts, not a medieval prose manuscript.

## Editions and translation

Sigfús Eymundsson published the text in 1897, and there is an edition by Guðni Jónsson. In English, Alan Boucher translated the story in 1983, in the same volume as Gunnlaugs saga ormstungu.
