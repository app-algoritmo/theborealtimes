Thorfinn Karlsefni, an Icelandic trader, is the leader of an expedition to Vinland that two sagas tell differently. Around the year 1000 the Norse had already reached lands west of Greenland, and Karlsefni tries to stay. What is known of him comes only from the two sagas, and they do not agree on everything.

## Who Karlsefni is

Karlsefni (c. 980 to after 1020) was an Icelandic explorer and trader. He married, in Greenland, at Brattahlíð, Gudrid, the widow of Thorstein Eiriksson, Leif's brother. In the *Grænlendinga saga*, she is the one who urges him to go to Vinland. Their son Snorri was born in Vinland and is the first child of European descent known to have been born in the New World.

## Two different numbers

The *Eiríks saga rauða* tells of a larger expedition, with three ships and about 140 men, and translations vary between 140 and 160. The *Grænlendinga saga* speaks of 60 men and five women. The *Eiríks saga* also credits Karlsefni with naming many places, which the *Grænlendinga saga* credits to Leif.

## The meeting with the skrælings

In the *Eiríks saga*, the settlers trade with the natives, the skrælings, until a bull breaks loose and frightens them. They return three weeks later with hostile intent, and there is a fight. In that episode Freydís, Erik's daughter, saves the men by baring her breast and slapping it with a sword, which scares the natives off. In the *Grænlendinga saga*, the bull also frightens the natives, but the settlers offer them milk, and the natives fall ill, and fighting begins. Freydís appears there on an expedition of her own, which ends in violence.

## How long they stayed

The reference text consulted says the settlers stayed about three years in what is now Newfoundland, and the colony was abandoned around 1006 according to one date and around 1010 according to another. The exact date is not known.

## Where Vinland was

No one knows the exact location of Vinland. The site of L'Anse aux Meadows, in Newfoundland, proves a Norse presence in Canada, and it may have been the colony, but the sagas do not allow us to say so.

## In short

Karlsefni is known from two versions that contradict each other in numbers, place names and episodes. What both say in common is that an Icelandic trader tried to stay in Vinland and came back.
