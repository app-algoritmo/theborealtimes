Sigyn is Loki's wife, and the sources say little about her. Almost everything we know comes from one scene: Loki is bound and she stays at his side. This text separates what the texts say from modern reading.

## Loki's family

According to the Prose Edda, Sigyn and Loki have a son called Nari or Narfi. Another passage also names Váli, who is described as Loki's son. The sources explain no more about the marriage. In kennings, Loki can be called "Sigyn's husband", according to the *Skáldskaparmál*.

## Loki's binding

After insulting the gods at the feast of the *Lokasenna*, Loki flees and hides. The gods find him and bind him. The prose that closes the *Lokasenna* and the *Gylfaginning* tell that they seized Loki's sons, Váli and Narfi. Váli was turned into a wolf and tore his brother apart. With Narfi's entrails the gods tied Loki over three stones, and the entrails turned to iron. The giantess Skaði hung a venomous serpent above his face so that the venom would drip.

## The bowl

Here Sigyn comes in. The *Lokasenna* says she stayed beside her husband and held a basin under the drops of venom. When the basin filled, she stepped away to empty it, and at that moment the venom fell on Loki's face. He writhed so hard that the earth shook. According to Snorri, this is how earthquakes are explained. Loki would stay bound until Ragnarök.

The *Völuspá* shows the same picture in a few words: the seeress says that Sigyn sat there, and that she was not at all happy about her husband's fate.

## The Gosforth Cross

In Cumbria, England, the Gosforth Cross, from the 10th century, has a scene in which a woman holds a bowl over a figure lying down. Some scholars read it as Sigyn and Loki. If so, it is one of the few signs that the scene was known outside Scandinavia. The cross is Christian, and the reading is an interpretation.

## In short

Sigyn is the faithful wife who stays with Loki when everyone abandons him. What the sources say of her fits in one scene: the bowl, the venom and the trembling of the earth. Outside that scene, almost nothing is known.
