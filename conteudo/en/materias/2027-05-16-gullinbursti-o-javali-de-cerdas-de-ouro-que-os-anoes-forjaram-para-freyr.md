Gullinbursti means "golden bristles" or "golden mane". It is the boar of the god Freyr, and the Prose Edda tells how it was made: by dwarves, in a furnace, from a pig's skin.

## The wager that produced it

In the Skáldskaparmál, the same episode that gives Odin the ring Draupnir and Thor the hammer gives Freyr the boar. Loki bets his head with the dwarf Brokkr that his brother Sindri cannot make treasures equal to those of the sons of Ivaldi: Sif's hair, Freyr's ship Skíðblaðnir and Odin's spear.

## In the furnace

To make Freyr's gifts, the brothers put a pig's skin in the furnace. Brokkr works the bellows, and Sindri keeps the fire until the boar is finished. In the middle of the work a fly stings the hand of the dwarf who is blowing, but he does not stop. When the boar comes out, Brokkr gives it to Freyr.

## What the boar does

According to the text, Gullinbursti runs through air and water better than any horse, and the bristles of its mane shine so brightly that they light the way in the darkness. It is an animal of light and speed, not of combat.

## In the other sources

The poem Húsdrápa says Freyr rode to Baldr's funeral on Gullinbursti. In the Gylfaginning, Snorri tells that Freyr travelled in a chariot pulled by the boar. The animal has another name too, Slíðrugtanni, "sharp tooth" or "fearsome tooth".

## In short

Gullinbursti is a treasure of the forge, with an owner, a fire and a dark night that it lights up. To run with it over water and through air, play [Gullinbursti runs over the water](/game-gullinbursti-runs-over-the-water/).
