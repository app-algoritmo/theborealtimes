Þórðar saga hreðu is a late Icelandic saga, of about 1350, about a man who flees Norway and about the sword Skofnung, one of the most famous weapons in Icelandic literature.

## The fugitive

Þórðr hreða leaves Norway, on the run. The journey takes him to Iceland, where the story unfolds among farms and local chieftains.

## Skeggi and Skofnung

Skeggi has the sword Skofnung in his hands. Around him appear Eiðr, Ásbjörn and Sigríðr, and a sequence of attacks leaves a trail of dead. Ǫzurr is killed. Þórhallr is killed by Skeggi, with Skofnung.

## Sǫrli's combat

The saga also has a single combat of Sǫrli, one of the moments where the text pauses, as often happens in sagas, to describe a one-on-one fight in detail.

## To read

The modern edition is that of Jon Hagland, from 2004.

It is the saga of the sword and the flight, by a late author who mixes the elements of the classic sagas. To parry every blow with the shield, play [The sword Skofnung](/game-the-sword-skofnung/).
