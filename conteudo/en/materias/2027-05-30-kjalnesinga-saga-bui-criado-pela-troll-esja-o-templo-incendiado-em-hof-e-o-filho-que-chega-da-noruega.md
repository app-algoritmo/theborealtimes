Kjalnesinga saga is an Icelandic saga of the 14th century, set on the Kjalarnes peninsula, near Mount Esja, and tells the life of a young man who does not fit in.

## The family

Búi Andríðsson is the son of Andríð, a Christian settler from Ireland, and of Úríðr, a Norse woman. Andríð founded the farm of Brautarholt. Búi is raised by the troll Esja, in a cave on Mount Esja. He is withdrawn, refuses to sacrifice to the gods and carries a sling instead of a sword.

## The temple

Þorsteinn, son of Þorgrímr, the priest of the temple at Hof, accuses Búi of illegal forestry and of "wrong religion". At twelve, Búi is outlawed. Búi kills Þorsteinn in the temple and burns it. Esja hides him, and Þorgrímr has Andríð killed, who is buried at Andríðsey.

## The king and the son

On learning of the fire, King Harald Fairhair sends Búi to the jǫtunn Dofri, to fetch a game board. There Búi meets Fríðr, Dofri's daughter, and spends Yule. She becomes pregnant and says she will send the boy at twelve. Búi kills, at the king's request, a "blue man" and returns to Iceland after three years. Twelve years later his son Jökull arrives, and the two fight. Jökull throws Búi down, who dies three nights later. Helga, Þorgrímr's daughter and Búi's wife, lives at Esjaberg and buries him by a church wall.
