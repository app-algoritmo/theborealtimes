Valla-Ljóts saga is an Icelandic saga of the 13th century, set in Svarfaðardalur, in the north of Iceland, during and after the introduction of Christianity.

## A continuation

It can be read as the continuation of Svarfdæla saga, which is set in the same region.

## The two sides

At the centre is Ljót Ljótólfsson, chieftain of Völlum in Svarfaðardalur, and his opponent, Guðmundr inn ríki Eyjólfsson, "the mighty", the same chieftain who appears in Ljósvetninga saga.

## The text and the studies

The bilingual edition, in Icelandic and English, by Peter A. Jorgensen, from 1991, is based on manuscript AM 161 fol. Among the references for the saga is the study "The Old Icelandic Duel", by Marlene Ciklamini, from 1963. The full text can be read in the Icelandic Saga Database (sagadb.org).

## In short

It is a short saga about a valley chieftain facing a powerful man, in the time when religion was changing. To gather the parchment leaves, play [The parchment leaves](/game-the-parchment-leaves/).
