Erling Ormsson was born around 1115 in Etne, in Hordaland, son of Orm Sveinsson, of the Kyrpinga family. He married Kristin Sigurdsdatter, daughter of King Sigurd the Crusader. It is through the marriage that his son Magnus would have some claim to the throne, and it is through Erling's political talent that this claim became a crown.

## The crusade and the tilted neck

Between 1152 and 1155, Erling went with Rögnvald Kali Kolsson, earl of Orkney, to the Mediterranean. He sailed to the Holy Land, then to Constantinople and Rome. In a battle against Arab warriors in Sicily, an opponent with a sword cut his neck. After that, he kept his head tilted to one side. *Skakke* means "slanted" or "tilted".

## The guardian and the regent

Erling was guardian of King Inge I. When Inge died, in 1161, he took over the leadership of the party of the *lendmenn* and had his son Magnus elected king, still a child. The source says Magnus was crowned in 1163, at eight. Erling took the title of jarl and held the real power while his son was a minor. He remained the effective ruler even after Magnus grew up.

## Rydjokul and Stanger

In 1166, while Erling was in Denmark, Sigurd Agnhatt and his foster son Olav Ugjæva raised men in Oppland and proclaimed Olav king. He was the son of Maria Øysteinsdotter, a daughter of King Eystein I. On 2 February 1167, Olav and his men ambushed Erling at Rydjokul, in Sørum. Erling was wounded and barely escaped. In 1168, Olav's forces were defeated at Stanger, near Våler. Sigurd died and Olav fled to Denmark.

## A monastery and a rival

In 1164, Erling founded Halsnøy Abbey, of Augustinian canons, on the island of Halsnøya in the Hardangerfjord. When Sverre Sigurdsson took over the leadership of the *Birkebeiner*, Erling's position weakened. He fell in the battle of Kalvskinnet, outside Nidaros, in 1179. The reference text does not say that Sverre killed him. It says Sverre gave a speech at his funeral, in the church, honouring the fallen opponent.

## What remains

The source describes Erling as talented but not always noble, and says his enemies were ruthless when his own position or his sons' was at stake. He was born around 1115 and died on 18 June 1179. The crusade that twisted his neck, the crowned son and the ambush in the snow show a career politician who crossed a civil war without ever being king.
