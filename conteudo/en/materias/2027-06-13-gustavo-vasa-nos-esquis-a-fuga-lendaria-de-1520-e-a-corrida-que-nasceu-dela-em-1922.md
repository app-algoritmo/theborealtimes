Gustav Vasa became king of Sweden on 6 June 1523. Before that, according to legend, he fled on skis from the Danish king's soldiers. The source warns that it cites no contemporary document and that the episode is disputed. The date, besides, varies: winter 1520–21 or 1520.

## The legend

Gustav's father, Erik Johansson, was executed. The men of Mora refused to join him, and Gustav headed west, toward Norway. Afterwards, hearing of new taxes, the Mora men sent two brothers, Lars and Engelbrekt, the best skiers, who caught him at Sälen. He turned back.

## The king

On 6 June 1523 Gustav became king.

## The race

The idea of the race was published on 10 February 1922 by Anders Pers. The club IFK Mora approved the plan on 5 March. The first Vasaloppet was on 19 March 1922: 136 registered, 119 started, and Ernst Alm, aged 22, won in 7h32min49s. The newspaper Dagens Nyheter sponsored it with a thousand kronor. The course is 90 km, from Berga, near Sälen, to Mora, the opposite way to the legend. The article describes it as the oldest cross-country ski race in the world.

To glide on skis, pick up the provisions and dodge the spears, play [The flight on skis](/game-the-flight-on-skis/).
