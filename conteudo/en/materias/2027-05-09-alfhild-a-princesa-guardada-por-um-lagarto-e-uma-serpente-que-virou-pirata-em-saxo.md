Alfhild, or Alvilda, is another warrior woman of Saxo. Her story, in Book VII of the Gesta Danorum, has a fairy-tale beginning, a middle of sea adventure and a royal wedding for an end.

## What Saxo tells

According to the sources, Alfhild is a Geatish princess, daughter of King Siward. Her chamber was guarded by a lizard and a snake, which drove off unworthy suitors. The Danish prince Alf overcame the two guards, but Alfhild, advised by her mother, fled dressed as a man and became a shieldmaiden.

She commanded a fleet of longships crewed by young women pirates, who raided the Baltic coast. Alf and his Scanian companion Borgar found the fleet off southern Finland. After a deadly battle, Alfhild's helmet fell off and she was recognised. Alf embraced her, she gave up her warrior clothes, and they married in Denmark.

## After the wedding

The story goes on with war. Alf, his brothers and his father, King Sigar, die fighting a rebellious Danish clan. The daughter of Alf and Alfhild, Gurid, survives, marries Borgar's son Halfdan, and bears Harald, who becomes king of Denmark.

## Origin of the story

The source says that Saxo's version is the oldest and was based on "one of the old folktales or songs" he gathered. Saxo's details place the events in the fifth century, but that claim carries a citation-needed tag in the article. There is little interpretation beyond that.

## Later readings

The story inspired works over the centuries. Among them are the opera L'Amazzone Corsara by Carlo Pallavicino (1686), Vivaldi's opera Alvilda, regina de' Goti (1731), Tasso's tragedy Il re Torrismondo, in which Alvida kills herself rather than choose between love and honour, and even nineteenth-century whalers' scrimshaw. This shows how the idea of a woman pirate crossed the ages, more than it proves she existed.

## In short

Alfhild is a character of Saxo, who takes her out of a tale of animal guardians and makes her the captain of a fleet. The story ends in a genealogy: Alfhild's daughter bears a king of Denmark, which ties Saxo's royal house to a heroic past.
