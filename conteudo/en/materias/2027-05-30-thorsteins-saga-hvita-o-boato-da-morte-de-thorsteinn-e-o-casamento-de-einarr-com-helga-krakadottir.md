Þorsteins saga hvíta is an Icelandic saga of the 13th century, set in the 9th century, in the east of Iceland, in the Vopnafjörður region.

## The rumour

Einarr Þórisson was a friend of Þorsteinn hvíti, "the White". To marry his betrothed, Helga Krákadóttir, Einarr spreads the rumour that Þorsteinn has died. Here the weapon is the rumour, not the sword.

## The revenge

When Þorsteinn finds out, he takes revenge on his former friend. The saga says the revenge had tragic consequences, and it is in them that the weight of the narrative lies.

## Studies

Works that deal with the saga include "Eddas and Sagas" by Jónas Kristjánsson (1997) and "Feud in the Icelandic Saga" by Jesse Byock (1993). In 2020 Christopher Crocker published in the journal Gripla an article on blindness and seeing in Þorsteins saga hvíta.
