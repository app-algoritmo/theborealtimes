Vápnfirðinga saga is an Icelandic saga about the people of Vopnafjörður, in the east of Iceland, and the disputes that divided the families of the region.

## Two rivals

The plot begins with Brodd-Helgi and Geitir, two chieftains who become rivals. There is a curse laid by Svart, a woman called Halla and another, Thorgerd Silver, and a prophetic dream that announces what is coming. Bjarni kills Geitir.

## Thorkel's revenge

Thorkel, who wants to avenge Geitir's death, follows the path of the dispute. The two sides meet in a hayfield, at Eyvindastaðir, where the men fight among heaps of cut hay. Thorvard, a healer, appears in this stage of the story.

## The peace

The fight ends with compensation in money: a hundred pieces of silver, which shows how the law could end a conflict when both sides accepted.

## The text

The saga was preserved in late paper manuscripts and on a vellum leaf. William Miller and Lönnroth, among others, studied it, and John Tucker translated it into English in 1997.
