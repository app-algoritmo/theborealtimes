Víglundar saga is a later Icelandic saga, from the late 14th or early 15th century, and is closer to a love story than to a chronicle of revenge.

## The parents' elopement

It begins with the elopement of Þorgrímr and Olof, who love each other and run away together. That is the earlier generation, and the children's story grows out of this beginning.

## Víglundr and Ketilríðr

Víglundr and Ketilríðr love each other. But a vendetta is under way, driven by Ketill, which threatens Víglundr's life. The saga shows the young man hunted and the young woman who will not leave him.

## The haystack ambush

One of the most remembered moments is the ambush set in a haystack. His enemies wait for Víglundr, and it is there that the confrontation reaches its critical point. Hákon appears in the plot as a figure of authority.

## Four weddings

Sigurðr and Gunnlaugr, who had been on the other side, change their minds. In the end the saga closes with a quadruple wedding, and the enmities dissolve.

## Text and echoes

The saga is known from two manuscripts of the 15th century, among them AM 551 A 4to. It inspired the opera Thorgrim, of 1890.

## In short

Víglundar saga swaps the grim ending of so many sagas for a festive finish. To raise the haystack block by block, play [The ambush haystack](/game-the-ambush-haystack/).
