Odin is the Norse god everyone knows, but the sources remind us that he did not act alone at the start of the world. Snorri Sturluson names two brothers of his, Vili and Vé, who almost never appear outside a trio. This text gathers the little that is written about the two and separates what the sources say from what scholars suppose.

## The three sons of Borr

In the *Gylfaginning*, the first part of the Prose Edda, Odin, Vili and Vé are the sons of Borr and the giantess Bestla. The three kill the giant Ymir and make the world from his body. This is how Snorri tells the creation. Vili and Vé appear there as Odin's partners in that task, with no lines and no deeds of their own.

## The gifts to the first couple

Afterwards, the brothers find two trees by the sea and make from them the first man, Ask, and the first woman, Embla. According to the *Gylfaginning*, each brother gives some gifts. Odin gives breath and life. Vili gives understanding and one more gift that translations render as motion or feeling, because the original word, *hræring*, allows both readings. Vé gives the face, speech, hearing and sight.

## The same gifts, other names

In the *Völuspá*, the poem Snorri knew, the ones who give the gifts to Ask and Embla are a trio made of Odin, Hœnir and Lóðurr, and the gifts have other names. For this reason it is thought that Hœnir and Lóðurr may be other names for Vili and Vé. The page I consulted presents this idea as a hypothesis and notes that Snorri knew the poem.

## Frigg, according to Loki and according to the saga

The two brothers also appear in an accusation. In the *Lokasenna*, Loki tells Frigg, Odin's wife, that she lay with Vili and Vé. This is a line of insult, spoken at a feast where Loki attacks all the gods, and not a neutral report.

In the *Ynglinga saga*, the first of the kings' sagas in the *Heimskringla*, Snorri treats Odin as a human chieftain who spends a long time away from home. The brothers rule, divide his property and divide Frigg as well. When Odin returns, he takes his wife back. The scholar Jacob Grimm saw in this a sign that the three brothers were basically the same figure, so that Frigg could be seen as the wife of any of them, according to the page consulted.

## In short

Vili and Vé are two brothers of Odin who help to kill Ymir, to make the world and to give life to Ask and Embla. No source gives them deeds of their own, and in the *Völuspá* they may appear under other names.

## Terms

*Gylfaginning* is the first part of the Prose Edda, in which King Gylfi asks the gods questions. *Völuspá* is a poem of the Poetic Edda in which a seeress speaks of the beginning and the end of the world. *Lokasenna* is the poem in which Loki insults the gods.

## What remains uncertain

The identification of Vili and Vé with Hœnir and Lóðurr is a hypothesis of scholars. The word *hræring* can mean motion or feeling, and so Vili's gift has two readings. Loki's line in the *Lokasenna* is an accusation made in a poem of insults, and the *Ynglinga saga* is a work written centuries after the time it describes.
