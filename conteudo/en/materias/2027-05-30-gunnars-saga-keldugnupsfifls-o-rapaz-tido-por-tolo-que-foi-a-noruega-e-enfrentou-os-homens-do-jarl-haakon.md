Gunnars saga Keldugnúpsfífls is a late Icelandic saga, from the 15th and 16th centuries, about a lad whom everyone takes for a fool. The nickname in the title means, roughly, the "fool of Keldugnúpur".

## The fool

Gunnar is considered a fool. That puts him in a position that similar sagas like to explore: the one everyone underestimates, who ends up showing more than was expected.

## The journey

With his brother Helgi, Gunnar goes to Norway. There he faces and defeats one of Jarl Haakon's men, the powerful ruler of Norway in the 10th century, and the victory brings him fame. He ends up marrying.

## To read

The saga was translated into English by Anderson, in 1997.

## In short

It is a short story of reversal, in which the "fool" turns out more capable than he seemed. To run along the deck and the lanes of the voyage, play [The voyage to Norway](/game-the-voyage-to-norway/).
