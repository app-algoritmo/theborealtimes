Freydís Eiríksdóttir is, according to the source consulted for this piece, "said to be" the daughter of Erik the Red, born around 965. Everything known of her comes from two medieval sagas, which give different accounts. This piece tells each one separately, points out where they contradict each other and keeps to what the article carries.

## In the Saga of the Greenlanders

In chapter 8, Freydís wants the prestige and wealth of a voyage to Vinland, after the expeditions of Leif, Þorvaldr Eiríksson and Þorfinnr Karlsefni. The article places Vinland in present-day Newfoundland and Labrador. She makes a deal with the Icelandic brothers Helgi and Finnbogi: they would sail together and split the profits equally. Leif allowed them to use his houses in Vinland, but the brothers arrived first and occupied them, and Freydís ordered them out, saying the houses were meant for her.

## The extra crew and the quarrel

Freydís had secretly brought more men on her ship, which broke the agreement that both sides would bring equal numbers. The groups quarrelled and set up separate settlements. A later visit by Freydís led to a peace, but the saga moves on to violence.

## The killings

Freydís told her husband that the brothers had beaten her and demanded revenge, threatening to divorce him if he refused. He killed Helgi, Finnbogi and the men in their camp while they slept. When her men refused to kill the five women in the camp, Freydís herself killed them with an axe. She then threatened death to anyone who told, and returned to Greenland about a year later.

## The return

She told Leif that Helgi and Finnbogi had chosen to stay in Vinland. Leif learned the truth after torturing three of her men, who confessed. He did not want to punish her, but he predicted that her descendants would prosper little. The saga says people came to think poorly of her descendants.

## In the Saga of Erik the Red

This saga calls her a notable, strong woman and Leif's half-sister, whereas the Saga of the Greenlanders makes her his full sister. Freydís joins Karlsefni's expedition and appears only once, in an attack by the Skrælingar, who used slings or catapults. Many men panicked. She, eight months pregnant, rebuked them and asked for a weapon. Ignored, she took the sword of the fallen Thorbrand Snorrisson and fought. She exposed her chest and struck it with the blade, and the attackers fled to their boats. Karlsefni and the survivors praised her courage. This saga does not mention the killings of the other account.

## Terms

*Vinland* is the Norse name for the land west of Greenland. *Skrælingar* is the sagas' word for the Indigenous peoples encountered. The *Saga of the Greenlanders* and the *Saga of Erik the Red* are the two sources. *Karlsefni* is the leader of one of the expeditions.

## What remains uncertain

The article says Freydís is "said to be" Erik's daughter, and the medieval sagas are the only evidence of her. The two sagas contradict each other about her kinship with Leif, half-sister or full sister, and about her character and deeds. The claim that the Saga of Erik the Red was written after the other appears in the article without a citation.
