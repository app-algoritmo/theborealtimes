Haustlöng is a skaldic poem composed around the beginning of the 10th century by the Norwegian skald Þjóðólfr of Hvinir. The name means "autumn-long", and may refer to the time the poet took to compose it. It is a poem about a shield.

## The shield

According to the poem, Þjóðólfr received the shield from a man named Thorleif. The shield had mythological scenes painted on it, and the poem describes them. The descriptions are cryptic: the shield and the figures appear almost always through kennings, the metaphors of skaldic poetry.

## First scene: Iðunn and Þjazi

In stanzas 1 to 13, Odin, Loki and Hœnir meet the giant Þjazi, who is in eagle form. Loki strikes the eagle with a staff, and the staff sticks to both the bird and his hand. Þjazi lets him go only if Loki delivers Iðunn and her apples, which the poem calls the gods' "old-age medicine". Þjazi takes Iðunn, the Æsir grow old and Loki is forced to bring her back. The poem is more cryptic than the account in the Prose Edda, which adds Freyja's falcon cloak, the wind sent by the giants and Þjazi's death in a bonfire.

## Second scene: Thor and Hrungnir

In stanzas 14 to 20, the poem shows Thor's journey to his duel with the giant Hrungnir, and the whole cosmos reacts. The two fight by hurling their weapons: Hrungnir's whetstone and Thor's hammer. The poem alludes to a piece of the whetstone that ends up in Thor's head. Þjóðólfr gives more attention to Thor's journey, with noise and flames. Snorri gives it less weight and also describes Hrungnir's journey. Thor's servant Þjálfi and the clay giant Mokkurkálfi do not appear in the poet's version.

## What survived

The poem has come down to us mainly in the Prose Edda, in the 13th century, where Snorri quotes two groups of stanzas and other verses to illustrate skaldic diction. Snorri also used the poem when retelling these myths. Two verses he attributed to Þjóðólfr were later identified as the work of the 9th-century skald Þorbjörn Hornklofi.

## In short

Haustlöng is a poem about a painting, and it is one of the oldest sources for the two stories. To defend yourself from the splinters of Hrungnir's stone, play [Hrungnir's splinters](/game-hrungnir-s-splinters/).
