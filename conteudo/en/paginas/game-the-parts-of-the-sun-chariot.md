## How to play

The Trundholm sun chariot was found in 1902 in a peat bog, and in 1998 another 21 parts turned up. Here you stack bronze blocks with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the base in the bog. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Trundholm sun chariot, of about 1400 BC, was found in 1902 in a peat bog on Zealand and has a bronze horse and a disc with one gilded face. The stacked blocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
