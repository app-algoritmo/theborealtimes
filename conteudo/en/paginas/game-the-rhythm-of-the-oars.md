## How to play

Norwegian fishermen said the sea serpent appeared in the calms. Here you mark the rhythm of the oars to escape it. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to row with the crew. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Hans Egede described in 1734 a monster seen off Greenland, and Bishop Erik Pontoppidan collected fishermen's reports of the sjøorm in his natural history of Norway, published in 1752 and 1753. There is no proof of an unknown sea serpent. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Sjøorm: the sea serpent of Norway, the fishermen's reports, Egede's description and Bishop Pontoppidan](/sjoorm-the-sea-serpent-of-norway-the-fishermen-s-reports/).
