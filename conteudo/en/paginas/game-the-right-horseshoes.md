## How to play

Before each trial, Dapplegrim asks for different horseshoes. The trial appears at the top of the screen. Tap the right horseshoes before the time for the request runs out: the small ones, of silver and iron, for the wall up to the troll's cave; the heavy dark ones for the ridge that blocks the sun; the largest ones, with a sack of supplies, for the journey to hell. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. After each trial, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a trial left unanswered, is a mistake. Prepare 10 trials before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, Dapplegrim asks for iron and silver horseshoes to climb the wall up to the troll's cave, heavier horseshoes to jump on the ridge, and even larger ones, with supplies, to go to hell. The order of the requests, the sizes and the swaps of place are invention.
