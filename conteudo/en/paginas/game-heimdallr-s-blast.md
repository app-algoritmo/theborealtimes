## How to play

In the Gylfaginning, Heimdallr blows the Gjallarhorn at Ragnarök and the gods awake and assemble. Here you blow in rhythm. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to give a blast on the horn. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Gylfaginning, at Ragnarök the enemies gather on the plain of Vígríðr and Heimdallr blows the horn mightily, so that the gods awake and assemble at the thing. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
