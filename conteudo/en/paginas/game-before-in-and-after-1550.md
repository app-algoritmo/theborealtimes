## How to play

Jón Arason was beheaded in 1550. Take each phrase to the right box: before 1550, in 1550 or after 1550. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Jón Arason, Catholic bishop of Hólar, was captured at Sauðafell and beheaded in 1550 with his sons Ari and Björn; the source gives the death on 7 November. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
