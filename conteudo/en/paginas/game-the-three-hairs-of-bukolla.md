## How to play

Búkolla helps the boy escape the trolls: each hair from her tail becomes an obstacle. Watch the order in which the river, the fire and the mountain rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the objects in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong object is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, three hairs from Búkolla's tail become a river, a wall of fire and a mountain, and the big troll ends up stuck in the rock. The order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [Búkolla: the Icelandic tale of the magic cow and the boy who outran two trolls with three hairs](/bukolla-the-icelandic-tale-of-the-magic-cow-and-the-boy/).
