## How to play

Ingigerðr's life runs through Sweden, Ladoga and Kievan Rus. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 10 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, Ingigerðr was born in Sweden, received Ladoga as a wedding gift and lived in Kievan Rus as the wife of Yaroslav the Wise. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
