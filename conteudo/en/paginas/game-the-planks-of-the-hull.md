## How to play

The Nydam boat is 23 metres long, with an oak hull of overlapping planks. Here, the hull rises plank by plank. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the hull. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the Nydam boat is 23 metres long, with an oak clinker hull and 14 pairs of oars, and was dated to 310 to 320 CE. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
