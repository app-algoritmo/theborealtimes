## How to play

In the Möttuls saga, a cloak that tests fidelity arrives at King Artús's court and hangs well only on those who were faithful. Here you tap the cloaks that hang well and avoid the ones that hang crooked. Tap the cloaks that hang well as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the crooked cloaks: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Möttuls saga, also called Skikkju saga, translates a French tale of the early thirteenth century about a cloak that tests fidelity and arrives at King Artús's court. It was translated under the patronage of King Hákon Hákonarson, gained a long introduction about Artús and misnames some characters. The complete manuscripts are from the seventeenth century. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
