## How to play

Start at the golden mark. Tap a neighbouring mark (beside, above or below) to move on. Pass through all of them, once each. To step back, tap the mark you are on (or press Backspace). Arrows move too. Time is limited.

## The story behind it

At Lindholm Høje there are graves with markers set in the shape of a ship. The route in the game is made up. Read the story in [Lindholm Høje: the Danish cemetery of stone ships covered by sand](/en/2026/11/22/lindholm-hoje-barcos-de-pedra/).
