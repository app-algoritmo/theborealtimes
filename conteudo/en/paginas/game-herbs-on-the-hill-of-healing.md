## How to play

In the Fjölsvinnsmál, Eir is among Menglöð's maidens, who sit on Lyfjaberg, the “hill of healing”. Tap the green herbs as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the dry thistles: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Fjölsvinnsmál, Eir is among the maidens of Menglöð on Lyfjaberg, the “hill of healing”, and in the Gylfaginning she is “an extremely good physician”. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
