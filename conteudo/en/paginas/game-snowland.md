## How to play

According to the Landnámabók, Naddoddr saw snow fall as he left an unknown coast and called it Snæland, "Snowland". Here you move his boat to catch the snow and avoid the rocks. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Naddoddr's boat and catch the snowflakes. Avoid the rocks: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Landnámabók, Naddoddr was driven by a storm to an unknown coast and, as he left, saw snow fall; that is why the place became known as Snæland, "Snowland". The flakes and the rocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
