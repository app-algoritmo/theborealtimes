## How to play

Fill the grid with two symbols (tap to cycle: empty → ◯ → ●). Rules: never three of a kind in a row, horizontally or vertically; every row and every column has three of each. Golden cells come filled in. Conflicts turn red.

## The story behind it

At Ridgeway Hill (Dorset), a mass grave from the Viking age was found. The symbol puzzle is made up. Read the story in [Ridgeway Hill: the Dorset mass grave and what the teeth tell](/en/2026/11/29/ridgeway-hill-sepultura-coletiva-dorset/).
