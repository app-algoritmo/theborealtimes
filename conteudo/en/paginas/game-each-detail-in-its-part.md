## How to play

The Tjängvide stone, on Gotland, has an upper field, a lower field and an inscription. A detail appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the part of the stone where it is, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the upper field, the second is the lower field and the third is the inscription. A wrong box, or a detail left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 details before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the Tjängvide stone (G 110) has above an eight-legged horse, a rider, a woman with a drinking horn and perhaps a hunter with a bow, below a ship with a wide sail and, along the edges, short-twig runes in memory of a brother called Hjôrulfr or Jórulfr, a name meaning "sword wolf". The queue of details, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
