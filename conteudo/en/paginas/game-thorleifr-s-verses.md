## How to play

In the Þorleifs þáttr jarlsskálds, the poet Þorleifr recites a verse of insult against Jarl Hákon, and Hákon sends a wooden figure against him. Here you tap the boards with verses and avoid the wooden figures. Tap the boards with verses as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the wooden figures: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Þorleifs þáttr jarlsskálds, a tale in the Flateyjarbók, the Icelander Þorleifr loses his ship to Jarl Hákon, recites a verse of insult at his court and earns the name jarlsskáld. Years later, Hákon sends a wooden figure that kills him at the Alþingi, around 990. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
