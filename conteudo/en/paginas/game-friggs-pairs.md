## How to play

Tap a piece to turn it over and tap another to find its match. If they match, they stay face up; if not, they turn back. Find the 8 pairs within 20 tries and 120 seconds. (Keyboard: arrows move, Space flips.)

## The story behind it

Frigg is Odin's wife and lives at Fensalir. The matching game is invented. Read the story in [Frigg: Odin's wife who made everything swear to spare Baldr, except one thing](/en/2027/01/03/frigg-esposa-de-odin-fensalir/).
