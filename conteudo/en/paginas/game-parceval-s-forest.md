## How to play

In the Parcevals saga, the young Parceval grows up in the forest, goes to King Artús's court and, in a castle, sees a procession with a bleeding lance and a precious dish. Here you run through the forest in three lanes, gather the golden dishes and dodge the fallen trunks. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the golden dishes and dodge the fallen trunks: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Parcevals saga ok Valvens þáttr is the Norse version of Chrétien de Troyes's Perceval, associated with the court of King Hákon Hákonarson. Parceval, raised in the forest by his widowed mother, goes to Artús's court and, in a castle, sees a bleeding lance and a precious dish, but does not ask what they mean. The saga ends abruptly, and the Valvens þáttr tells the adventures of Valven, the Gawain of Arthurian legend. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
