## World of mist

Niflheim (Niflheimr, “the world of mist”) is the realm of cold and darkness, in the north. According to the Prose Edda, it existed many ages before the earth was made. At its center is the spring Hvergelmir, and from it run the rivers called Élivágar. The venom these rivers carried hardened into ice and slowly filled the northern part of the void of Ginnungagap.

## Life from the ice

When the ice of the north met the heat of the south, the melted drops took the shape of Ymir, the first giant. To feed him the cow Auðumbla appeared. She licked blocks of salty ice and, after three days, freed a man called Búri from them, grandfather of Odin.

## Hvergelmir and the dragon

One of the three roots of Yggdrasil goes down to Niflheim and covers the spring Hvergelmir. There the dragon Níðhöggr gnaws the root from below, along with many serpents. Between him and the eagle at the top of the tree runs the squirrel Ratatoskr, carrying insults.

## The realm of Hel

According to Snorri, those who die of sickness or old age, and not in battle, go down to the realm of Hel. Hel was the daughter of Loki and the giantess Angrboða. Odin cast her into Niflheim and gave her power over nine worlds. It was there that Hermóðr rode for nine nights to try to bring Baldr back.

## The wolf of the Sun

In the sky, two wolves chase the Sun and the Moon: Sköll runs after the Sun and Hati after the Moon. In Ragnarök they catch them.
