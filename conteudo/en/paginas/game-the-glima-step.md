## How to play

In glíma, the wrestlers step in a circle clockwise, a movement called stígandi, to avoid a stalemate. Here you mark that step to the beat of the belt. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to mark the glíma step. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In glíma, Iceland's belt-and-trouser wrestling, the wrestlers step in a circle clockwise (stígandi); the first modern competition was in 1888 and the Belt of Grettir has been contested since 1906. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
