## How to play

The shadow of the disc shows where the ship points. Keep the shadow on the north mark. Hold the left or the right side of the screen, or use the arrow keys, A and D, to correct the course. Stay on course for 40 seconds before time runs out. If the shadow strays too far from the mark, it is a mistake. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Uunartoq, in Greenland, a fragment of a wooden disc was found that, according to one hypothesis, was a sun compass. The game is invented. Read the story in [Uunartoq: the wooden disc found in Greenland and the idea of the sun compass](/en/2027/03/07/uunartoq-o-disco-de-madeira-achado-na-groenlandia-e-a-ideia-da-bussola-de-sol/).
