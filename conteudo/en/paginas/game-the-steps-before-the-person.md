## How to play

In Norwegian belief, the vardøger is heard before the person arrives. Three doors stand in front of you. Wait in silence. When footprints and steps appear in front of a door, tap that door before the person arrives, or use keys 1, 2 and 3. Tapping before steps appear, tapping the wrong door or letting the person arrive without tapping is a mistake. Welcome 10 visits before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Store norske leksikon, the vardøger is a spirit that announces and that is heard, more rarely seen, before the person arrives. The three doors and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Vardøger: the spirit heard before the person arrives, in Norwegian belief](/vardoger-the-spirit-heard-before-the-person-arrives/).
