## How to play

In the Þorsteins þáttr skelks, a man at King Óláfr's court talks at night with a demon and comes away with the nickname "Shiver". Take each fact to the box of whoever said or did it. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Þorsteins þáttr skelks, a tale in the Flateyjarbók, Þorsteinn Þorkelsson disobeys King Óláfr Tryggvason, talks with a demon in the privy and asks it to imitate Starkaðr's cry. When the bell rings, the demon flees. Þorsteinn says he only shivered once and becomes known as Skelkr, "Shiver". The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
