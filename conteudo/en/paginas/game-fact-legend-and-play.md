## How to play

Little of the Hólar student is documented, and the rest is legend or came from the play of 1914. Sort each phrase. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Loftur Þorsteinsson studied at Hólar from 1716 to 1722. The book Rauðskinna, buried with Bishop Gottskálk in 1520, is legend, and the play "Galdra-Loftur", by Jóhann Sigurjónsson, premiered in 1914. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
