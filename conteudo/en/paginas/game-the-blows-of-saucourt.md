## How to play

In 881, at Saucourt, the Franks of Louis III faced a Viking army. Here you block with the shield every blow from the Norse. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

On 3 August 881, the Frankish kings Louis III and Carloman II defeated the Vikings at Saucourt; the Annals of Fulda speak of about nine thousand dead, a figure probably exaggerated. The victory was celebrated in the Ludwigslied, a poem in Old High German. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
