## How to play

Tap one of your 6 pits (bottom) to pick up its stones and sow them, one per pit, to the right and then around the rival's pits. If the last one lands in your store, play again; if it lands in an empty pit of yours, capture the stones in the pit opposite. Store more than your rival.

## The story behind it

In the Heimskringla, four guardians protect Iceland from an invasion. The sowing game is made up. Read the story in [Landvættir: the dragon, the bird, the bull and the giant that protect Iceland, according to the Heimskringla](/en/2026/12/13/landvaettir-guardioes-islandia-heimskringla/).
