## How to play

In the Hrafnsmál, a valkyrie asks and a raven answers about King Harald Fairhair. Here the answers arrive as feathers. Tap the raven's black feathers, with a golden thread as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the white seagull feathers: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Hrafnsmál, the valkyrie and the raven talk about the life and battles of Harald Fairhair. The feathers in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Hrafnsmál: the poem in which a valkyrie and a raven talk about King Harald Fairhair](/hrafnsmal-the-poem-in-which-a-valkyrie-and-a-raven-talk/).
