## How to play

At Norna-Gest's cradle, his life lasts as long as the candle does. Here, four winds come from every side to put out the flame. One wind grows at a time. Tap the wind to disperse it once its bar passes the mark, or use the arrow keys. Tapping a side with no wind, or acting too early, is a mistake. If the wind passes the full bar, it hits the flame and that is also a mistake. Drive off 12 winds before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Norna-Gests þáttr, a norn says Gest will live only as long as the candle beside the cradle keeps burning; the eldest puts out the flame and the mother hides the candle. The four winds, the bars and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Norna-Gest: the man who lived three hundred years as long as a candle did not burn out](/en/2027/04/04/norna-gest-o-homem-que-viveu-trezentos-anos-enquanto-uma-vela-nao-acabasse/).
