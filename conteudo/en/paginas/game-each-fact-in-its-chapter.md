## How to play

The Nydam boat has measurements, an excavation and a journey to Germany. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “The boat”, the second is “The excavation of 1859 to 1863” and the third is “After 1864”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the Nydam boat is 23 metres long, has 14 pairs of oars and dates to 310 to 320 CE. Engelhardt found it in 1863, and today it is at Gottorf, in Germany. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
