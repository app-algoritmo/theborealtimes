## How to play

Ilmarinen forges a bride of gold, and the servants work the bellows. Twelve embers form a circle, and a metal ladle travels around it without stopping. One of the embers glows orange. Tap the screen, or use Space, when the ladle is over the glowing ember, to put gold there. Tapping off the ember is a mistake, because the gold burns or cools. Put 10 pieces of gold on the embers before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the 37th rune of the Kalevala, Ilmarinen forges a bride of gold and silver, with servants working the bellows, and finds her cold and lifeless. The twelve embers, the ladle and the rhythm of the game are invention.
