## How to play

King Harald wants to test Brand's generosity. The king's request appears at the top of the screen. Tap the requested object, among the cloak, the axe and the tunic, before the request runs out of time, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. After each request, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a request left unanswered, is a mistake. Give 10 gifts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale of Brand the Generous, King Harald asks for Brand's cloak, axe and tunic, and Brand gives them, keeping only one sleeve of the tunic. The random order of the requests and the swaps of place are invention.
