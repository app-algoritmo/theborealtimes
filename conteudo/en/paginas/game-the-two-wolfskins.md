## How to play

Two wolfskins cross and share two beads. The beads are out of place. Use the four buttons to turn one skin, A or B, one way or the other. Bring the beads to the right colours: one side one colour, the other side another, and the two beads at the crossing golden, as the small picture shows. The number of turns and the time are limited. There are 3 rounds.

## The story behind it

In Völsunga saga, Sinfjötli and Sigmund put on cursed wolfskins and fall under a curse of lycanthropy. Turning rings of beads is invented. Read the story in [Sinfjötli: Sigmund's son, the wolfskins and the vengeance of the Völsungs](/en/2027/02/14/sinfjotli-filho-de-sigmund-peles-de-lobo-volsunga/).
