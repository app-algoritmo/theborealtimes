## How to play

On 23 December, many Icelanders eat fermented skate, and the last of the Yule Lads, Kertasníkir, arrives at night. Pop the skates and spare the candles. Tap the fermented skates as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap Kertasníkir's candles: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

On 23 December, Iceland celebrates Þorláksmessa, in memory of Bishop Þorlákur, who died in 1193, and many eat fermented skate at midday. The skates and candles in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
