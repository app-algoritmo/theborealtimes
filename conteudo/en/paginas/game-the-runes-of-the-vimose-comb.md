## How to play

The Vimose comb, from about 160, bears the word harja in runes. Here you find the runes among the teeth of the comb. Tap the comb teeth with a scratched rune as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the smooth teeth with no rune: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Vimose, on the Danish island of Funen, objects with runes of the 2nd to 3rd century turned up. The comb, of about 160, says harja and is considered the oldest datable runic inscription known. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
