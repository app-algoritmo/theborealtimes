## How to play

A block slides from side to side. Tap (or press Space) to drop it on the tower: any part that sticks out is cut off, and the next block is narrower. If you miss by a little, the block fits whole. Stack 10 floors in 90 seconds without losing the whole block.

## The story behind it

At Jarlshof, in Shetland, houses of different ages were built on top of one another. The tower of blocks is invented. Read the story in [Jarlshof in Shetland: four thousand years of stacked houses and a Norse longhouse](/en/2027/01/03/jarlshof-shetland-assentamento-nordico/).
