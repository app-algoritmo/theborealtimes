## How to play

The final verses of the Hamðismál recall an inscription on a runestone. Here the brothers ride through the mist. Tap the inscription stones, with clear runes as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the blocks of dark mist: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Hamðismál, Hamðir and Sörli ride over a mountain of mist to avenge Svanhild, and the final verses recall an inscription on a runestone. The stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
