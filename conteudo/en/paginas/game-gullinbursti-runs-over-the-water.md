## How to play

According to the Skáldskaparmál, Gullinbursti runs through air and water better than any horse, and its bristles light up the dark. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the sparks from the furnace and dodge the dark waves: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Skáldskaparmál, Brokkr and Sindri made Gullinbursti from a pig's skin in the furnace. It runs through air and water better than any horse, and its bristles glow in the dark. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
