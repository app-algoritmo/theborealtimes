## How to play

In the Gylfaginning, the goddess Vár hears the oaths and punishes those who break them. Here you tap the oath rings that appear in the hall and do not tap the thorns. Tap the oath rings as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the thorns: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Gylfaginning, of Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda, the goddesses Sjöfn, Lofn, Vár, Vör, Syn, Hlín, Snotra and Gná appear in a list with a short function each; of Vár it is said that she hears oaths and punishes those who break them. Very little survives about most of them. The rings and the thorns in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
