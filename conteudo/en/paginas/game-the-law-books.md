## How to play

The Landslov of Magnus Lagabøte existed in four books, one for each jurisdiction. Here you stack the law books with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the scribe's table. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 1274, King Magnus VI, called Lagabøte, gave Norway the Landslov, a code for the whole kingdom that existed in four books, one for each jurisdiction; it stayed in use for more than four hundred years. The books in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
