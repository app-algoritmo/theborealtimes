## How to play

The Franks Casket is made of whale-bone plates carved with scenes and runes. Here you stack the plates with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the base of the casket. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Franks Casket, of whale bone, from the early 8th century, was found in parts: today it is in the British Museum, and one side is in the Bargello, in Florence. The stacked plates in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [The Franks Casket: the whale-bone box of about 700 with Weland, the Magi and runes in Old English](/the-franks-casket-the-whale-bone-box-of-about-700/).
