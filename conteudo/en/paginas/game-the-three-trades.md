## How to play

Disguised as a beggar, Hacon trades three golden objects with the princess. Watch the order in which the three objects rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the objects in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong object is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, the beggar Hacon trades a golden spinning wheel, its stand and a golden wool winder for nights near the princess. The order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [Hacon Grizzlebeard: the Norwegian tale of the proud princess and the beggar who was a prince](/en/2027/03/28/hacon-barba-grisalha-conto-noruegues-da-princesa-orgulhosa-e-do-mendigo-que-era-principe/).
