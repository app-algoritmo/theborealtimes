## How to play

There are three heaps of coins. On your turn, tap a coin to take it and all those above it in the same heap. Whoever takes the last coin wins. The rival plays right after you. Win within 90 seconds.

## The story behind it

In the Bandamanna saga, Ófeigr wins a case with cunning and payments. The coin game is made up. Read the story in [Bandamanna saga: the case at Thingvellir and Ófeigr's cunning](/en/2026/12/20/bandamanna-saga-oddr-ofeigr-suborno/).
