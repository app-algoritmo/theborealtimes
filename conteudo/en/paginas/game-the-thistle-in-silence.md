## How to play

The sister has to gather thistle down without speaking, laughing or crying. Purple thistles and red bubbles fall from above. Tap the left or right side of the screen to change lane, or use the arrow keys. Catch the purple thistles and dodge the red bubbles, which are the words, the laughs and the tears. Catching a bubble is a mistake. Gather 14 thistles before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the sister must gather thistle and make twelve shirts without speaking, laughing or crying. The rain of thistles and bubbles and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [The Twelve Wild Ducks: the Norwegian tale of the sister who could not speak, laugh or weep](/the-twelve-wild-ducks-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-sister/).
