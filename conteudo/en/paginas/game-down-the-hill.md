## How to play

The pancake rolls down the hill and the animals on the road want to eat it. In each row there are two animals and one free lane. Tap the left side of the screen, or press the left arrow, to move to the left lane, and the right side, or the right arrow, to move to the right lane. Hitting an animal is a mistake. Pass 10 rows before time runs out. Three hits end the game.

## The story behind it

In Asbjørnsen's tale in Dasent's translation, the pancake runs from a family and from several animals before it is swallowed by a pig. The lanes and the rows of animals are invention. Read the story in [The runaway pancake: an Asbjørnsen tale with the pig at the brook](/the-runaway-pancake-an-asbjornsen-tale-with-the-pig/).
