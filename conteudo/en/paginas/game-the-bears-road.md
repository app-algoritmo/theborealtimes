## How to play

Each square shows the cost of passing through it. Start from the top-left square and reach the bottom-right square, tapping one neighbouring square at a time, with no diagonals and no repeated squares. The total cost cannot pass the limit. Tapping the last square of the path undoes it, but only 3 times. Time is limited. There are 3 maps.

## The story behind it

In the tale of Auðun, a poor Icelander buys a polar bear in Greenland and takes it as a gift to King Sveinn of Denmark. Finding the cheapest path is invented. Read the story in [Auðun of the West Fjords: the polar bear, the Danish king and the gold ring](/en/2027/02/21/audun-dos-fiordes-do-oeste-o-urso-polar-e-o-rei-sveinn/).
