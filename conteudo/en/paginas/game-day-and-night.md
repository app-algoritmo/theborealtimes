## How to play

Each square of the grid must hold a sun or a moon. Tap an empty square to place a sun, tap again to switch to a moon and once more to empty it. In every row and every column, half the squares hold a sun and half a moon, and never three alike in a row. Fixed squares do not change. Squares in conflict turn reddish. Time is limited. There are 3 grids.

## The story behind it

In the Eddas, Nótt and Dagr are each given a chariot and a horse by Odin and travel across the sky every 24 hours, with Nótt ahead. Filling suns and moons is invented. Read the story in [Nótt and Dagr: Night and Day in the Eddas, each with a chariot and a horse](/en/2027/02/14/nott-dagr-noite-dia-carruagens-eddas/).
