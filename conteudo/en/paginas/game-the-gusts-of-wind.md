## How to play

Thorvard's sail ran ahead of all the others. Gusts of wind cross the sea. The blue ones point to the front of the ship and fill the sail: tap them to catch them. The red ones point backwards, against the sail: let them pass. Tapping a red gust is a mistake. Catch 14 blue gusts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Icelandic tale, King Harald refuses Thorvard's sail, and later Eystein's sail runs ahead of the king's ship. The gusts of wind in the game and the rhythm are invention. Read the story in [Thorvard Crow-Nose: the Icelander whose sail King Harald refused](/thorvard-crow-nose-the-icelander-whose-sail-king-harald/).
