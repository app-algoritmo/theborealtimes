## How to play

The edition by Peter A. Jorgensen is based on manuscript AM 161 fol. Here you move the scribe's desk to gather the leaves. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the scribe's desk and catch the parchment leaves. Avoid the ink blots: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The bilingual edition of Valla-Ljóts saga, by Peter A. Jorgensen, from 1991, is based on manuscript AM 161 fol. The leaves and ink blots in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
