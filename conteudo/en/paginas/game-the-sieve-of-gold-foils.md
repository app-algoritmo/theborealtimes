## How to play

At Uppåkra, the earth of the cult building held small gold foils. The finds pass along the sieving table. Tap the gold foils, small and shiny, to keep them. The stones are grey and round: do not tap them. Tapping a stone is a mistake. Keep 14 gold foils before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Uppåkra, the excavation of the cult building found about 200 guldgubbar, small gold foils with figures, more than at any other site in Sweden. The sieving table, the stones and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Uppåkra: the cult building rebuilt six times and the 200 gold foils](/uppakra-the-cult-building-rebuilt-six-times-and-the-200/).
