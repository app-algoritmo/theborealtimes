## How to play

Two brothers walk at the same time, each in his own field. Tap the screen to move: the brother on the left goes the way you tapped, and the one on the right goes the opposite way when the move is left or right, and the same way when it is up or down. Rocks block the way. Bring each brother to his star at the same time. The number of moves and the time are limited. There are 3 fields. You can also use the keyboard arrows.

## The story behind it

In Droplaugarsona saga, the brothers Grímr and Helgi, sons of the widow Droplaug, live near Lagarfljót in eastern Iceland around the year 1000. Helgi is killed and Grímr avenges him. Moving brothers in mirror is invented. Read the story in [Droplaugarsona saga: the brothers Grímr and Helgi, vengeance near Lagarfljót](/en/2027/02/07/droplaugarsona-saga-grimr-helgi-lagarfljot/).
