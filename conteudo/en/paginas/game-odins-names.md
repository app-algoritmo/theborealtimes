## How to play

Guide the spark around the hall. Arrows or WASD, or tap in the direction you want to go (dragging works too). Every name collected makes it grow. Collect 12 names without touching the flames at the edges or your own trail.

## The story behind it

In the Grímnismál, Odin in disguise tells many of his names, between two fires. The name-collecting game is made up. Read the story in [Grímnismál: Odin between two fires, speaking about the world](/en/2026/11/29/grimnismal-odin-entre-fogos/).
