## How to play

When Snæfríðr's body was moved, the Heimskringla says toads, snakes and lizards came out of it. Watch the order in which the toad, the snake and the lizard rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the creatures in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong creature is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Heimskringla, when the servants moved Snæfríðr's body, toads, snakes and lizards came out of it, and it crumbled to dust. The order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [Snæfríðr, the Sami wife of Harald Fairhair, and the three years the king would not leave her body](/snaefridr-the-sami-wife-of-harald-fairhair-and-the-three/).
