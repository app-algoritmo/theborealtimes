## How to play

In Denmark, whoever escaped a serious illness had "given death a bag of oats". Here you run through the churchyard in three lanes, gather the bags of oats and dodge the three-legged horse. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the bags of oats and dodge the three-legged horse: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Danish folklore, the Helhest is a three-legged horse linked to the goddess Hel and to death, and "giving death a bag of oats" meant escaping a serious illness. The run, the oats and the horse in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
