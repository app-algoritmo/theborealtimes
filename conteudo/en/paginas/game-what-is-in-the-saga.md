## How to play

Ättestupa is a word that was born from a saga and gathered other stories over time. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the right box: the first is what is in the saga, the second is what came in the 17th to 19th centuries and the third is what came in the 20th and 21st centuries. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Sort 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Gautreks saga, King Gauti gets lost and spends the night at the house of the farmer Skinflint, and the place is called Ættarstapi. The ättestupa cliffs came later, and the practice was never proven. The division into three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Gautrek and the miserly family: the saga behind the Swedish "ättestupa"](/en/2027/03/28/gautrek-e-a-familia-avarenta-a-saga-por-tras-da-attestupa-sueca/).
