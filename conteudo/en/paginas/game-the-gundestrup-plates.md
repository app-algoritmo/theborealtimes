## How to play

The Gundestrup cauldron was found dismantled in a peat bog, with the plates stacked inside the base. Here you collect the plates. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the basket and catch the silver plates. Avoid the lumps of peat: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 1891, peat cutters found in Rævemose, Denmark, the dismantled silver cauldron, with the plates stacked in the base. Today seven outer plates, five inner plates and a base plate survive. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
