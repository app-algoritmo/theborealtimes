## How to play

Tap (or press Space) to stop the marker inside the golden band. Five right stops push Fenrir's jaw. Three wrong stops, or three times the marker runs seven seconds without being stopped, and the wolf escapes. The band narrows and the marker speeds up with every hit.

## The story behind it

At Ragnarök, Víðarr steps on the jaw of Fenrir with a shoe made from scraps of leather. The marker is invented. Read the story in [Víðarr: the silent god, the thick shoe and the end of the wolf Fenrir](/en/2026/12/27/vidarr-deus-silencioso-sapato-fenrir/).
