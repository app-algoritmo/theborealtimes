## How to play

Marjatta has lost the boy, and the sun knows where he is. Nine clumps of reeds are before you in a swamp. A gleam of sunlight appears on one clump at a time. Tap the clump that gleams before the gleam fades, or use keys 1 to 9. Tapping a clump without a gleam, or letting a gleam fade, is a mistake. Follow 12 signs of the sun before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In rune 50 of the Kalevala, Marjatta asks the moon and the stars, and only the sun answers: the boy is hidden among reeds in a swamp. The clumps with a gleam and the rhythm of the game are invention.
