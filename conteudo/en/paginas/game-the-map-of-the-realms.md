## How to play

The map is divided into realms. Tap a realm to paint it: each tap changes the colour (each colour has a symbol). Two realms that share a border cannot have the same colour. Paint every realm using only the colours in the palette, which has fewer colours in the later rounds. Neighbouring realms of the same colour get a red border. There are 3 maps, and time is limited.

## The story behind it

The Knýtlinga saga, written around 1250, tells the story of the kings of Denmark from Harald Bluetooth to the end of the 12th century. Colouring maps is invented. Read the story in [Knýtlinga saga: the kings of Denmark from Harald Bluetooth to the 12th century, written in Iceland](/en/2027/01/31/knytlinga-saga-reis-da-dinamarca-harald-valdemar/).
