## How to play

In the Áns saga bogsveigis, the dwarf Litr gives Án a strong bow and five arrows that hit the target he chooses. Here you tap the straw targets and do not tap your friends' shields. Tap the straw targets as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap your friends' shields: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Áns saga bogsveigis, an Icelandic legendary saga, Án, the youngest son of a farmer of Hrafnista, receives a bow and five arrows from the dwarf Litr and becomes the "bow-bender" at the court of King Ingjaldr of Naumdalir. The saga is usually dated to the fourteenth century; the dwarf belongs to folktale tradition. The targets and the shields in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
