## How to play

In the Oddrúnargrátr, Oddrún saddles a horse and rides to the hall where Borgný cannot give birth. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the charm stones and dodge the fallen logs: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Oddrúnargrátr, Oddrún saddles a horse and rides to the hall of Borgný, daughter of King Heiðrekr, and chants charms over her until a boy and a girl are born. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Oddrún: the lament of Atli's sister, Borgný's childbirth and the love for Gunnarr](/oddrun-the-lament-of-atli-s-sister-borgny-s-childbirth/).
