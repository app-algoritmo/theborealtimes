## How to play

Hekla erupted in 1104 and covered much of Iceland in tephra. Here you run in three lanes, save sheep and dodge the rain of ash. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the sheep of the flock and dodge the blocks of tephra: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Hekla, a volcano in southern Iceland, erupted in 1104 (VEI 5), and in the Middle Ages gained a reputation as the gate to hell. The run, the sheep and the rain of tephra in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
