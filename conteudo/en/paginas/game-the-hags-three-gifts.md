## How to play

Three one-eyed hags give Shortshanks gifts: a sword, a ship and the art of brewing. Watch the order in which the three gifts rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the gifts in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong gift is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, in Dasent's translation, the hags give Shortshanks a sword that puts an army to flight, a ship that shrinks to pocket size and grows when he steps in, and the art of brewing a hundred lasts of malt at one strike. The order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [Shortshanks and the three one-eyed hags: the Norwegian tale of the three gifts](/shortshanks-and-the-three-one-eyed-hags-the-norwegian-tale/).
