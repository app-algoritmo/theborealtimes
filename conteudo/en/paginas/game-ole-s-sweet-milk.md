## How to play

In Andersen's tale, Ole Lukøje sprinkles sweet milk in children's eyes and opens an umbrella over them. Here you move Ole's umbrella to catch the drops of sweet milk and avoid the hail. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Ole's umbrella and catch the drops of sweet milk. Avoid the hail: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale "Ole Lukøje", published by Hans Christian Andersen on 20 December 1841, Ole sprinkles sweet milk in children's eyes and opens over them an umbrella with pictures, if they are good, or a plain one, if they are naughty. The drops and the hail in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
