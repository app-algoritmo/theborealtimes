## How to play

The cards lie face down, and each symbol appears on two cards. Tap a card to turn it over, then another one. If the symbols match, the pair stays face up; if they differ, both cards turn back and you make a mistake. Find all the pairs without passing the limit of mistakes. Time is limited. There are 3 tables, each with more cards.

## The story behind it

In the Grímnismál, the squirrel Ratatoskr runs up and down Yggdrasil and carries messages between the eagle, at the top, and Níðhöggr, below. Finding pairs of cards is invented.
