## How to play

Forseti has kept a verdict of four coloured symbols, which may repeat. Tap the colours to build your guess. After each guess, a gold dot marks a right colour in the right place, and a pale dot marks a right colour in the wrong place. Find the verdict in up to 8 guesses. (Keyboard: keys 1 to 6, Backspace deletes.)

## The story behind it

In the Gylfaginning, Snorri says that Forseti lives in the hall Glitnir and that whoever brings a dispute to him leaves reconciled. The hidden verdict is invented. Read the story in [Forseti, the god who reconciles quarrels: what is known of the hall Glitnir](/en/2027/01/17/forseti-deus-justica-glitnir-gylfaginning/).
