## How to play

Row upriver, against the current. Tap the left side of the screen to row with the left oar and the right side for the right oar. Alternate the sides, one after the other: rowing twice in a row on the same side is a mistake. Advance 100 metres before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, the husband searches upstream and finds his wife above a waterfall, floating against the current. The rowing is invented. Read the story in [The Contrary-Minded Woman: a Norwegian tale about stubbornness and the river](/en/2027/03/07/a-mulher-contraria-conto-noruegues-sobre-a-teimosia-e-o-rio/).
