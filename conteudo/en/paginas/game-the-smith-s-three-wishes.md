## How to play

In the Norwegian tale, the smith wishes that whoever climbs his pear tree, sits in his armchair or enters his steel purse is held fast. Watch the order in which the three objects rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the objects in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong object is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the tale collected by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the smith traps the devil first in the pear tree, then in the armchair and finally in the steel purse, which he beats on the anvil. The shapes, the order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [The Master-Smith: the Norwegian tale of the smith who trapped the devil in a pear-tree, a chair and a steel purse](/the-master-smith-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-smith/).
