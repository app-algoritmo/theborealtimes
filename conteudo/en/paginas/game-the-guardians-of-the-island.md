## How to play

You are the sorcerer in whale form, at the centre, and the island is guarded on four sides. Each time a guardian appears, tap its side before time runs out: bird to the north, dragon to the east, giant to the south, bull to the west. (Keyboard: arrow keys.) Face 20 guardians with at most 2 mistakes; the third mistake, by a wrong tap or by being slow, ends the game.

## The story behind it

In the Heimskringla, King Harald Bluetooth sends a sorcerer in whale form to spy on Iceland, and he is driven off from each side: a dragon in the east, a great bird in the north, a bull in the west and a giant in the south. The reflex game is invented. Read the story in [The landvættir, spirits of the land: the whale sorcerer, the forbidden dragon head and Egill's curse](/en/2027/01/17/vaettir-landvaettir-espiritos-da-terra-islandia/).
