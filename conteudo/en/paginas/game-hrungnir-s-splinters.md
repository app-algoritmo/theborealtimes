## How to play

In the Haustlöng, Thor and the giant Hrungnir fight by throwing their weapons: the hammer and the whetstone. Here you defend with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Haustlöng, Hrungnir's whetstone breaks against Thor's hammer, and a piece of it stays in the god's head. The splinters in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Haustlöng: the shield poem in which Þjóðólfr of Hvinir described Thor, Hrungnir and the abduction of Iðunn](/haustlong-the-shield-poem-in-which-thjodolfr-of-hvinir/).
