## How to play

Fill the grid with the numbers from 1 up to its size, with no repeats in any row or column, and obeying the signs between neighbouring squares: the sign points to the smaller number. The fixed numbers do not change. Tap a square to change the number. Time is limited. There are 3 grids.

## The story behind it

In Eiríksmál, Eric arrives in Valhalla and says that five kings follow him and that he is the sixth. Ordering numbers with signs is invented. Read the story in [Eiríksmál: the poem that welcomes Eric Bloodaxe into Valhalla](/eiriksmal-the-poem-that-welcomes-eric-bloodaxe-into-valhalla/).
