## How to play

In Skáldskaparmál, the mead of Suttungr is kept at Hnitbjörg, and Odin drinks it in three draughts: Óðrerir, Boðn and Són. Tap the golden cups full of mead as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the empty clay cups: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Skáldskaparmál, Suttungr hid the mead at Hnitbjörg and set his daughter Gunnlöð to guard it. Odin spent three nights with her and drained the vessels Óðrerir, Boðn and Són in three draughts. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
