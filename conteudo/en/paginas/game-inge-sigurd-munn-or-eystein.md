## How to play

After 1136, three sons of Harald Gille reigned together over Norway: Inge, Sigurd Munn and Eystein. Here you take each phrase to the brother it is about. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

After the death of Harald Gille, in 1136, Norway had three kings at the same time, the sons Inge, Sigurd Munn and Eystein, all over the whole country. In 1155, in Bergen, Inge ordered an attack on the house where Sigurd was, and Sigurd died. In 1157, near Moster, Eystein's men abandoned him, and he was killed on 21 August 1157, in Bohuslän. Inge remained as sole king. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
