## How to play

Each player has three stones. First you place the stones, one at a time, on free points. Then each player moves one stone at a time to a free neighbouring point, following the lines of the board. Whoever forms a line of three stones wins the game. Win two games before losing two. Drawn games are replayed.

## The story behind it

In the tale Gudbrand i Lia, the farmer trades the cow for a horse, the horse for a pig, and so on, until he is left with nothing. Even so he wins the bet, because his wife praises every trade. Playing three in a row is invented.
