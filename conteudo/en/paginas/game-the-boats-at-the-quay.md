## How to play

At Birka, on Lake Mälaren, merchants came from afar. Twelve quays form the harbour, and a silver marker runs around it without stopping. One of the quays glows. Tap the screen, or use Space, when the marker is over the quay that glows, to dock the boat there. Tapping off the quay is a mistake. Dock 10 boats before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Birka, on the island of Björkö in Lake Mälaren, was founded around 750, and most of the coins found there are silver dirhams from the Middle East. The twelve quays of the game, the marker and the rhythm are invention.
