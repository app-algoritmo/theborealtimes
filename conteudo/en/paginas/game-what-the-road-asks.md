## How to play

In the tale, Boots needs the sword, the little pitcher and the wine from the church. The request appears at the top of the screen. Tap the object asked for before the time for the request runs out: the sword, the pitcher or the flask of wine. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. After each request, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a request left unanswered, is a mistake. Answer 10 requests before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, Boots can only wield the rusty sword after drinking three times from the little pitcher, and brings the king the wine and the wafer from the church as proof. The order of the requests, the timing and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [The Seven Foals: the Norwegian tale of the lad in the ashes who ran after seven foals and broke a troll's spell](/the-seven-foals-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-lad-in-the-ashes/).
