## How to play

Tap a square to change the number, which is the height of a banner. Each row and each column must hold every height, without repeating. The numbers on the edges say how many banners can be seen from that side, because taller ones hide shorter ones behind them. Repeated numbers turn red. There are 3 boards, and time is limited.

## The story behind it

At the Battle of Fulford, on 20 September 1066, south of York, Harald Hardrada and Tostig defeated the earls Edwin and Morcar. Stamford Bridge followed. Counting visible banners is invented. Read the story in [The Battle of Fulford, 1066: Harald Hardrada and Tostig against the earls of northern England](/en/2027/01/31/batalha-de-fulford-1066-hardrada-york/).
