## How to play

In the saga, Hávarðr, already old, rises to avenge his son. Here you block with the shield every blow from the enemies at Ísafjörður. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Hávarðar saga Ísfirðings, an Icelandic saga, tells the vengeance of Hávarðr, a man already old, for the death of his son. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
