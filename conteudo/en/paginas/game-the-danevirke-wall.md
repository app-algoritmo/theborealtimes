## How to play

Invaders advance along five lanes toward the wall on the left. Tap an empty square to place a guard, which costs 2 coins and throws darts along the lane. Coins arrive over time. Defeat all 22 invaders. If 3 get past the wall, the game ends. Keyboard: arrows and Space.

## The story behind it

The Danevirke was an earth-and-timber rampart in southern Denmark, extended over the centuries. The lane defence is made up for the game. Read the story in [Danevirke: the earthen rampart the Danes raised against the Frankish Empire](/en/2026/11/21/danevirke-muralha-dinamarquesa/).
