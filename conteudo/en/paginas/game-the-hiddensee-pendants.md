## How to play

Each tube holds coloured pieces, and 4 fit in each. Tap a tube, then another, to move the top piece of the first onto the second. A piece can enter only an empty tube or land on a piece of the same colour, and only if there is room. Leave every tube with a single colour, or empty, without passing the number of moves shown at the top. Time is limited. There are 3 tables.

## The story behind it

In 1873, after floods, 16 pendants, a brooch and a neck ring, all of gold and from the 10th century, were found on the German island of Hiddensee. The pendants combine Thor’s hammer and the cross. Sorting pieces by colour is invented.
