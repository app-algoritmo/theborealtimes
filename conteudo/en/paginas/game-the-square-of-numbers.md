## How to play

Put the numbers in the nine squares so that every row, every column and both diagonals add up to the value shown at the top right. Tap a number in the bottom row and then an empty square. Tap a number already placed to return it. The golden numbers are already fixed. Time is limited. There are 3 squares.

## The story behind it

The St Brice's Day massacre of 1002 followed an order from the English king Æthelred. Completing a square of numbers is invented.
