## How to play

Accused of the murder of Erik Klipping, Marsk Stig flees to the island of Hjelm. Here you ride along the coast in three lanes. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the sacks of provisions for the flight and dodge the pursuers' spears: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to tradition, King Erik V of Denmark was killed at Finderup, in 1286, and the marshal Stig Andersen Hvide, accused, fled and settled on the island of Hjelm, where he died in 1293. The ride and the sacks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
