## How to play

Þorsteinn serves Jarl Sigurðr of Orkney and fights at Clontarf, in 1014. Here you row in time with the boat. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to row to Orkney. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Þorsteins saga Síðu-Hallssonar, Þorsteinn serves Jarl Sigurðr of Orkney and fights at the battle of Clontarf, in 1014. The rowing rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
