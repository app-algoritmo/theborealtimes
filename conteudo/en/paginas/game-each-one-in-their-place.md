## How to play

In Kjalnesinga saga, trolls and giants live alongside settlers and the priest of Hof. Take each character to their group. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Kjalnesinga saga, of the 14th century, Búi is raised by the troll Esja, burns the temple at Hof and dies before his son Jökull. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Kjalnesinga saga: Búi, raised by the troll Esja, the temple burned at Hof and the son who arrives from Norway](/kjalnesinga-saga-bui-raised-by-the-troll-esja-the-temple/).
