## How to play

The elves come one by one along the path. The light ones belong to Álfheimr: tap the left side of the screen, or press the left arrow, to send them up. The dark ones live under the earth: tap the right side, or press the right arrow, to send them below. Each elf needs an answer before it reaches you. Send 14 elves to the right place before time runs out. Three mistakes, or elves that pass unanswered, end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Snorri says the light elves live in Álfheimr and the dark elves live down in the earth, blacker than pitch. The path and the sorting are invented. Read the story in [Álfheimr: light elves and dark elves in Snorri and in Grímnismál](/alfheimr-light-elves-and-dark-elves-in-snorri/).
