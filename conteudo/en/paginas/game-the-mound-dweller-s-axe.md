## How to play

In the Kumlbúa þáttr, the dweller of a burial mound appears in a dream with a great long-hafted axe and demands his sword back. Here you block with the shield every blow of the axe. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Kumlbúa þáttr, an Icelandic tale preserved in copies of the Vatnshyrna codex, Þorsteinn Þorvarðsson takes a sword from a burial mound, dreams of the dweller, who demands it back with an axe and a verse, and answers with a better-made verse. The next day, he cannot find the mound. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
