## How to play

In Limerick, the Norse lived from trade and piracy on the River Shannon. Here you run along the bank in three lanes, collect the silver coins and dodge the Irish spears. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the silver coins and dodge the spears: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Norse settled on King's Island, in Limerick, probably in 922. At Sulcoit, in 967, the Dál gCais of Mathgamain and Brian Boru defeated them; the liveliest account comes from the Cogad Gáedel re Gallaib, written more than a century later. The run, the coins and the spears in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
