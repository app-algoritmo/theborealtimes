## How to play

At Illerup Ådal, the weapons of a defeated army were thrown into a lake as an offering. Here you tap the spearheads and do not tap the stones. Tap the spearheads as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the stones: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Illerup Ådal, in Jutland, excavations from 1950 to 1985 found more than 15,000 Iron Age objects, among them 300 spearheads in the oldest deposit. The spears and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Illerup Ådal: the Danish valley where a lake kept more than 15,000 objects of war, spearheads with the word "Wagnijo" and 124 combs](/illerup-adal-the-danish-valley-where-a-lake-kept-more-than/).
