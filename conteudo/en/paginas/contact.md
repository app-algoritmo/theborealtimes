For any matter, write to **post@borealtimes.org**.

## Suggestions and errors

Is there a legend, a saga or an episode of Nordic history you would like to see here? Write to us. If you found an error in a text, send the page address and the passage, preferably with the source that supports the correction. Confirmed errors go on the [corrections](/en/corrections/) page.

## Newsletter and privacy

To receive the next stories, use the [subscribe](/en/subscribe/) page. To stop receiving them, use the link at the bottom of any email from us. Requests about personal data also come to this address and are explained in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).

Messages sent here are used only to reply and to correct the content. They are not added to the newsletter list.
