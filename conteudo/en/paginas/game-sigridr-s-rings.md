## How to play

In Heimskringla, Sigríðr's goldsmiths find that the ring sent by Olaf Tryggvason is gilded brass. Tap the real gold rings, bright yellow and shiny as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the gilded-brass rings, dull and with a reddish edge: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Heimskringla, Sigríðr's goldsmiths found that Olaf Tryggvason's gold ring was gilded brass, and she began to doubt him. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
