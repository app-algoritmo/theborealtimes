## How to play

The Hryggjarstykki, by Eiríkr Oddsson, is lost, and is known only through quotations. Here you tap the parchment leaves that still shine and avoid those already lost. Tap the leaves that shine as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the lost leaves: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Hryggjarstykki is a lost kings' saga, written around 1150 by the Icelander Eiríkr Oddsson. It told the story of Harald Gilli and his sons, Magnus the Blind and Sigurd Slembe, and of the sons Inge and Sigurd. No copy survives. It is known from Heimskringla, which cites it and stresses its reliability, from Morkinskinna, and from Fagrskinna, which used it without explicitly citing it. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Hryggjarstykki, the lost saga of Eiríkr Oddsson: the first written account of the Norwegian civil wars, known only through quotations](/hryggjarstykki-the-lost-saga-of-eirikr-oddsson-the-first/).
