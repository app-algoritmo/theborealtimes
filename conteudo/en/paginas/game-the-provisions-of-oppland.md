## How to play

In 1166, Olav Gudbrandsson was proclaimed king in Oppland, with the support of farmers. Here you move the supply cart to catch what feeds the army and avoid the stones of the ambush. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the supply cart and catch the provisions. Avoid the stones of the ambush: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Olav Gudbrandsson, "Ugjæva", the unlucky, was a grandson of King Eystein I through his mother, Maria Øysteinsdotter, and was raised in Oppland by Sigurd Agnhatt. In 1166 he was proclaimed king. On 2 February 1167, his men ambushed Erling Skakke at Rydjokul, who escaped wounded. In 1168 he was defeated at Stanger, and Sigurd Agnhatt died. Olav fled to Denmark and died in 1169. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
