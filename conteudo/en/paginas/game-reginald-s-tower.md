## How to play

In Waterford, in southern Ireland, Reginald's Tower has guarded the quay since the Middle Ages. Here you stack the tower's stone blocks with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the quay of Waterford. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Irish annals point to around 914 for the arrival of a Norse fleet on the river Suir, the date used for the founding of Waterford; the present stone Reginald's Tower is generally dated to the 13th century. The blocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
