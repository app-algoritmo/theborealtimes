## How to play

At Haugsnes, where Iceland's largest battle took place, there is now a memorial of more than a thousand stones. Here you stack the memorial stones with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the field of Haugsnes. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

On 19 April 1246, at Haugsnes, Þórður kakali faced Brandur Kolbeinsson, with estimates of about 600 men on each side; the accounts speak of about 110 dead. Today a memorial of more than a thousand stones, with iron crosses, recalls the battle. The blocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
