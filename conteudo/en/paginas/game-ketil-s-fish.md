## How to play

In the Ketils saga hœngs, Ketil fishes alone in the fjords when famine afflicts the region. Here you move Ketil's net to catch the fish and avoid the giant's stones. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Ketil's net and catch the fish. Avoid the giant's stones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Ketils saga hœngs, an Icelandic legendary saga, young Ketil, son of Hallbjörn, of Hrafnista, fishes in the fjords in time of famine and faces giants, among them Surtr and Kaldrani. Scholars do not fix a secure date for the saga. The net, the fish and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
