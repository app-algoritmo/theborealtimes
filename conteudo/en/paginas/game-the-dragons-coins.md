## How to play

Gold coins are scattered over a board. Tap a coin and then an empty square two squares away in a straight line, with a coin in between: the coin jumps and takes the middle one away. Leave only one coin on the board. Time is limited. There are 3 boards. If no jumps remain, the board starts over.

## The story behind it

In Gull-Þóris saga, Þórir obtains gold from dragons in a cave and later quarrels with Hallr over its division; at the end, a rumour says he became a dragon. Jumping coins is invented.
