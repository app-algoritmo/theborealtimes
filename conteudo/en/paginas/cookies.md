## How the choice works

On your first visit a consent window appears, provided by Google and certified for use in the European Union. It records your choice for measurement and for advertising. Until you choose, nothing beyond what is necessary is stored. You can change your choice at any time.

## Necessary

These keep your consent preference and, for the length of the visit only, the fact that you dismissed the language notice, and keep the site working. They do not depend on authorisation.

## Audience measurement

Google Analytics, in aggregate counts. It is switched on only after you accept, and refusing removes no access.

## Advertising

The site shows Google AdSense ads, which may use cookies to limit repetition, measure performance and, if you authorise it, personalise what is shown. If you refuse personalisation you still see ads, chosen by the content of the page and not by your history.

## What we do not use

The games neither store nor send data. The newsletter form keeps only what you type into it, and only when you submit it.

## Third parties

Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects for advertising and measurement. Its processing follows Google's own policies, which the consent window presents at the time of choice. More in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
