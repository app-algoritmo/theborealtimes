## How the choice works

A consent window appears on your first visit. It is Google's consent management platform, certified for use in the European Union, and it records your choice for advertising and for measurement.

Until you choose, nothing beyond the necessary is stored. The choice can be changed at any time from the site footer.

## Necessary

These store your consent preference and keep the site working. They require no permission.

## Audience measurement

Google Analytics, in aggregate. It is activated only after you accept, and can be refused without losing access.

## Advertising

The site carries Google AdSense advertising, which may use cookies to cap repetition, measure performance and, with your permission, personalise what is shown.

If you refuse personalisation you will still see advertising — it is then chosen by the content of the page and its context, not by your history.

## Third parties

Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects for advertising. Its processing is governed by Google's own policies, which the consent window presents at the moment you choose.
