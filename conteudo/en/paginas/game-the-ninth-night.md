## How to play

The ring Draupnir lets go of its eight rings only every ninth night. Watch the count of nights pass at the top of the screen. Tap the screen, or press Space, only when the count reaches the number 9. Tapping on another night is a mistake, and so is letting the ninth pass without tapping. Release the rings on 10 ninth nights before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Snorri says that every ninth night eight gold rings of equal weight fall from Draupnir. The fast count of the nights and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Draupnir: Odin's ring that drips eight rings every nine nights](/en/2027/03/21/draupnir-o-anel-de-odin-que-pinga-oito-aneis-a-cada-nove-noites/).
