## How to play

Borgund church is built of vertical wooden planks, darkened with tar. Here you stack the planks with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the stone base. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Borgund church, in Lærdal, was built around 1200, of vertical staves, and stopped being used for worship in 1868. The stacked planks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
