## How to play

Four discs sit on the left post. Take all of them to the right post, one at a time, never putting a larger disc on a smaller one. Tap a post to pick up the top disc and tap another to drop it (or use the arrows and Space). There is a move limit.

## The story behind it

Tynwald Hill, on the Isle of Man, has four stepped levels. The discs and the rules of the game are made up. Read the story in [Tynwald: the Isle of Man assembly whose name comes from Norse and which still meets on a hill](/en/2026/11/20/tynwald-ilha-de-man-thing/).
