## How to play

A heavy stone slides from side to side. Tap to drop it onto the wall. Whatever sticks out past the stone below is lost, and the wall gets narrower. If the stone falls completely outside, it counts as a mistake. With three mistakes the game ends. Reach the golden line. There are 3 walls.

## The story behind it

The stone church at Hvalsey, in Greenland, has walls of stones weighing many tonnes. Stacking to the right rhythm is invented. Read the story in [Hvalsey: the stone church of Greenland and the wedding of 1408](/hvalsey-the-stone-church-of-greenland-and-the-wedding/).
