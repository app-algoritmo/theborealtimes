## How to play

Each field hides raiders. Tap a square to reveal it: the number shows how many raiders are in the neighbouring squares, diagonals included. If the square holds a raider, he is found and counts as a mistake. Reveal every square without a raider to win. The first square never holds a raider. Three mistakes end the game. There are 3 fields.

## The story behind it

On 5 August 910, an army of Wessex and Mercia defeated the Northumbrian Vikings near Tettenhall, and three Viking kings died, according to the account. The hidden raiders are invented. Read the story in [Battle of Tettenhall, 910: Edward, Æthelflæd and the end of a Viking army in Mercia](/battle-of-tettenhall-910-edward-aethelflaed-and-the-end/).
