## How to play

In the Knock y Doonee boat, about three hundred iron rivets remained. Here you move the archaeologist's basket to catch the rivets and avoid the stones. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the archaeologist's basket and catch the iron rivets. Avoid the stones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 1927, the director of the Manx Museum, P. M. C. Kermode, excavated at Knock y Doonee, on the Isle of Man, a Viking boat burial of 900–950, with about three hundred iron rivets, a man wrapped in a cloak, weapons, smith's tools and the remains of a horse and a dog. The rivets and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
