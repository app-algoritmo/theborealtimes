## How to play

Hallgerðr had three husbands, and Njáls saga ties each one to different scenes. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “Þorvaldr”, the second is “Glúmr” and the third is “Gunnar”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia and Dasent's translation of Njáls saga, Hallgerðr married Þorvaldr, Glúmr and Gunnar, and Þjóstólfr killed the first two. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
