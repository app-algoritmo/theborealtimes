## How to play

In Egil's saga, Kveld-Úlfr fights the king's men when night falls. Here you block with the shield every blow that comes out of the dark. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Egil's saga, written in the 13th century, Kveld-Úlfr, the "Evening Wolf", was said to be hamrammr, "shape-changing", and the frenzy left him weakened in the fight against the king's men. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
