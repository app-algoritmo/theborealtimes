## How to play

At Sigtuna, Olof had the first Swedish coins struck. Four minting benches stand around the mint, and silver piles up at one of them at a time. Tap the bench to strike when its bar passes the mark, or use the arrow keys. Tapping a bench with no silver, or striking too early, is a mistake. If the silver passes the full bar, it is lost and that is also a mistake. Strike 12 times before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Olof is the first Swedish king known to have minted coins, and a few hundred survive. They were struck at Sigtuna and use the Latin rex, as in OLUF REX. The benches, the piling silver and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Olof Skötkonung: the Swedish king who minted the first coins and was baptized in four versions](/olof-skotkonung-the-swedish-king-who-minted-the-first-coins/).
