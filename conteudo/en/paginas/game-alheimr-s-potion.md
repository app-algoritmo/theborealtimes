## How to play

In the Dámusta saga, the knight Dámusti defeats the giant Alheimr at the church where Gratiana is buried, and the giant gives him a potion that revives her. Here you move Dámusti's vial to catch the drops of the potion and avoid the giant's stones. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Dámusti's vial and catch the drops of the potion. Avoid the giant's stones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Dámusta saga is an Icelandic chivalric saga. Dámusti, a pious knight at the court of King Katalaktus of Greece, kills out of jealousy King Jón of Smáland, Gratiana's husband. Gratiana falls ill, the Virgin Mary appears to Dámusti, and he defeats the giant Alheimr, who had faked her death in order to marry her. The giant gives him a potion that revives her. The saga survives in manuscripts from the fifteenth century on. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
