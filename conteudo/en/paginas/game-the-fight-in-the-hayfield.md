## How to play

At Eyvindastaðir, the men of Brodd-Helgi and of Geitir fight among the heaps of cut hay. Here you defend with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Vápnfirðinga saga, the fight at Eyvindastaðir took place in a hayfield and ended with compensation of a hundred pieces of silver. The spears in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Vápnfirðinga saga: Brodd-Helgi, Geitir and the fight in the hayfield at Eyvindastaðir](/vapnfirdinga-saga-brodd-helgi-geitir-and-the-fight/).
