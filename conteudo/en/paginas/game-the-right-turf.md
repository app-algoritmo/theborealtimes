## How to play

The sagas say Einarr earned the name Turf by teaching Orkney to burn peat. Nine mounds of earth stand before you on a hillside. A glow appears on one mound at a time, marking the turf to cut. Tap the mound that glows before the glow fades, or use keys 1 to 9. Tapping a mound with no glow, or letting a glow fade, is a mistake. Cut 12 turfs before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The sagas say Einarr earned the name "Torf", "peat", by introducing the burning of peat because wood was scarce. The source says this is incorrect, since the use of peat predates the Norse, and that the origin of the nickname is unknown. The nine mounds, the glows and the pace of the game are invention.
