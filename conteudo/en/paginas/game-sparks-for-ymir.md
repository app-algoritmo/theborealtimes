## How to play

According to the Gylfaginning, where the heat from the sparks of Múspell met the frost of Niflheim, the frost melted, and Ymir took shape from the drops. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the bowl and catch the sparks of Múspell. Avoid the blocks of hard ice: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Gylfaginning, where the heat of Múspell met the frost of Niflheim and melted it, life formed from the drops, in the shape of Ymir. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
