## How to play

Each round shows seven coins with values. Tap coins to add them up, trying to get as close as possible to 21 without going over. When you find the best possible sum, tap Stop. Going over 21 or stopping on a sum that could have been higher counts as a mistake. Play 6 rounds with at most 2 mistakes and within 100 seconds. (Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space picks, Enter stops.)

## The story behind it

In 1999, archaeologists found in Gotland more than 14,000 coins and almost 500 silver arm-rings. The game of adding up to 21 is invented. Read the story in [Spillings: the 67-kilo silver hoard found on Gotland during a film shoot](/en/2026/12/27/spillings-tesouro-prata-gotland-1999/).
