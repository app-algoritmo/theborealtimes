## How to play

In Selma Lagerlöf's novel, Nils travels across Sweden on the back of the goose Martin. Here you run across the fields in three lanes, gather the grains of wheat and dodge the fences. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the grains of wheat and dodge the wooden fences: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

*Nils Holgerssons underbara resa genom Sverige*, by Selma Lagerlöf, came out in 1906 and 1907, and in it the boy Nils travels with the goose Martin and with wild geese. The run, the grains and the fences in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Nils Holgersson: Selma Lagerlöf's wonderful journey, the shrunken boy who crossed Sweden on the back of a goose](/nils-holgersson-selma-lagerlof-s-wonderful-journey/).
