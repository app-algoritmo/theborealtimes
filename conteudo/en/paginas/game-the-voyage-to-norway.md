## How to play

Gunnar and his brother Helgi travel to Norway. Here the ship follows three lanes of sea to the coast of the fjords. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the water barrels for the voyage and dodge the rocks of the fjord: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Gunnars saga Keldugnúpsfífls, Gunnar goes to Norway with his brother Helgi, defeats one of Jarl Haakon's men and ends up married. The run and the barrels in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Gunnars saga Keldugnúpsfífls: the lad thought a fool who went to Norway and faced the men of Jarl Haakon](/gunnars-saga-keldugnupsfifls-the-lad-thought-a-fool/).
