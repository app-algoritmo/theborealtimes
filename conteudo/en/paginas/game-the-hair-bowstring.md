## How to play

In Njáls saga, Gunnar asks Hallgerðr for locks of her hair to remake his bowstring, and she refuses. In the game, the cord gets twisted. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to twist the cord. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 10 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Njáls saga (chapter 76), Gunnar asks Hallgerðr to twist locks of her hair into a new bowstring, and she answers by recalling the slap. In the game the cord is made, and that is not in the saga. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
