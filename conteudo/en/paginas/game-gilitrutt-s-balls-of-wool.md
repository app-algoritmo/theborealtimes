## How to play

In the Icelandic tale, a giant troll woman weaves the lazy wife's wool. Here you move the wool basket to catch the balls of wool and avoid the troll's stones. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the wool basket and catch the balls of wool. Avoid the troll's stones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Gilitrutt is an Icelandic tale collected by Jón Árnason (1862–1864): a lazy young woman hands her wool to a troll woman, who will only return it if the young woman guesses her name; the husband hears the name in a verse of the troll's. The versions vary. The balls of wool and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
