## How to play

Hold your finger (or the space bar) to move along the trail. When the eyes of Stállo glow, he is about to look back: let go! If he sees you moving, you go back a stretch and count a mistake. The third mistake ends the game. Reach the end of the trail before time runs out.

## The story behind it

Stállo is a giant of Sámi tales. The trail and the giant’s gaze are made up for the game. Read the story in [Stállo: the foolish giant of the Sámi tales, who always loses to the cunning of those he wants to devour](/en/2026/11/19/stallo-gigante-conto-sami/).
