## How to play

Tap the bubbles with the six impossible things (a cat’s footsteps, a woman’s beard, a mountain’s roots, a bear’s sinews, a fish’s breath, a bird’s spittle) and avoid the ordinary ones: three mistakes and the dwarfs give up. Then hold the screen (or the space bar) to tighten the ribbon and release to loosen it. Keep the marker in the green zone until the wolf gives in.

## The story behind it

In the story told by Snorri Sturluson in the Prose Edda, the dwarfs make the ribbon Gleipnir from six things that do not exist, and the gods use it to bind Fenrir. Gathering them as a game is the game’s invention. Read the story in [How the Norse gods bound the wolf Fenrir, and why Týr lost his hand](/en/2026/11/12/fenrir-lobo-corrente-gleipnir/).
