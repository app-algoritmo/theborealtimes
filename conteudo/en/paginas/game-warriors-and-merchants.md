## How to play

According to the Primary Chronicle, Oleg hid warriors in boats and posed as a merchant. Warriors in iron helmets and merchants in blue cloaks walk toward the quay of Kiev. Tap the warriors to stop them before they reach the quay. Do not tap the merchants, who come to trade. A warrior who reaches the quay is a mistake, and so is tapping a merchant. Stop 14 warriors before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Primary Chronicle, in 882 Oleg hid his warriors in boats and sent word to Askold and Dir that he was a merchant on his way to Greece. When they came out, the soldiers leaped from the boats. The source reminds us that the story is semi-legendary. The advancing warriors and the pace of the game are invention.
