## How to play

The Bifröst bridge has three colours, and the gods can cross it only if you light the colours in the right order. Watch the sequence of colours that lights up and, when it ends, tap the colours in the same order, or use keys 1, 2 and 3. Each round the sequence grows by one colour. A wrong colour is a mistake, and the sequence is shown again. Get 10 colours right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Snorri's Prose Edda, written in the thirteenth century, the Bifröst bridge has three colours, and the gods cross it every day, except Thor. The sequence of colours you repeat is invention. Read the story in [Bifröst: the three-coloured bridge the gods cross every day](/en/2027/03/21/bifrost-a-ponte-de-tres-cores-que-os-deuses-cruzam-todo-dia/).
