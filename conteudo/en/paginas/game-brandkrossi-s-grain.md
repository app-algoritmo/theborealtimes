## How to play

In the Brandkrossa þáttr, Grímr returns from Norway with the hide of the ox Brandkrossi full of grain, a gift from Geitir, his wife's father. Here you stack the sacks of grain with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it in Grímr's barn. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Brandkrossa þáttr, an Icelandic tale linked to the Droplaugarsona saga, Grímr's ox Brandkrossi swims out to sea in a fit of fury. Grímr goes to Norway, meets Geitir, son of a troll, marries his daughter Droplaug and learns that it was Geitir who made the ox vanish. Geitir sends him off with many gifts, among them the ox's hide full of grain. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
