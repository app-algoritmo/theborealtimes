## How to play

Askeladden comes back from the forest with the silver and gold he found on the hillside. Nine mounds of earth are before you. A gleam of metal appears on one mound at a time. Tap the mound that gleams before the gleam fades, or use keys 1 to 9. Tapping a mound without a gleam, or letting a gleam fade, is a mistake. Gather 12 finds before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, after beating the troll, Askeladden takes the silver and gold he finds on the hillside, and the money helps pay off the family's debt. The nine mounds, the gleams and the rhythm of the game are invention.
