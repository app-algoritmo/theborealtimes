## How to play

In the Þorsteins þáttr uxafóts, Þorsteinn and Styrkárr face the trolls of the forest of Heiðarskógr, at King Óláfr's request. Here you block with the shield every blow from the trolls. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Þorsteins þáttr uxafóts, a tale preserved in the Flateyjarbók, King Óláfr Tryggvason wants to clear the forest of Heiðarskógr of trolls. Þorsteinn, son of Ornný and Ívarr, and his friend Styrkárr kill the trolls and take the treasure. The king gives him the nickname Uxafótr, "Ox-Foot". The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
