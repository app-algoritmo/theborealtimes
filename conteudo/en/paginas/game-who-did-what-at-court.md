## How to play

In Morkinskinna, Halldór Snorrason and King Harald quarrel over money. An act appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the one who did it, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is Halldór, the second is King Harald and the third is the queen. A wrong box, or an act left unanswered, is a mistake. Link 10 acts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Halldórs þáttr Snorrasonar inn síðari, preserved in Morkinskinna, Harald invents a war so that Halldór can recruit men and later refuses to pay the last half mark. Halldór wakes the king with a sword at his throat and takes the queen's ring, as she criticizes her husband's stinginess. Then he flees to Iceland. The row of acts, the three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention.
