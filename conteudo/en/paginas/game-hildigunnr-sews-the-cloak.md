## How to play

In Dasent's translation of Njáls saga, Hildigunnr is so skilful with her hands that few were equally skilful. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to make a stitch in the cloak. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Dasent's translation of Njáls saga, Hildigunnr was so skilful with her hands that few women were equally skilful. The cloak of the saga is Flosi's gift to Höskuldr, which she keeps in her chest. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Hildigunnr and the blood-stained cloak: the widow who made Flosi avenge Höskuldr](/hildigunnr-and-the-blood-stained-cloak-the-widow-who-made/).
