## How to play

In 939, Eldgjá opened a fissure of about 40 km in the Katla system. Here you run along three lanes, pick up the sheep and dodge the lava cracks. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the sheep and dodge the lava cracks: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.
