## How to play

The door of the mound is hidden in one square of the map. Tap a square to probe it: the number shows the distance to the door, counting steps up, down and sideways, with no diagonals. Zero is the door. Find the door with a limited number of probes. Time is limited. There are 3 maps, each larger than the last.

## The story behind it

In Orkney folklore, the hogboon, or hogboy, is a spirit of mounds; tales warn against digging them, and storms after excavations are linked to this. Searching for the door is invented. Read the story in [Hogboon: the mound spirit of Orkney and the stories of Maeshowe](/en/2027/02/14/hogboon-o-espirito-do-monte-orcades-maeshowe/).
