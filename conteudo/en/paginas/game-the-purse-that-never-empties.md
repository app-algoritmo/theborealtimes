## How to play

In the Januals ljóð, the fairy lady gives the knight Janual wealth and a purse that never empties, but the queen accuses him before the king. Here you move the purse to catch the coins and avoid the accusations. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Janual's purse and catch the coins. Avoid the queen's accusations: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Januals ljóð, from the Strengleikar and the Norwegian version of Marie de France's Lanval, the knight Janual, forgotten at King Artús's court, receives from a fairy lady wealth and a purse that never empties, on condition that he not speak of her. In refusing the queen, he lets the secret slip, and she accuses him. At the trial the lady arrives on a white palfrey, and he is cleared. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
