## How to play

The Ragnarsdrápa describes the scenes of a shield, speaks of a Ragnar and uses the dróttkvætt metre. Sort what belongs to each subject. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Ragnarsdrápa, the shield Ragnar gave to Bragi had scenes such as Hamðir and Sörli against Jörmunrekkr. The poem uses the dróttkvætt and was addressed to a Ragnar whom scholars debate. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
