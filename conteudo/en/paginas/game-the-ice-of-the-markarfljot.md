## How to play

In Njáls saga, Skarphéðinn crosses the ice of the Markarfljót river to reach Þráinn. Here you run across the ice in three lanes, pick up the axes and dodge the cracks of black water. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the axes and dodge the cracks of black water: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Njáls saga, written in Iceland between 1270 and 1290, Skarphéðinn crosses the ice of the Markarfljót and kills Þráinn with the axe Rimmugýgr. The run, the axes and the cracks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
