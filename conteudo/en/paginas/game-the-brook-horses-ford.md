## How to play

The brook horse waits at a hidden ford in the river. Tap a stone to step on it: the colour shows whether the ford is far or near (blue cold, yellow mild, orange warm, red boiling). Find the ford within 7 tries and 90 seconds. (Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space steps.)

## The story behind it

In Swedish tradition, the brook horse waits by the water and whoever climbs on it never comes down. The hidden ford and the clues are invented. Read the story in [Bäckahäst: the white brook horse that drowns whoever climbs on its back](/en/2026/12/27/backahast-cavalo-do-riacho-folclore-sueco/).
