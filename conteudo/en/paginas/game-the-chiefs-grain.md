## How to play

Every store-room holds 4 grains. Pick one of yours (bottom row) and the grains are sown one per room, towards the right. If the last one lands in your granary (right), play again. If it lands in an empty room of yours, you also take the grains in the room opposite. End with more grain in your granary than your rival.

## The story behind it

At Borg, in the Lofoten Islands, a great chieftain longhouse was excavated. The grain-sowing game is made up. Read the story in [Borg, in the Lofoten: the 83-metre longhouse of a chieftain of the far north](/en/2026/11/22/borg-lofoten-casa-longa-do-chefe/).
