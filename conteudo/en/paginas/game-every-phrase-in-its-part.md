## How to play

The Karlevi stone, on Öland, has a prose part, a stanza and an inscription on the back. A phrase appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the part of the stone where it is, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the prose, the second is the stanza in dróttkvætt and the third is the back. A wrong box, or a phrase left unanswered, is a mistake. Link 10 phrases before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the Karlevi stone (Öl 1), from the late tenth century, has a prose part that names Sibba, son of Fuldar, a stanza in dróttkvætt and, on the back, a possibly Christian inscription, a cross and a hammer of Thor. The row of phrases, the three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention.
