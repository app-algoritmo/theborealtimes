## How to play

At the Althing of the year 1000, the Icelander Þorgeirr decided on a single law for Christians and heathens. Here you stack the blocks of that law with precision on the Law Rock. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the Law Rock. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At the Althing of the year 1000, according to Ari Þorgilsson, in the Íslendingabók, Þorgeirr Ljósvetningagoði lay under his cloak for a day and a night and then ruled that all Icelanders were to be baptised. The blocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
