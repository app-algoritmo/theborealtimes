## How to play

In the Kalevala, the tiny hero sharpened his axe on stones and felled the oak in four blows. The chips drift out over the sea. Tap the wood chips, small and brown, to gather them. The whetstones are grey and round: do not tap them. Tapping a stone is a mistake. Gather 14 chips before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In runo 2 of the Kalevala, a tiny hero who came out of the sea sharpens his axe on stones and fells the oak in four blows. The chips drift over the sea, and a maiden of Pohjola gathers them. The current, the stones and the rhythm of the game are invention.
