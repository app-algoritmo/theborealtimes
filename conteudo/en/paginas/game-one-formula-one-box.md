## How to play

The Norwegian black books mixed recipes of healing, protection and dangerous magic. Take each formula to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

More than a hundred Norwegian svarteboker survive, from about 1480 to 1850; the oldest, the Vinjeboka, found in 1796 under the floor of a church, measures 7 by 4.8 cm. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
