## How to play

Ölkofri fell asleep by the charcoal, and the fire spread through the forest of the six chieftains. Three stretches of woodland stand before you. Wait in silence. When sparks appear in front of a stretch, tap it to put them out before the fire spreads, or use keys 1, 2 and 3. Tapping before sparks appear, tapping the wrong stretch or letting the fire spread without tapping is a mistake. Put out 10 fires before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Ölkofra þáttr, Ölkofri burns charcoal, falls asleep, and the fire spreads to the forest of six chieftains. The three stretches of woodland, the sparks and the rhythm of the game are invention.
