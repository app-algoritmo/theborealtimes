## How to play

The light leaves the left bonfire and travels straight until it hits a mirror. Tap a mirror to flip it (from / to \ and back). Make the beam reach the other bonfire, at the edge of the board, flipping mirrors no more than the number shown, within 75 seconds. (Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space flips.)

## The story behind it

The Orkneyinga saga makes Thorfinn Sigurdsson a warrior earl with lands in Scotland. The mirrors and the bonfires are invented. Read the story in [Thorfinn the Mighty: the earl of Orkney who, according to the saga, became earl at five](/en/2026/12/27/thorfinn-conde-das-orcades-orkneyinga-saga/).
