## How to play

Tap Roll to throw the die: each result adds to the voyage haul on the boat. If a 1 comes up, the voyage haul is lost and a new voyage begins. Tap Bank to take the haul to the harbour. Bank 50 points within 14 voyages and 140 seconds. (Keyboard: Space rolls, Enter banks.)

## The story behind it

Among the Vanir, Njörðr is the lord of wind, sea and fishing. The dice game and the harbour are invented. Read the story in [Njörðr: the god of the sea and wealth who married Skaði and could not stand the mountains](/en/2026/12/27/njordr-deus-do-mar-skadi-nootun/).
