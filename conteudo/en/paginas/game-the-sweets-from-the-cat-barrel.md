## How to play

At Fastelavn, children hit a barrel of sweets painted with a cat until it breaks. Here you catch in the basket the sweets and oranges that fall and dodge the pieces of wood. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the basket and catch the sweets and oranges. Avoid the pieces of the barrel: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Danish Fastelavn, a barrel of sweets, sometimes with oranges, is painted with a cat and hit until it breaks; whoever knocks out the bottom becomes queen of the cats. The sweets, oranges and pieces of wood in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
