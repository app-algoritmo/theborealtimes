## How it works

You enter your email and tick the consent box. We send a confirmation message, and the subscription only counts once you click its link. This makes sure nobody is signed up by someone else.

## What we keep

Your email, the language you chose, the date of sign-up and of confirmation, and the version of the consent text. We do not store your IP and we do not ask for a name. The data is held on a server in Norway. Details are in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).

## How to unsubscribe

Every email carries an unsubscribe link at the bottom. If you prefer, write to post@borealtimes.org.
