## How to play

In the Gríms saga loðinkinna, Grímr sails back to Hrafnista with a boat full of whale meat. Here you tap the pieces of whale that appear on the beach and do not tap the trolls' claws. Tap the pieces of whale as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the trolls' claws: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Gríms saga loðinkinna, an Icelandic legendary saga, Grímr, of Hrafnista, fights Hreiðarr and his men over a stranded whale, is saved by a troll who turns out to be his bride Lopthæna, bewitched by her stepmother, and sails home with a boat full of whale meat. One translation dates the saga to the early fourteenth century; the transformation into a troll is a folktale motif. The pieces and the claws in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
