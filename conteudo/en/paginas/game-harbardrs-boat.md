## How to play

Take everyone to the other bank. Tap a passenger to put it in the boat (or take it out) and then ▶ to cross. Puzzle 1: Thor rows and takes one at a time; without him, the wolf eats the goat and the goat eats the hay. Puzzle 2: three men and three trolls, two seats in the boat; on a bank with men, the trolls may never outnumber them. Crossings are limited.

## The story behind it

In the Hárbarðsljóð the ferryman refuses to take Thor. The crossing puzzle is made up. Read the story in [Hárbarðsljóð: the ferryman who would not take Thor across](/en/2026/11/29/harbardsljod-thor-odin-barqueiro/).
