## How to play

The ram and the pig built a house in the forest with the goose, the hare and the rooster. Grey wolves and white animals of the house walk toward the door. Tap the wolves to drive them off before they reach the door. Do not tap the animals of the house. A wolf that reaches the door is a mistake, and tapping an animal of the house is too. Drive off 14 wolves before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the ram, the pig, the goose, the hare and the rooster build a house in the forest and drive off two wolves, each in his own way. The wolves arriving in a line and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [The sheep and the pig who set up house in the forest: an Asbjørnsen and Moe tale and the wolves](/the-sheep-and-the-pig-who-set-up-house-in-the-forest/).
