## How to play

In the Hjálmþés saga ok Ölvis, the slave Hörðr leads the companions along beaches where troll women attack at night. Here you run along the beach in three lanes, gather the gold coins and dodge the troll women. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the gold coins and dodge the troll women: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Hjálmþés saga ok Ölvis, an Icelandic legendary saga, the stepmother Lúða curses Hjálmþér, who may rest nowhere but on ships or in a tent until he sees Hervör, Hundingr's daughter. The slave Hörðr guides him, kills nine troll women on a beach and in the end turns out to be King Hringr, bewitched by Lúða. There is no secure date for the saga. The run and the coins in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
