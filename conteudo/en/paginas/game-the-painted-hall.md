## How to play

Óláfr pái had myths painted on the walls of the hall at Hjarðarholt, and the poet Úlfr Uggason described the paintings. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the hall wall. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Húsdrápa, Óláfr pái's hall had painted myths, such as Baldr's funeral and Thor fishing for Jörmungandr. The assembly of panels in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Húsdrápa: the poem Úlfr Uggason recited in Óláfr pái's painted hall at Hjarðarholt](/husdrapa-the-poem-ulfr-uggason-recited-in-olafr-pai-s/).
