## How to play

In the eighth branch of the Karlamagnús saga, Charlemagne's rearguard is attacked by the Saracens at Runzival, and Roland fights to the end. Here you block with the shield every blow of the Saracen warriors. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The eighth branch of the Karlamagnús saga, the battle of Runzival, is the Norse version of the Song of Roland and is textually close to the Oxford manuscript of the poem, with a few notable differences. Ganelon betrays the army, Roland's rearguard is surrounded by the Saracens, and Roland sounds the horn only at the end. The branch was the basis of the Norwegian ballad Roland og Magnus kongen. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
