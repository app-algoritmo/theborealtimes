## How to play

On Odin's spear, the Skáldskaparmál tells the origin, the Völuspá a thrown spear and the Sigrdrífumál the runes. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Skáldskaparmál, the sons of Ivaldi made Gungnir and it hits any target. The Völuspá speaks of a spear thrown over the Vanir, without naming it, and the Sigrdrífumál says runes were carved on its point. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
