## How to play

After Holmengrå, in 1139, Sigurd Slembe was captured and betrayed. Here you run through the forest in three lanes, collect the torches and flee King Inge's soldiers. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the torches and dodge the soldiers: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Sigurd Slembe, the "noisy deacon", claimed to be the illegitimate son of King Magnus III. He proclaimed himself king in 1135 and, in 1136, had Harald Gille killed in his sleep. He freed Magnus the Blind and made him co-king, but was declared an outlaw. On 12 November 1139, after Holmengrå, he was captured and died under torture. The sources rest on the lost Hryggjarstykki and are hostile to him. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
