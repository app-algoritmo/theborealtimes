## How to play

The rowing regattas are one of the highlights of Ólavsøka, in Tórshavn. Here you mark the rowing stroke at the right moment. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to keep the rhythm of the rowing. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Ólavsøka, "the wake of Olaf", is celebrated on 28 and 29 July in the Faroe Islands, and the national rowing finals are on 28 July. The rhythm and the regatta in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Ólavsøka: the Faroe Islands' national festival on 28 and 29 July, tied to Saint Olaf's death at Stiklestad](/olavsoka-the-faroe-islands-national-festival-on-28-and-29/).
