## How to play

In the Sturlaugs saga starfsama, Sturlaugr sails north in search of an aurochs horn, as far as Bjarmaland. Here you mark the rhythm of the fleet's oars, one stroke at a time. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to row the fleet to Bjarmaland. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Sturlaugs saga starfsama, an Icelandic legendary saga usually dated to the fourteenth century, Sturlaugr earns the nickname "the Industrious" when he is sent to fetch King Haraldr's aurochs horn; the fleet sails north, passes Hundingjaland and reaches Bjarmaland, where he takes the horn. Dates, names of peoples and numbers belong to adventure tradition. The rhythm of the oars in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
