## How to play

Avaldsnes lies on the Karmsundet, where ships pass through a narrow channel. Here the ships appear in the channel, among the rocks. Tap the ships that pass through the channel as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the rocks of the channel: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Avaldsnes, on Karmøy, lies on the Karmsundet strait, and tradition links it to Harald Fairhair around 870. There are the oldest ship burial of the Nordic countries and a 13th-century stone church. The ships and rocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
