## How to play

Widsith is a travelling singer who goes from court to court and people to people. Here you keep the beat of the harp that goes with the song. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to play the singer's harp. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Widsith, an Anglo-Saxon poem of 143 lines preserved in the Exeter Book, lists kings, peoples and heroes visited by a travelling singer. The harp and the rhythm in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
