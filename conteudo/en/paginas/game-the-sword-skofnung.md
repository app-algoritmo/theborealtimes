## How to play

In the saga, Skeggi has the sword Skofnung and kills Þórhallr with it. Here you block each sword blow with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Þórðar saga hreðu, written around 1350, Skeggi kills Þórhallr with the sword Skofnung. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Þórðar saga hreðu: the fugitive from Norway, the sword Skofnung and Sǫrli's duel](/thordar-saga-hredu-the-fugitive-from-norway-the-sword/).
