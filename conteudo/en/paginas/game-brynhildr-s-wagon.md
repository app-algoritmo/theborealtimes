## How to play

In the poem, Brynhildr rides a richly decorated wagon toward Hel and speaks with a giantess on the way. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to keep the wagon steady on the road. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Helreið Brynhildar, Brynhildr is burned on a pyre and travels on a decorated wagon to Hel. The chocks and wheels in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Helreið Brynhildar: Brynhildr's journey by wagon to Hel and her talk with the giantess](/helreid-brynhildar-brynhildr-s-journey-by-wagon-to-hel/).
