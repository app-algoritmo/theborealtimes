## How to play

The Karlamagnús saga gathers in ten branches the stories of Charlemagne and his paladins. Here you stack ten blocks, one for each branch, with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the pedestal of the throne. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Karlamagnús saga was compiled in Norway in the late thirteenth century and gathers in ten branches stories of Charlemagne and his paladins, from Latin historiography and French chansons de geste. Branch IV, about King Agulandus, is the longest, and branch VIII, the battle of Runzival, is a version of the Song of Roland. A Danish abridgment and a Swedish verse translation show that the work was read outside Norway. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
