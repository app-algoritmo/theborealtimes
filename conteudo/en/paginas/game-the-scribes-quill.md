## How to play

On Iona, the monks copied books by hand, page by page. Here you mark the rhythm of a scribe's quill before the ships arrive. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to copy the pages. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Annals of Ulster record an attack on islands of the western coast in 795 and the killing of 68 members of the Iona community in 806; afterwards part of the community went to Kells, in Ireland. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Iona: the Vikings at Columba's monastery, the raid of 806, Martyrs' Bay and the book that fled to Kells](/iona-the-vikings-at-columba-s-monastery-the-raid-of-806/).
