## How to play

In the tale of the Icelander who knew sagas, everything happens in order. A scene appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the moment of the tale it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the beginning, the second is the middle and the third is the end. A wrong box, or a scene left unanswered, is a mistake. Put 10 scenes in place before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Íslendings þáttr sögufróða, the young Icelander tells sagas to King Harald, fears telling the one about the king's travels at Christmas and later says he learned it from Halldórr Snorrason. The three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [The Icelander who told sagas: the Christmas story at the court of King Harald Hardrada](/the-icelander-who-told-sagas-the-christmas-story/).
