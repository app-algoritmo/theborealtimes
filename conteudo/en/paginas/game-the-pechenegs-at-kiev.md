## How to play

The Pechenegs attacked Kiev in Vladimir's time. Here you block with the shield each blow of the Pecheneg horsemen. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the Pechenegs killed Vladimir's father, Sviatoslav, in 972, and their attacks on Kiev cut short the campaign of 992. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
