## How to play

In 1239, the supporters of Skule Bårdsson proclaimed him king at the Øyrating, the assembly of Trøndelag. Here you tap the banners of the Øyrating and do not tap the torches. Tap the banners as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the torches: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 1239, the supporters of Skule Bårdsson proclaimed him king at the Øyrating, in Trøndelag; on 24 May 1240 he was killed at Elgeseter priory, burned by Håkon's men. The banners and the torches in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
