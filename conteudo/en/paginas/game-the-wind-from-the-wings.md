## How to play

At the edge of the sky, the giant Hræsvelgr, in the shape of an eagle, beats his wings, and the wind is born beneath them. Gusts of wind cross the screen. The blue ones go to the right, toward the peoples: tap them to push them on. The red ones come back against the wings: let them pass. Tapping a red gust is a mistake. Push 14 blue gusts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Vafþrúðnismál and the Gylfaginning, the giant Hræsvelgr, in the shape of an eagle, sits at the edge of the sky, and the wind comes from his wings. The blue and red gusts and the rhythm of the game are invention.
