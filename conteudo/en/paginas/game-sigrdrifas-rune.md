## How to play

The numbers show how many cells in a row must be filled in each row and column, in order, with gaps between the groups. Tap a cell to fill it; tap again to mark ✕ (empty) and once more to clear it. Reveal the rune before time runs out.

## The story behind it

In the Sigrdrífumál, the valkyrie teaches runes to Sigurd. The clue grid is made up. Read the story in [Sigrdrífumál: the valkyrie who woke up and taught Sigurd the runes](/en/2026/12/06/sigrdrifumal-valquiria-ensina-runas/).
