## How to play

In the poem, twelve women weave on a loom made of warriors' parts and sing as they work. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to pass the shuttle through the loom. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Darraðarljóð, twelve valkyries weave on a warp-weighted loom, with entrails for warp, swords for treadles and an arrow for a beater, and the cloth is the fate of the battle of Clontarf. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Darraðarljóð: the weaving song of the valkyries that Dörruð saw in Caithness before the battle of Clontarf](/darradarljod-the-weaving-song-of-the-valkyries-that-dorrud/).
