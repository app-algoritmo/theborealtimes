## How to play

Steer the ship across the northern sea. Hold the left or right side of the screen to turn, or use the arrow keys, A and D. Collect the golden runes and dodge the drifting rocks and ice. Gather 10 runes before time runs out. Three mistakes sink the ship.

## The story behind it

At Gjellestad, in Norway, a georadar found a buried Viking ship. The sea crossing is invented. Read the story in [Gjellestad: the Viking ship found by georadar in Halden, Norway](/en/2027/02/28/gjellestad-o-navio-viking-achado-por-georadar-em-halden-noruega/).
