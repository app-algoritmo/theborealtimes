## How to play

A farmer catches a marbendill, the sea man. Nine spots of water lie before you. The marbendill appears at one spot at a time, with a blue-green glow. Tap the spot where he appears before he dives, or use keys 1 to 9. Tapping empty water, or letting the marbendill dive, is a mistake. Catch 12 appearances before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Jón Árnason's tale, a farmer catches a marbendill, who laughs three times and frees himself by explaining why. The spots of water, the appearances and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [The marbendill who laughed three times: the merman of Icelandic folklore](/en/2027/04/04/o-marbendill-que-riu-tres-vezes-o-homem-do-mar-do-folclore-islandes/).
