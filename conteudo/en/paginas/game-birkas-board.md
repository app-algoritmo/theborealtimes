## How to play

You play the light pieces. Tap a marked square to place a piece: all rival pieces trapped in a straight line between it and another of yours flip to your side. At the end, whoever has more pieces wins. You have 3 minutes.

## The story behind it

Ansgar was a missionary in Sweden (Birka) in the 9th century. The board game is made up. Read the story in [Ansgar: the monk who took Christianity to Denmark and Birka, and saw Hamburg burn](/en/2026/12/06/ansgar-missionario-birka-hamburgo/).
