## How to play

Three old women help the girl spin, weave and sew the flax. A sentence about them appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the aunt it refers to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the spinner, the second is the weaver and the third is the seamstress. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the first hag spins the flax and has a long nose, the second weaves it and has a humped back, and the third makes the shirts and has red eyes, as large as saucers. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention. Read the story in [The Three Aunts: the Norwegian tale of the girl who spun, wove and sewed without working](/the-three-aunts-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-girl-who-spun/).
