## How to play

Two groups of boats meet on a narrow stretch, with a single free place. Swap the sides: the blue boats only go right and the orange ones only go left. Tap a boat to move it into the free place, next to it or jumping over one boat. If no one can move any more, it is over. Time is limited. There are 3 stretches.

## The story behind it

Gnezdovo is a large Varangian site near Smolensk. Swapping boats from side to side is invented. Read the story in [Gnezdovo: the great Varangian site near Smolensk, three thousand mounds and an inscription](/en/2027/02/28/gnezdovo-o-grande-sitio-varegue-perto-de-smolensk-tres-mil-tumulos-e-uma-inscricao/).
