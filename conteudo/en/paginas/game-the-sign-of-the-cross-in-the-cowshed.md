## How to play

According to the legends, the sign of the cross was made under the udder to protect the cattle from the tilberi. Here you block each attack with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Icelandic folklore, the tilberi is made by a woman from a stolen rib and wool, and tradition protected the animals with the sign of the cross under the udder and over the rump. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
