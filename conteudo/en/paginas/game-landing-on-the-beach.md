## How to play

The ship falls by itself. Hold your finger on the screen (or the Space key) to fire the thrust, which burns fuel, and drag your finger (or use ← →) to take the ship to the golden beach. Land on the beach slowly enough: speed below the green mark on the bar. Hitting the rocks or falling in the sea ends the voyage.

## The story behind it

The Landnámabók tells who arrived in Iceland and where they settled. The landing with limited fuel is invented. Read the story in [Landnámabók: the book that lists more than three thousand people and about 1,400 farms of Iceland](/en/2026/12/27/landnamabok-livro-dos-assentamentos-islandia/).
