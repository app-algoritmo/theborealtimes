## How to play

The Kalmar Union joined Denmark, Sweden and Norway under one monarch. Here you stack the blocks of the three-realm crown with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the cushion of the crown. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Erik of Pomerania was crowned in Kalmar on 17 June 1397 as king of Denmark, Sweden and Norway, and the union lasted more than a century. The stacked blocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
