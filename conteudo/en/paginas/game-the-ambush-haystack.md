## How to play

In the saga, Víglundr's enemies set an ambush in a haystack. Here you stack the bales until the haystack stands. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it in the lovers' field. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Víglundar saga, an ambush is set in a haystack and the story ends with a quadruple wedding. The stacked bales in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
