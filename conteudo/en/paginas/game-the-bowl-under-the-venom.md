## How to play

Bound to the rocks, the serpent drips venom on Loki, and Sigyn holds the bowl. Three spots are before you. Wait in silence. When green drips appear before one spot, tap that spot to put the bowl there before the venom falls, or use keys 1, 2 and 3. Tapping before the drips appear, tapping the wrong spot or letting the venom fall is a mistake. Catch 10 drops before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the prose of the Lokasenna and the Gylfaginning, Sigyn holds a basin under the serpent's venom and, when it fills, steps away to empty it, and then the venom falls on Loki's face. The three spots and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Sigyn: Loki's wife, who held the bowl under the venom](/sigyn-loki-s-wife-who-held-the-bowl-under-the-venom/).
