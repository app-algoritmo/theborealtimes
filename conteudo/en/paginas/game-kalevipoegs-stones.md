## How to play

Each stone shows its weight (large number) and its value (number with a star). Tap stones to put them in or take them out of the sack. The sack holds a maximum weight, and you must gather at least the target value without going over that weight. There is a choice that reaches the target. There are 3 rounds, and time is limited.

## The story behind it

The Kalevipoeg, an Estonian epic compiled by Friedrich Reinhold Kreutzwald after 1850, tells of the deeds of a giant hero, among them stone-throwing. Choosing stones for the sack is invented. Read the story in [Kalevipoeg: Kreutzwald's Estonian epic, the giant hero and the thrown stones](/en/2027/01/31/kalevipoeg-epopeia-estoniana-kreutzwald/).
