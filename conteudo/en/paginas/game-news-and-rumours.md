## How to play

Einarr spreads the rumour that Þorsteinn has died. Here the news arrives as parchment scrolls, white if true and red if rumour. Tap the white scrolls of true news as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the red scrolls of rumours: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Þorsteins saga hvíta, Einarr spreads the rumour of Þorsteinn's death to marry Helga Krákadóttir, and Þorsteinn's revenge brings tragic consequences. The scrolls in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
