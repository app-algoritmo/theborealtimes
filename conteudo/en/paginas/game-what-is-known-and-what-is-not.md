## How to play

Little comes from the poem, and the rest is hypothesis or gaps. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “What the poem says”, the second is “What is proposed” and the third is “What is not known”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, in the Völuspá Gullveig is pierced by spears and burned three times, and the etymology of the name, the identification with Freyja and the idea that she caused the war are proposals, not certainties. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
