## How to play

In the Hrólfs saga Gautrekssonar, Þornbjörg fortifies Ullarakr, and Hrólfr only takes it after building a wooden siege engine. Here you stack the timbers of the siege tower with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it at the foot of the walls of Ullarakr. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Hrólfs saga Gautrekssonar, an Icelandic legendary saga, the Gautish prince Hrólfr is repelled with pitch, water and stones when he attacks Ullarakr, the stronghold of the warrior princess Þornbjörg, and only wins when he builds a wooden siege engine; he then marries her. The saga has no known historical basis. The tower in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
