## How to play

In the Fáfnismál, Sigurd says the fear-helm shields no man who faces a valiant foe. Here you face the dragon with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Fáfnismál, the dragon says he wore the fear-helm to frighten men and felt mightier than any of them. Sigurd answers that the helm does not shield whoever faces a valiant foe. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
