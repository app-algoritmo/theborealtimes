## How to play

The Sonatorrek has 25 stanzas, and Wikipedia divides them into seven parts. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “Stanzas 1 to 12”, the second is “Stanzas 13 to 21” and the third is “Stanzas 22 to 25”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the Sonatorrek mourns the sons Gunnar and Böðvarr, passes through anger at Óðinn and ends in the calm of stanza 25. This game does not reproduce the verses. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention. Read the story in [Sonatorrek, the irreparable loss of sons: the poem of mourning Egill composed so as not to starve](/sonatorrek-the-irreparable-loss-of-sons-the-poem/).
