## How to play

In the Hrafns þáttr Guðrúnarsonar, Hrafn kills Ketill in Trondheim, tells Signý and Helga everything, who give him clothes and food, and flees into the forest. Here you run through the forest in three lanes, gather the food and dodge the pursuers' spears. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect Signý's and Helga's food and dodge the pursuers' spears: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Hrafns þáttr Guðrúnarsonar, an Icelandic tale, Hrafn, son of Guðrún, avenges his father, is declared an outlaw, flees to Norway and kills there the king's official Ketill. Signý and Helga give him clothes and food, and he flees into the forest. Later, King Magnús the Good finds him, and Hrafn is pardoned after fighting in Denmark beside the king and his uncle, the poet Sighvatr. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
