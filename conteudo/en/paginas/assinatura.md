## What the subscription gives you

Reading without advertising, in the app, with articles in both languages and the full archive.

Everything in the paper stays open and free on the website, without exception and without a reading limit. The subscription does not buy access: it buys quiet.

## How it works

Subscriptions are taken out on the App Store or Google Play and billed by the store. Price, currency, renewal and cancellation appear in your Apple or Google account.

## Cancellation

Cancel in the store itself, under your account's subscription settings. Access continues to the end of the period already paid for. Refund requests follow the policy of the store that processed the payment.

## Team access

Organisations wanting a subscription for several people should write to assinatura@borealtimes.org. There is no editorial consideration of any kind.
