## How to play

Move the basket (drag your finger or use ← → / A D) to catch what falls: bread, milk, wool and eggs. Do not catch the pig! Collect 25 items before making 3 mistakes or time runs out.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the husband swaps places with his wife and everything goes wrong, even the cow on the roof. The basket game is made up. Read the story in [The man who was to mind the house: the Norwegian tale of the cow on the roof](/en/2026/11/29/mannen-som-skulle-stelle-hjemme-vaca-no-telhado/).
