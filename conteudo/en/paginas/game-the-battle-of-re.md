## How to play

In January 1177, the Birkebeiners suffered a crushing defeat at Re, in Vestfold. Here you hold the shield and parry the blows and arrows that come over the snow. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Eystein Meyla, "the maiden", was the son of King Eystein II. The Birkebeiners formed around him in 1174, took Trondheim and proclaimed him king at the Øretinget in 1176. In January 1177, they were crushed at Re, in Vestfold. Eystein escaped the battle, but was killed by a farmer when he asked for grið. After that, Sverre Sigurdsson became head of the Birkebeiners. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
