## How to play

String all the beads on a single thread. Start at the bead marked 1 and tap a neighbouring bead, above, below, left or right, to extend the thread. Pass through every square once and finish at the final bead. Tap the last bead of the thread to step back, or an earlier bead to go back to it. Time is limited. There are 3 necklaces, each larger than the last.

## The story behind it

The Hoen hoard was found in 1834, in Buskerud, Norway, with more than a hundred beads; today it is in Oslo. Stringing beads on a thread is invented. Read the story in [The Hoen treasure: gold, Arab coins and beads found in Buskerud](/en/2027/02/14/tesouro-de-hoen-ouro-moedas-e-contas-da-era-viking/).
