## How to play

By tradition, Birger Jarl founded Stockholm on an island in Mälaren around 1250. Here you stack the logs with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the island. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

By tradition, Birger Jarl founded Stockholm around 1250, and the first known mention of the city is two letters of 1252. The stacked logs in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
