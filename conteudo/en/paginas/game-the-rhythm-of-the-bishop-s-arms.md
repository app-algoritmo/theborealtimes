## How to play

In the legend, the Danes advance when Bishop Anders Sunesen raises his arms and fall back when he lowers them. Here you raise his arms in time with the battle. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to raise the bishop's arms in time with the battle. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the legend, at the battle of Lindanise, in 1219, a red flag with a white cross fell from the sky while Bishop Anders Sunesen prayed with raised arms. The oldest texts that tell it are from the 16th century. The arms and the rhythm in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
