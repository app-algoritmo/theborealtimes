## How to play

Aim with your finger (or use the arrows) and tap above the launcher (or press Space) to throw a rune. Three or more of the same colour together vanish, and any left hanging loose fall too. Clear all the runes before the throws run out.

## The story behind it

The poem Völuspá is recited by a seeress before Odin. The runes thrown in the game are made up. Read the story in [Völuspá, the seeress's prophecy that tells Odin of the beginning and end of the world](/en/2026/11/22/voluspa-a-vidente-fala-a-odin/).
