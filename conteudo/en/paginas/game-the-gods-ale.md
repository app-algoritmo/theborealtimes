## How to play

At Ægir's feast the gods drink fast. Five horns stand on the table and slowly empty. When a horn is empty, it turns red: tap it, or use keys 1 to 5, to fill it. Filling a horn that still has ale spills everything and is a mistake. A horn left empty too long is also a mistake. Fill 12 empty horns before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the prose introduction to the Lokasenna, Ægir welcomes the gods to a feast where the ale serves itself and the servants Eldir and Fimafeng are praised. The horns that empty and the rhythm of the game are invention.
