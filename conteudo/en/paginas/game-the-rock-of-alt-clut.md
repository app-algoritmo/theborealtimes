## How to play

In 870, Amlaíb and Ímar besieged the rock of Dumbarton for four months. Here you defend the rock with the shield against every blow of the attackers. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 870, the Viking kings Amlaíb and Ímar besieged the fortress of Dumbarton for four months, until the well ran dry, according to the Annals of Ulster and other sources. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
