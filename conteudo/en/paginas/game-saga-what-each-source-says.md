## How to play

The sources say little of Sága: a stanza in Grímnismál, a line in the Prose Edda and a debate among scholars. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 10 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Grímnismál shows Odin and Sága drinking at Sökkvabekkr, the Prose Edda places her second among the ásynjur and scholars debate whether she is another name for Frigg. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Sága and Sökkvabekkr: the goddess who drinks with Odin from golden cups and the doubts about who she is](/saga-and-sokkvabekkr-the-goddess-who-drinks-with-odin/).
