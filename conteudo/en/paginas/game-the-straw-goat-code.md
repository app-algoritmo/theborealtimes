## How to play

There is a secret code of 4 bundles, in 5 colours that can repeat. Pick the colours and tap Submit. Each black peg says one colour is right in the right place; each white peg, that the colour exists but is elsewhere. Crack the code in 8 guesses and 180 seconds. (Keyboard: 1 to 5 pick, Backspace undoes, Enter submits.)

## The story behind it

The julbock, the Scandinavian Yule goat, has taken many forms, and the straw goat is common to this day. The code is invented. Read the story in [The Scandinavian Yule goat: from the last sheaf of grain to the great straw goat](/en/2027/01/03/julbock-bode-de-natal-palha-escandinavia/).
