## How to play

Harald Gille has to walk barefoot over nine ploughshares that heat up and cool down. Each ploughshare glows red when hot and turns dark when it cools. Tap the screen, or press Space, to step to the next ploughshare only when it is dark. Stepping on a hot ploughshare burns and is a mistake. Reach the 9 ploughshares before time runs out. Three burns end the game.

## The story behind it

In Heimskringla, Harald Gille proves he is the son of King Magnus Barefoot by walking over nine glowing ploughshares, in the presence of two bishops. The ploughshares that heat and cool in cycles are invention.
