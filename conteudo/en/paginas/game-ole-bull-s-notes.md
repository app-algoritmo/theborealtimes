## How to play

Ole Bull was a violinist whom Schumann compared to Paganini. Here you tap the golden notes and do not tap the snapped strings. Tap the golden notes as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the snapped strings: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the sources, Robert Schumann wrote that Ole Bull was on a level with Paganini for speed and clarity. The notes and the strings in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
