## How to play

In Andersen's tale, the Mosekonen keeps "poetry in bottles" and warns that the lygtemænd, the bog lights, are loose. Here you run through the bog in three lanes, pick up the bottles and dodge the lights. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the bottles of poetry and dodge the bog lights: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In "The will-o'-the-wisps are in town, says the Bog Woman", of 17 November 1865, Hans Christian Andersen has the Mosekonen keep "poetry in bottles" and warn that the lygtemænd are loose. The run and the bottles in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
