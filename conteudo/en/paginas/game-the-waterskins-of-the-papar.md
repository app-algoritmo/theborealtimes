## How to play

You have two waterskins of different capacities, with no measuring marks, a spring and a drain. Tap a skin and then the spring to fill it, the drain to empty it, or another skin to pour the water into it, until the other is full or the first is empty. Leave one of the skins with exactly the target amount. The number of actions and the time are limited. There are 3 rounds.

## The story behind it

According to Landnámabók, Irish monks lived in Iceland before the Norse and left books, bells and crosiers; archaeology has not confirmed their presence. Measuring water is invented.
