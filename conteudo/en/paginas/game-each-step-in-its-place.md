## How to play

In Laxdæla saga, Melkorka goes from the market in Norway to the farm in Iceland and then to Ireland. Take each moment of her life to the right place. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Laxdæla saga, written in the 13th century, Höskuldr buys Melkorka from a Rus' merchant and only learns that she can speak when he hears her with her son; Ólafr goes to Ireland around 956. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
