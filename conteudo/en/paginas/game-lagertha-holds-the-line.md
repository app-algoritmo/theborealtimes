## How to play

In Saxo, Lagertha arrives with 120 ships to fight beside Ragnar. Here you defend with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Saxo, Lagertha came with 120 ships to help Ragnar and, at the height of the fight, attacked the enemy from behind. Scholars regard this part of the Gesta Danorum as largely fictional. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
