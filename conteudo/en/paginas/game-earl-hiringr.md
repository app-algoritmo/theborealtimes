## How to play

In the Mírmanns saga, Cecilia disguises herself as Earl Hiringr, joins Mírman's enemies and defeats him in combat. Here you tap Mírman's knights and avoid the allied knights. Tap Mírman's knights as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the allied knights: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Mírmanns saga, an Icelandic chivalric saga probably of the fourteenth century, tells how Mírman kills his heathen father and is made a leper by his mother. Cecilia, daughter of the king of Sicily, heals him and marries him, but later he forgets her and marries the queen of France. Cecilia disguises herself as Earl Hiringr, joins his enemies and defeats him in combat. The two are reconciled, and Mírman becomes king of Sicily. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
