## How to play

In the great hall, a sword is thrust into the trunk of the Branstock. To pull it out, alternate your hands: tap the left side and then the right, or use the left and right arrow keys. Two taps in a row on the same side slip your hand and count as a mistake. If you stop for too long, the sword sinks back a little. Make 18 pulls before time runs out. Three slips end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Saga of the Völsungs, a one-eyed old man thrusts a sword into the trunk in the hall, and only Sigmund can pull it out. The alternating pulls are invented. Read the story in [Sigmund and the sword in the Barnstokkr, in the Völsunga saga](/sigmund-and-the-sword-in-the-barnstokkr-in-the-volsunga-saga/).
