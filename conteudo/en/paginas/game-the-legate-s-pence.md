## How to play

In 1152, Cardinal Nicholas Breakspear arrived in Scandinavia as legate and later became Pope Adrian IV. Here you move the legate's purse to catch the silver pence and avoid the arrows of war. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the legate's purse and catch the silver pence. Avoid the arrows of war: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Nicholas Breakspear, born in England around 1100, was sent as legate to Scandinavia in 1152. In Nidaros, the council raised the see to an archbishopric, which covered Norway, Iceland, Greenland, the Faroe Islands, Orkney and Shetland. In Sweden, he managed to introduce Peter's pence. He left the region in autumn 1154 and was elected pope on 4 December 1154, taking the name Adrian IV. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
