## How to play

In Kormáks saga, Kormák borrows Skofnung from Skeggi for the duel with Bersi, who has the sword Hviting. Here you defend with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Kormáks saga, Kormák borrowed Skofnung from Skeggi to face Bersi, ignored the instructions for its use and lost the duel. Here, instead, you win. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
