## How to play

Each character makes a statement about the others. A truth-teller always speaks truths, and a liar always speaks lies. Tap a row to mark the person: ? (unknown), ✓ (tells the truth) or ✗ (lies). When all are marked, tap Confirm. Each round has exactly one correct answer. Three wrong confirmations end the game. There are 5 rounds.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Loki guides the hand of the blind god Höðr, who shoots the mistletoe dart that kills Baldr. Working out who lies and who tells the truth at this table is invented. Read the story in [Höðr, the blind god: the mistletoe dart, the death of Baldr and the two versions of the story](/en/2027/01/31/hodr-deus-cego-balder-visco-loki/).
