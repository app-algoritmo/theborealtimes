## How to play

On 15 June 1184, in the Sognefjord, Sverre's Mariusud, with about thirty benches, beat Magnus Erlingsson's lashed fleet. Here you row in the right beat. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to row the Mariusud. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The battle of Fimreite was fought on 15 June 1184, in the Sognefjord. Magnus had about 26 ships, and Sverre about 14. Sverre's ship, the Mariusud, had about thirty benches and handled badly, but was useful in narrow fjords. Magnus lashed many ships together, and the Mariusud, lying across, trapped them. Magnus died in the battle, and Sverre reigned from 1184 to 1202. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
