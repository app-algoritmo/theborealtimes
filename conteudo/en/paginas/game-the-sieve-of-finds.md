## How to play

In a basement in Reykjavík, the excavation found the remains of a hall from the settlement age. The finds pass along the sieve. Tap the golden finds to keep them in the box. The grey stones are worth nothing: do not touch them. Tapping a stone is a mistake. Keep 14 finds before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Aðalstræti 16 in Reykjavík, the excavation of the settlement-age hall found a layer of ash dated to 871, give or take two years. The finds passing along the sieve and the rhythm of the game are invention.
