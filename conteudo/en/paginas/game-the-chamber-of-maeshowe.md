## How to play

There is a secret sequence of 4 runes, from 6 possible kinds (they may repeat). Tap a rune below to put it in the next slot and tap Try. Gold peg: right rune in the right place. Light peg: right rune in the wrong place. You have 7 tries.

## The story behind it

In Maeshowe, in the Orkney Islands, Vikings left runic inscriptions on the walls. The secret sequence is made up. Read the story in [Maeshowe: the five-thousand-year-old tomb where Norsemen wrote on the walls](/en/2026/12/06/maeshowe-runas-vikings-orcadas/).
