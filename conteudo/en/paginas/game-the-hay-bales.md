## How to play

Hay bales of different kinds are mixed on posts. Tap a post to lift its top bale and tap another to set it down. You can only set a bale on an empty post or on a bale of the same kind, and each post holds four bales. Leave each post with a single kind. The number of moves and the time are limited. There are 3 rounds.

## The story behind it

In Hœnsa-Þóris saga, the enriched merchant Hœnsa-Þórir refuses to sell hay to his neighbours one winter; they take it, and he burns them alive in a farmhouse. Stacking bales is invented. Read the story in [Hœnsa-Þóris saga: the merchant, the refused hay and a 13th-century law](/en/2027/02/07/haensa-thoris-saga-feno-vizinhos-lei/).
