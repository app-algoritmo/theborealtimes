## How to play

In the Sonatorrek, the sea opens a breach in the “fence of kin”. Here, the fence is raised again, piece by piece. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the fence. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the sources, in stanzas 5 to 12 of the Sonatorrek the sea opens a breach in the “fence of kin”, an image of Egill's grief for Böðvarr. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Sonatorrek, the irreparable loss of sons: the poem of mourning Egill composed so as not to starve](/sonatorrek-the-irreparable-loss-of-sons-the-poem/).
