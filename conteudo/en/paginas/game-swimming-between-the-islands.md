## How to play

Alternate your strokes: tap the left and the right side of the screen, one after the other (or use A and D, or the arrows). Two strokes in a row on the same side waste breath without moving you. When breath runs out you barely move. Cover 120 metres before time runs out.

## The story behind it

The Saga of the Faroe Islanders tells of a man who escaped by swimming. The route and the distances in the game are made up. Read the story in [The Faroese saga: Sigmundur, Tróndur and the escape by swimming between the islands](/en/2026/11/22/foroyinga-saga-sigmundur-nado/).
