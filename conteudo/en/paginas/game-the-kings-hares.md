## How to play

Osborn keeps the king's hares in the field. Now and then a hare turns orange and runs for the edge of the field. Tap it, or use keys 1 to 5, to call it back with the pipe. Tapping a hare that is resting scares the herd and is a mistake. A hare that reaches the edge of the field is also a mistake. Call back 12 hares before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Asbjørnsen's tale in Dasent's translation, Osborn keeps the king's hares with a magic pipe that scatters and gathers whatever he wishes. The hares that run for the edge of the field are invention. Read the story in [Osborn's pipe and the king's hares: a tale from the Asbjørnsen collection](/en/2027/03/21/a-flauta-de-osborn-e-as-lebres-do-rei-conto-da-colecao-de-asbjornsen/).
