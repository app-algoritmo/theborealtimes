## How to play

Each square is worth a number: that of its column, for the row sum, and that of its row, for the column sum. Tap a square to mark or unmark it. The number to the right of each row is the sum of the column numbers of the squares marked in it; the number below each column is the sum of the row numbers of the squares marked in it. Time is limited. There are 3 grids.

## The story behind it

In the Danelaw, in England, there are many hundreds of place-names of Scandinavian origin; "-by" means a farmstead and is the most common element, and "-thorpe" a secondary settlement. Marking squares by sums is invented. Read the story in [Norse place-names in England: what -by, -thorpe and -thwaite say about the Danelaw](/en/2027/02/21/toponimos-nordicos-na-inglaterra-by-thorpe-e-o-danelaw/).
