## How to play

Hrafn Sveinbjarnarson was an Icelandic chieftain and physician who travelled as far as Santiago de Compostela. Take each fact of his life to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Hrafn Sveinbjarnarson, chieftain of the Westfjords, died in 1213 after a conflict with Þorvaldr Snorrason; Hrafns saga Sveinbjarnarsonar shows him as a physician and as a pilgrim who went to Santiago de Compostela. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
