## How to play

Oddi Helgason, "Star-Oddi", watched the sky and slept in Flatey before having the dream of the Stjörnu-Odda draumr. Here you move Oddi's basket to catch the stars and avoid the clouds. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Oddi's basket and catch the stars. Avoid the clouds: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Stjörnu-Odda draumr, an Icelandic tale, Oddi Helgason, a twelfth-century sky observer, sleeps in Flatey and dreams of a saga of kings of Gautland, in which he enters as the poet Dagfinnr. The text reached us in a copy of 1686 by Árni Magnússon. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
