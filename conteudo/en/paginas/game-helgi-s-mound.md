## How to play

In Helgakviða Hundingsbana II, Helgi is buried in a mound, and Sigrún goes there to visit him. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the mound. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Helgakviða Hundingsbana II, Helgi is buried in a mound and Sigrún goes there: she finds him covered with frost and spends the night with him, before he returns to Valhalla. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
