## How to play

According to folklore, the spirit of the church is an animal buried alive in the foundation. Here you stack the foundation stones with precision, under the lamb's gaze. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the foundation of the church. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Scandinavian folklore, the kirkegrim is the spirit of an animal buried alive in the foundations of a church, so that the building would stand firm. The stacked stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
