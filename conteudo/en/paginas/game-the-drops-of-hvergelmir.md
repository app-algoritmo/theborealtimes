## How to play

In the Grímnismál, drops fall from the antlers of the stag Eikþyrnir into the spring Hvergelmir, from which all the rivers flow. Here you move the spring to catch the drops and avoid the dry twigs. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the spring Hvergelmir and catch the drops. Avoid the dry twigs: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Grímnismál, of the Poetic Edda, the stag Eikþyrnir stands above Valhalla and bites the branches of the tree Læraðr; drops fall from his antlers into the spring Hvergelmir, from which the rivers flow. The drops and the twigs in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
