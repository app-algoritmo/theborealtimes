## How to play

In the Styrbjarnar þáttr Svíakappa, Þorgnýr has oxen and horses with brushwood tied to them driven against Styrbjörn's army. Here you run across the plain of Fyrisvellir in three lanes, gather the banners and dodge the herd. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the banners and dodge the herd with brushwood: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Styrbjarnar þáttr Svíakappa, an Icelandic tale, Styrbjörn, nephew of King Eiríkr of Sweden, returns from Jómsborg with a fleet, burns the ships at Fyrisvellir and loses the battle: Þorgnýr has oxen and horses with brushwood tied to them driven against the army, and a reed spear, thrown on the advice of a hooded man, brings a cloud and a landslide. Styrbjörn orders the banners planted and falls with all his troop. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
