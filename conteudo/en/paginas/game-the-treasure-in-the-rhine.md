## How to play

In the poem, Hagen takes Siegfried's hoard from Kriemhild. Here you catch the coins of the treasure in the chest and avoid the logs that the current drags along. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the chest and catch the coins of the treasure. Avoid the logs of the current: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Nibelungenlied, composed around 1200, Hagen takes Siegfried's hoard and later refuses to reveal where it is. The coins and the logs in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [The Nibelungenlied: the German poem of Siegfried, Kriemhild and Hagen, a medieval cousin of the Völsunga saga](/the-nibelungenlied-the-german-poem-of-siegfried-kriemhild/).
