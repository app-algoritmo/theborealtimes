## How to play

On 22 October 1253, the wedding of Gissur Þorvaldsson's son ended in a fire at Flugumýri. Take each fact to the right moment of the story. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

On 22 October 1253, at Flugumýri, Eyjólfr ofsi attacked with about 40 men and burned Gissur Þorvaldsson's house; according to the accounts, about 25 people died, and Gissur survived hidden in a barrel of whey. The account comes from the Íslendinga saga, by Sturla Þórðarson. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Flugumýri, 22 October 1253: the wedding that ended in a fire, the barrel of sour whey where Gissur hid and the saga written by the bride's father](/flugumyri-22-october-1253-the-wedding-that-ended-in-a-fire/).
