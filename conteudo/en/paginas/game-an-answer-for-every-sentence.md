## How to play

The princess answers everything and always wants the last word. The youngest brother gathered things along the way. A sentence from the princess appears at the top of the screen. Tap the object that answers it, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right: the wedge answers loose, the pot shard answers fat and the horn answers crooked. After each sentence, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Answer 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the youngest brother answers the princess's sentences with what he found on the way: the wedge, the pot shard and the goat horn. The order of the sentences and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [The princess who could not be silenced: the Asbjørnsen and Moe tale in which the youngest wins with six finds](/en/2027/04/04/a-princesa-que-ninguem-conseguia-calar-o-conto-de-asbjornsen-e-moe-em-que-o-cacula-vence-com-seis-achados/).
