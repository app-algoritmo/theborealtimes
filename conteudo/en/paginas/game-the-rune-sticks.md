## How to play

On the wharf at Bergen, merchants bring sticks with runes. Read the text of the stick, at the top of the screen, and tap the right box: Owner, for name tags, Message, for messages, or Prayer, for religious texts. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3. A wrong box is a mistake, and a stick left unsorted when the round time ends is also a mistake. Sort 12 sticks before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Among the runic inscriptions of Bryggen, in Bergen, there are tags such as "Eysteinn á mik", messages such as "Ást min, kyss mik" and Latin texts such as "Ave Maria". Some phrases in the game are inspired by them, and the sorting into boxes is invention. Read the story in [Bryggen: the rune sticks of Bergen, merchants' notes and love charms](/en/2027/03/21/bryggen-os-paus-de-runas-de-bergen-bilhetes-de-comerciantes-e-juras-de-amor/).
