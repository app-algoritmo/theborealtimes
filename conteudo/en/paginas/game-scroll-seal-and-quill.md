## How to play

The treaty of 911 between the Rus and Byzantium has come down to us in the Primary Chronicle. Watch the order in which the scroll, the seal and the quill rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the objects in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong object is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Primary Chronicle keeps a treaty of 911 between the Rus and Byzantium. The scroll, the seal, the quill, the order in which they glow and the swaps of place are invention.
