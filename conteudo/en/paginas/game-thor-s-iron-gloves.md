## How to play

According to the Gylfaginning, Thor needs the iron gloves Járngreipr to hold the handle of the hammer. Here they catch the hammer. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the iron gloves and catch the hammers. Avoid the loose stones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Gylfaginning, Thor's main possessions are the hammer, the belt Megingjörð, which makes his divine strength grow, and the iron gloves Járngreipr, which he needs to hold the handle. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
