## How to play

Tap a vat and then another to pour the mead on top. You may only pour into an empty vat or onto the same colour, and only if there is room. Leave every vat full with a single colour. There is a limit on moves and time.

## The story behind it

In the saga of the Ynglings, King Fróði dies in a vat of mead. The mix of colours in the game is made up. Read the story in [The Ynglinga saga: the king who drowned in a vat of mead and the kings of Uppsala](/en/2026/11/21/ynglinga-fjolnir-tonel-de-hidromel/).
