## How to play

A stack of silver coins of different sizes is out of order. Tap a coin to flip upside down every coin from the top of the stack down to it. Put the stack in order, with the smallest on top and the largest at the bottom. The number of flips and the time are limited. There are 3 stacks, each taller than the last.

## The story behind it

In Orkney folklore, the Finfolk are sea sorcerers who abduct people; a silver coin is said to be a way to escape them. Flipping stacks of coins is invented. Read the story in [Finfolk: the sea sorcerers of Orkney, the silver coin and the chalk cross](/en/2027/02/14/finfolk-feiticeiros-do-mar-das-orcades-moedas-de-prata/).
