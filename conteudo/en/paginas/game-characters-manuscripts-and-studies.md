## How to play

Ljósvetninga saga has two redactions and many open questions. Sort who appears, where the text is and what scholars have done. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Ljósvetninga saga, of the 13th century, has reached us in two redactions, A and C, and sets Guðmundr inn ríki against the Ljósvetningar. The box for each phrase in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
