## How to play

The Þorsteins saga Víkingssonar speaks of the sword Angrvaðill, of a battle on a frozen lake and of the fight against the viking Ötunfaxi. Take each fact to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Þorsteins saga Víkingssonar, a late Icelandic legendary saga, Víkingr kills Hárekr with the sword Angrvaðill, and his son Þorsteinn survives the battle on a lake frozen by a storm and later kills the viking Ötunfaxi; the saga ends with the birth of Friðþjófr. The exact date is uncertain. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
