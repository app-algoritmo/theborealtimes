## How to play

Each move joins two neighbouring dots with a thread. Whoever closes the fourth side of a mesh keeps it and plays again. Tap a thread (or use the arrows and Space). Close at least 9 of the 16 meshes before Rán. Time is limited.

## The story behind it

In the sources, Rán owns a net with which she catches the drowned. The game of closing meshes is made up. Read the story in [Rán, lady of the Norse sea, and the net that caught the drowned](/ran-lady-of-the-norse-sea-and-the-net-that-caught/).
