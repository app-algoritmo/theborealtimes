## How to play

A Hanseatic fleet hunted the Victual Brothers at sea. Here you block each blow of the boarding with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Klaus Störtebeker, leader of the Victual Brothers, was supposedly executed in Hamburg in 1401 (records suggest perhaps 1400). The blows of the boarding in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
