## How to play

In the Ívens saga, whoever pours water from the enchanted fountain onto the stone causes a storm, and an armed knight appears to defend the place. Here you block with the shield every blow of the knight of the fountain. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Ívens saga, the Norse version of Chrétien de Troyes's Yvain linked to the circle of King Hákon Hákonarson, follows almost the whole plot of the romance but clothes it in dense alliteration. Íven defeats the knight of the enchanted fountain, marries the widow, forgets the one-year deadline to return, goes mad and later saves a lion that accompanies him. Date and authorship remain disputed. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
