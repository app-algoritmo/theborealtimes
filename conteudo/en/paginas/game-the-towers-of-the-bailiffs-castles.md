## How to play

In Engelbrekt's revolt, the rebels destroyed the bailiffs' castles. Here you pop the castle towers and spare the miners' houses. Tap the towers of the bailiffs' castles as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the miners' houses: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Engelbrekt Engelbrektsson, owner of a mine in Bergslagen, led the Swedish revolt of 1434 against King Erik of Pomerania, and Wikipedia says the rebels destroyed bailiffs' castles. He died on 4 May 1436. The towers and houses in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
