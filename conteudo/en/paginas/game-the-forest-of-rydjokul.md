## How to play

On 2 February 1167, Erling Skakke was ambushed at Rydjokul, in Sørum, and escaped wounded. Here you run through the forest in three lanes, gather the loyal men and flee the ambushers. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the loyal men and dodge the ambushers: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Erling Ormsson, nicknamed Skakke, "the Crooked", was born around 1115 and married Kristin, daughter of Sigurd the Crusader. Between 1152 and 1155 he went to the Mediterranean with Rögnvald Kali, and a sword blow in Sicily left his neck crooked. He ruled as earl during the childhood of his son Magnus, crowned in 1163. He escaped the ambush at Rydjokul in 1167 and fell at Kalvskinnet, outside Nidaros, in 1179. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
