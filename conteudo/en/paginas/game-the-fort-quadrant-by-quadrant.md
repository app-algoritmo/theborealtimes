## How to play

Aggersborg had 48 longhouses, twelve in each quadrant. Four quadrants stand inside the ring of the fort, and timber piles up in one at a time. Tap the quadrant to raise a house when its bar passes the mark, or use the arrow keys. Tapping a quadrant with no timber, or raising too early, is a mistake. If the timber passes the full bar, it rots and is lost, and that is also a mistake. Raise 12 houses before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Aggersborg, from around 980, has an inner diameter of 240 metres, four streets in a cross and 48 longhouses, twelve per quadrant. The quadrants, the timber that piles up and the rhythm of the game are invention.
