## How to play

In the Tristrams saga ok Ísöndar, Tristram, banished from Cornwall, marries Ísodd of Brittany and builds a shrine with a statue of Ísönd. Here you stack the blocks of the shrine with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the base of the shrine. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Tristrams saga ok Ísöndar, the Norwegian translation of Thomas of Britain's Tristan attributed to Brother Robert and dated 1226, Tristram, banished from Cornwall, marries Ísodd of Brittany and builds a shrine with a statue of Ísönd. Wounded, he sends for Ísönd, but Ísodd lies about the colour of the sails. Tristram dies, Ísönd dies beside him, and the two are buried in the same churchyard. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
