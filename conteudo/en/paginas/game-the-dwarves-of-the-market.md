## How to play

At Ribe, a bone amulet with runes asked for protection against a dwarf. Dwarves in green hats and merchants in blue cloaks walk toward the market stalls. Tap the dwarves to stop them before they reach the stalls. Do not tap the merchants, who are peaceful people. A dwarf that reaches a stall is a mistake, and so is tapping a merchant. Stop 14 dwarves before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Ribe skull, of about 725, bears runes that seem to invoke Odin and protect against a dwarf. The dwarves that invade the market and the rhythm of the game are invention.
