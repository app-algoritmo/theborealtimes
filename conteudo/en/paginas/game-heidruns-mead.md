## How to play

Mead flows endlessly from the udder of the goat Heiðrún. You have two unmarked vessels, with only their capacity written on them. Use +A and +B to fill, −A and −B to empty, and A→B or B→A to pour one into the other, until the source is empty or the target is full. Leave one of the vessels with exactly the requested measure. There are 5 measures, each with a move limit. (Keyboard: keys 1 to 6.)

## The story behind it

In the Grímnismál and the Gylfaginning, the goat Heiðrún stands on Valhalla, eats the leaves of the tree Læraðr, and every day mead runs from her, enough to make all the einherjar fully drunk. The vessels and the measures are invented. Read the story in [Heiðrún, the goat of Valhalla whose mead serves all the einherjar](/en/2027/01/24/heidrun-cabra-valhalla-hidromel-laeradr/).
