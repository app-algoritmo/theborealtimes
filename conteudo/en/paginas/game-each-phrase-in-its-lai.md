## How to play

The Strengleikar gather Breton lais rendered into Norse at King Hákon's court. Here you take each phrase to the lai it comes from: Bisclaretz ljóð, Januals ljóð or Geitarlauf. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Strengleikar, a thirteenth-century Norwegian collection made for King Hákon Hákonarson, contain twenty-one lais in Norse prose, plus a prologue. The main manuscript is De la Gardie 4-7, in Uppsala, of 1250 to 1270. The translation follows the French closely, with the exception of Milun, which was abridged. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
