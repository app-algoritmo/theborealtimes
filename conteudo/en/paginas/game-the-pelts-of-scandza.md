## How to play

Jordanes says the Suehans supplied the Roman market with black fox pelts. Here you run through the snow in three lanes, gather the pelts and dodge the blocks of ice. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the black fox pelts and dodge the blocks of ice: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Getica, of about 551, Jordanes says the Suehans of Scandza supplied the Roman market with black fox pelts. The run, the pelts and the ice in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Jordanes and the island of Scandza: the northern "workshop of peoples", the forty-day sun and the black-fox pelts of the Suehans](/jordanes-and-the-island-of-scandza-the-northern-workshop/).
