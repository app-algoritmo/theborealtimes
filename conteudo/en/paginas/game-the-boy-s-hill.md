## How to play

With the blue belt on his waist, the boy feels strong enough to lift the whole hill. Here, he stacks stones. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the hill. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Dasent's translation, the boy ties the blue belt around his waist and suddenly feels strong enough to lift the whole hill. The stack of stones in the game is invention. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [The Blue Belt: the Norwegian tale of the poor boy who found a belt, beat a troll and danced as a bear to find the princess](/the-blue-belt-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-poor-boy-who-found/).
