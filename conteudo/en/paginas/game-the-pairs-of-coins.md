## How to play

In 1957, a Norwegian silver penny was reported found at an Indigenous site in Maine. Sixteen coins appear open for a few seconds and then turn over. Tap two coins to open them: if they match, the pair stays open. If they differ, both close again and that is a mistake. Find the 8 pairs before time runs out. Nine mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The penny from the Goddard site in Maine is a Norwegian silver penny from 1065 to 1080, from the time of Olaf Kyrre. Whether it arrived by voyage, by trade or by fraud is disputed. The pairs and coins of the game are invention. Read the story in [The Maine penny: an eleventh-century Norwegian penny found at a Native American site in the United States](/the-maine-penny-an-eleventh-century-norwegian-penny-found/).
