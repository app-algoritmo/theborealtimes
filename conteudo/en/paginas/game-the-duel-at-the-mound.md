## How to play

Þorsteinn and Bjarni fight beside a mound, and the shields are cut away until nothing is left. Here you strike in time with the duel. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to cut away the rival's shield. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Þorsteins þáttr stangarhöggs, Þorsteinn and Bjarni fight at a mound until the shields are cut away, and Bjarni, without delivering the blow, accepts Þorsteinn as a loyal man. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
