## How to play

At Þorrablót, preserved food goes to the table on wooden trays, the bakkar. Here you move the tray and catch the food that falls. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the wooden tray and catch the pieces of cured food. Avoid the icicles: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Þorrablót is an Icelandic midwinter dinner, in its modern form since the 19th century, with preserved food served on wooden trays. The tray, the food and the ice in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
