## How to play

In the Illuga saga Gríðarfóstra, Illugi crosses the fjord to fetch fire from the cave of the troll Gríðr, who threatens him with her knife. Here you move the bucket to catch the embers and avoid the knives. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Illugi's bucket and catch the embers. Avoid Gríðr's knives: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Illuga saga Gríðarfóstra, a short Icelandic legendary saga, Illugi goes to fetch fire for the crew, faces the troll Gríðr without fearing her knife, lifts the curse that bound her and returns with gold and fire. The motif of the bewitched woman freed by someone without fear also appears in Scandinavian ballads. Names and numbers belong to folktale tradition. The embers and the knives in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
