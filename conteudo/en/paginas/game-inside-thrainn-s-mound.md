## How to play

In the Hrómundar saga Gripssonar, the hero enters the mound of King Þráinn and comes out with a ring, a necklace and a sword. Here you run through the mound in three lanes, pick up the gold rings and dodge the bones. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the gold rings and dodge the bones: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Hrómundar saga Gripssonar, an Icelandic legendary saga, Hrómundr enters Þráinn's mound, faces the revenant and takes the sword Mistilteinn, a ring and a necklace. The prose version we know is late, but the same story was already being told in 1119, according to the Þorgils saga ok Hafliða. The run and the rings in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
