## How to play

On the Björketorp stone, in Blekinge, a runic inscription threatens whoever breaks it. Here you tap the runes and do not tap the pickaxes. Tap the runes as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the pickaxes: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Björketorp stone, in Blekinge, Sweden, has a runic inscription that threatens whoever breaks the monument. The runes and the pickaxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
