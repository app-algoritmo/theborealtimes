## How to play

Three brothers quarrel on a moor over a hat, a cloak and a pair of boots. Watch the order in which the three objects rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the objects in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong object is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, whoever holds the hat, the cloak and the boots becomes invisible and can wish himself anywhere. The king offers to judge the brothers' quarrel, takes the three items and wishes himself away. The order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention.
