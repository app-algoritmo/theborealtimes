## How to play

According to the Orkneyinga saga, Sigurd challenged Máel Brigte to a battle of forty men on each side. Here you block each blow with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Orkneyinga saga, Earl Sigurd Eysteinsson, the Mighty, defeated Máel Brigte and hung the head from his saddle, and a tooth scratched him and wounded him fatally. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Sigurd the Mighty, Earl of Orkney: the head hung from the saddle and the tooth that killed him](/sigurd-the-mighty-earl-of-orkney-the-head-hung/).
