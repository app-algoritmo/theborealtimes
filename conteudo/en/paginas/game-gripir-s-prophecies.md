## How to play

Grípir foretells Sigurd's life, and Grímhildr makes him forget with a drink. Here the prophecies appear as rune stones, among the horns of the drink. Tap the rune stones of the prophecies as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the horns of the drink of forgetting: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Grípisspá, a poem of the Poetic Edda, Uncle Grípir foretells to Sigurd the dragon, Brynhildr, the forgetting through Grímhildr and his death. The stones and horns in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
