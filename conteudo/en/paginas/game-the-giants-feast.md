## How to play

In the Egils saga einhenda, Egill and Ásmundr enter the giants' wedding in disguise. Here you block with the shield every blow from the giants. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Egils saga einhenda ok Ásmundar berserkjabana, an Icelandic legendary saga, Egill the One-Handed and Ásmundr rescue two princesses carried off by giants in Jötunheimr; the saga says some ninety giants die. The saga has no known historical basis. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
