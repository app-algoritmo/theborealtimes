## How to play

You play the light pieces, which go up. Tap a piece and then the dark square to go to: pieces step one square diagonally forward. To capture, jump over a rival piece to the empty square right behind it; capturing is compulsory. A piece that reaches the last row becomes a queen and also moves backwards. Leave the rival without pieces or without moves, within 240 seconds. (Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space picks.)

## The story behind it

Ghostly riders, hounds and storm make up the Wild Hunt, called Åsgårdsreia or Oskoreia in the North. The capture board is invented. Read the story in [Åsgårdsreia: the Wild Hunt that Norway heard passing in midwinter](/en/2026/12/27/asgardsreia-cacada-selvagem-oskoreia/).
