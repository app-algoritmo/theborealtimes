## How to play

The wall grows on its own every second. Tap the hearts that appear on the screen to distract the horse Svaðilfari: each heart stops the work for a few seconds. Hold out for 60 days, the game’s seconds, until summer arrives with the wall unfinished. If it reaches 100%, the gods lose Freyja, the Sun and the Moon.

## The story behind it

In Snorri Sturluson’s *Prose Edda*, the builder had one winter to finish the fortress, and Loki, disguised as a mare, lured his horse away. The game simplifies the account. Read the story in [How Loki tricked the builder of Asgard's wall and gave birth to the horse Sleipnir](/en/2026/11/13/sleipnir-muro-asgard/).
