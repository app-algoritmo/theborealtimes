## How to play

A marker runs from side to side along the bar. Tap (or press Space) when it is inside the golden strip: the lash lands and a skin of the worm falls off. The strip narrows and the marker speeds up. Remove all 10 skins. Three mistakes and it is over.

## The story behind it

In the Danish tale Kong Lindorm, the bride has to face the worm king and free him from his skin. The lashes in the game are made up. Read the story in [Kong Lindorm: the Danish tale of the serpent prince and the bride of shirts](/en/2026/11/22/kong-lindorm-conto-dinamarques/).
