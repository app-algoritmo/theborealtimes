## How to play

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, Peter, the cat and the troll do very different things. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “Peter”, the second is “The cat” and the third is “The troll”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Dasent's translation, in “Lord Peter” the cat invents everything, Peter does what she tells him, and the troll who owns the silver castle bursts at sunrise. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention. Read the story in [Lord Peter: the Norwegian tale of the cat who made the youngest son pass for a lord and tricked a troll with a story about rye](/lord-peter-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-cat-who-made/).
