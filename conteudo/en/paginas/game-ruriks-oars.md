## How to play

Rurik and his men row upriver. Tap (or use the arrows) on the left and on the right, alternating, to take strokes: each alternate tap gives full power. Repeating the same side gives less power but turns the boat toward it. The current pushes the boat; the third bump on the bank ends the game. Reach the end of the river before time runs out.

## The story behind it

The Primary Chronicle says Rurik arrived at Ladoga and Novgorod around 862. The strokes in the game are made up. Read the story in [Rurik and Oleg: how the Primary Chronicle tells of the call to the Varangians that gave rise to Kyivan Rus', and what archaeology adds](/en/2026/11/19/rurik-oleg-rus-cronica-primaria/).
