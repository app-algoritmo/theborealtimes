## How to play

In the sagas, Þorgerðr appears with a large gold ring on her arm, and at Hjörungavágr she brings hail down on Hákon's enemies. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the tray and catch the gold rings. Avoid the hailstones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Njáls saga, Hákon's temple held a seated image of Þorgerðr with a large gold ring, and in the Jómsvíkinga saga the hailstones weighed an ounce each. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Þorgerðr Hölgabrúðr: the goddess of the jarls of Hlaðir, her sister Irpa and the gold ring](/thorgerdr-holgabrudr-the-goddess-of-the-jarls-of-hladir/).
