## How to play

In Saxo, Alfhild commands a fleet of longships crewed by young pirates, who raid the Baltic coast. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the silver bars and dodge the reefs: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Saxo, Alfhild fled dressed as a man and commanded a fleet of longships crewed by young pirates, who raided the Baltic coast. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
