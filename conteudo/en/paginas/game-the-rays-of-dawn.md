## How to play

In the Helgakviða Hjörvarðssonar, Atli keeps Hrímgerðr talking until the sun rises. Here you tap the rays of sun and do not tap the dark clouds. Tap the rays of sun as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the dark clouds: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Helgakviða Hjörvarðssonar, a poem of the Poetic Edda, the sailor Atli trades insults with the giantess Hrímgerðr until the sun rises, and she turns to stone. The rays and the clouds in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
