## How to play

In the Gylfaginning, Frigg sends Gná to other worlds, and she rides Hófvarpnir through the air and over the sea. Here you move the horse to catch the messages and avoid the storm clouds. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Gná's horse and catch the messages. Avoid the storm clouds: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Gylfaginning, Snorri tells that Frigg sends Gná to other worlds and that she rides Hófvarpnir, a horse that runs through the air and over the sea. The messages and the clouds in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Gná and Hófvarpnir: Frigg's messenger, the horse that runs through the air and over the sea and the question of the Vanir that nobody can explain](/gna-and-hofvarpnir-frigg-s-messenger-the-horse-that-runs/).
