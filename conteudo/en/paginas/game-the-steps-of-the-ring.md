## How to play

In the Faroese chain dance, people hand in hand mark the rhythm of the ballad with their feet. Here you stamp the step to the beat of the ring. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to dance with the ring. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the chain dance of the Faroe Islands, people sing the kvæði holding hands, without instruments, and the leader is called the skipari. The ring and the rhythm in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
