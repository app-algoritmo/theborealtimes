## How to play

Þorgils saga ok Hafliða speaks of the law written down in 1117–1118, of a wedding in 1119 and of the quarrel between two chieftains. Take each fact to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Þorgils saga ok Hafliða, part of the Sturlunga saga, tells the dispute between Hafliði Másson and Þorgils Oddason between 1117 and 1121 and describes the wedding at Reykjahólar, in 1119, where sagas were told. The date of the saga itself is debated. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
