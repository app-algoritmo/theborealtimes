## How to play

The Eggja stone has some 200 runes, the longest inscription in Elder Futhark. Here you scratch them in rhythm. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to scratch a rune on the stone. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Eggja stone was ploughed up in 1917 in Norway and has some 200 runes, the longest known inscription in Elder Futhark. A horse is carved on it, and the translation of the text remains disputed. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
