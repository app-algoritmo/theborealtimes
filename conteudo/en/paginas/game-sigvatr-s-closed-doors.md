## How to play

In Austrfararvísur, Sigvatr travels east and finds doors barred at several farms. Here you follow the road. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the poet's verses, on parchment and dodge the closed doors: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Austrfararvísur, Sigvatr was turned away at Hof and at two other farms during the journey to Sweden around 1019. The road and the parchments in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Austrfararvísur: Sigvatr and the closed doors on the journey to Sweden](/austrfararvisur-sigvatr-and-the-closed-doors-on-the-journey/).
