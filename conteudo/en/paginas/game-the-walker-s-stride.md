## How to play

In the Göngu-Hrólfs saga, the hero too heavy for any horse always goes on foot. Here you mark the rhythm of Hrólfr's stride along the roads of Garðaríki, one step at a time. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to walk on foot along the roads of Garðaríki. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Göngu-Hrólfs saga, an Icelandic legendary saga, Hrólfr, son of Sturlaugr, is so heavy that no horse can carry him and he always goes on foot; he is betrayed by Vilhjálmr, who cuts off his feet, is healed by a dwarf and ends up married to Ingigerðr, in Russia. The explanation of the nickname "Göngu" is a reading; the saga does not say how Hrólfr died. The rhythm of the stride in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
