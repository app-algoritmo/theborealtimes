## How to play

The Sheep Letter of 1298 regulates sheep, land, people, the sea and the power of the king. Take each rule to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Seyðabrævið, of 28 June 1298, is the oldest surviving document of the Faroe Islands and deals mainly with sheep, but also with land, whales and driftwood. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
