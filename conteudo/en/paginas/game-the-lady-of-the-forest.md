## How to play

The skogsrå, the lady of the forest, tests your knowledge. Eight questions about beings of Nordic folklore, each with four answers and twenty seconds. Tap the answer (or use the keys 1 to 4). A wrong answer, or running out of time, counts as a mistake; the third mistake ends the game.

## The story behind it

The skogsrå appears in Swedish folklore. The questions and answers summarise popular traditions. Read the story in [Skogsrå: the lady of the Swedish forest, who gives luck to hunters and leads charcoal burners into the woods](/en/2026/11/19/skogsra-senhora-da-floresta-suecia/).
