## How to play

In the hólmganga, each fighter generally used three shields, and the opponent's blows could break a shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the sources, in the hólmganga each fighter could use a fixed number of shields, generally three, the challenged man struck first, and the fight normally ended at first blood. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
