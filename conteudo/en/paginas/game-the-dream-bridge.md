## How to play

A bridge has four stones in each row, but only one stone per row is safe. The safe stones light up one by one, from the bottom up, showing the way. Memorise the path. When it goes dark, tap the stones in the same order, from the bottom up. Each wrong tap counts as a mistake, and three mistakes end the game. Each round the bridge grows longer. There are 3 rounds.

## The story behind it

In the Norwegian ballad Draumkvedet, Olav Åsteson sleeps from Christmas Eve until the thirteenth day and, on waking, tells of his dreams, among them a very tall bridge. Memorising the path is invented. Read the story in [Draumkvedet: Olav Åsteson's dream and the medieval ballad of Norway](/en/2027/02/07/draumkvedet-olav-asteson-sonho-balada/).
