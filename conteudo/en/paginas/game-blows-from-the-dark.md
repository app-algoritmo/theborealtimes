## How to play

In January 1135, Harald Gille entered Bergen and Magnus's army scattered. Here you hold the shield and parry the blows that come out of the dark. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 12 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Magnus Sigurdsson, son of Sigurd the Crusader, reigned with his uncle Harald Gille. In 1134 he beat his uncle at Fyrileiv, but dismissed his army and spent the winter in Bergen. Harald returned, entered the city on 7 January 1135 and captured his nephew. Magnus was blinded and mutilated, and lived as a monk at Munkholmen. He came back as co-king in 1136 and fell in the naval battle of Holmengrå, on 12 November 1139. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
