## How to play

On St. John's Eve, bonfires were lit. According to Professor Herleik Baklid, fire was seen as protection against evil, and witches were believed to gather on the Brocken or on Lyderhorn. Witches in black hats and villagers in blue cloaks walk toward the bonfires. Tap the witches to drive them off before they reach the fire. Do not tap the villagers, who come to celebrate. A witch that reaches the fire is a mistake, and so is tapping a villager. Drive off 14 witches before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to ScienceNorway, fire was seen as protection against evil, and witches were believed to gather on the Brocken, in Germany, or on Lyderhorn, in Bergen. These are folk beliefs recorded later, not proven Viking Age rituals. The advancing witches and the rhythm of the game are invention.
