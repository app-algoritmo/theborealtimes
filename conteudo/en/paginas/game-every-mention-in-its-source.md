## How to play

Jörð is the Earth and the mother of Thor. A mention of her appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the kind of source that carries it, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the Prose Edda, the second is the Poetic Edda and the third is the poems of the skalds. A wrong box, or a mention left unanswered, is a mistake. Link 10 mentions before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Jörð is the daughter of Nótt and Annar (Gylfaginning, section 10, which varies by manuscript) and the mother of Thor (section 25). In Lokasenna, Völuspá and Hárbarðsljóð, Thor is linked to Jörð, Hlóðyn and Fjörgyn. The skalds Þjóðólfr of Hvinir and Eilífr Goðrúnarson call Thor the son of Iord. The row of mentions, the three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention.
