## How to play

Each island has a number: how many bridges must leave it. Tap one island and then another in the same row or column, with no other island between them, to build a bridge. Tap the same pair again for a double bridge, and once more to remove it. Bridges do not cross. All islands must end up linked in one group, with the right number of bridges. There are 3 maps, and time is limited.

## The story behind it

According to the saga, the poet Hallfreðr served Hákon Sigurðarson, Óláfr Tryggvason and Eiríkr Hákonarson, and told in verse of his difficult conversion to Christianity. Linking islands with bridges is invented.
