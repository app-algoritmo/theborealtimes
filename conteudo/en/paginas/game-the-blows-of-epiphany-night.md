## How to play

Somerled faced Godred Olafsson in a naval battle, on Epiphany night. Here you block each blow of the fight on the deck with the shield. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 1156 or 1157, Somerled faced Godred Olafsson in a naval battle that the chronicle dates to Epiphany night; he died in 1164, at the battle of Renfrew. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
