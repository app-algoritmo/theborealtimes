## How to play

On Christmas Eve, the dead deacon of Myrká returns, and Guðrún must escape him. Three doors are before you. Wait in silence. When a pale light appears before one door, tap that door to bar it before he arrives, or use keys 1, 2 and 3. Tapping before the light appears, tapping the wrong door or letting the deacon in is a mistake. Bar 10 times before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale, the deacon drowns in the Hörgá and returns on Christmas Eve to fetch Guðrún. She escapes because she had put on only one sleeve of her coat, and she rings the church bell. The doors, the pale light and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [The Deacon of Myrká: the Icelandic tale of the dead bridegroom who rides on Christmas Eve](/the-deacon-of-myrka-the-icelandic-tale-of-the-dead/).
