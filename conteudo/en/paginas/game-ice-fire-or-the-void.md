## How to play

Before the world there was Niflheim in the north, Múspell in the south and the Void between them. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “Niflheim”, the second is “Múspell” and the third is “The Void”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Gylfaginning, Niflheim lies in the north, with the spring Hvergelmir, and Múspell in the south, guarded by Surtr. Between them lies Ginnungagap, where the heat melts the frost and Ymir appears. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
