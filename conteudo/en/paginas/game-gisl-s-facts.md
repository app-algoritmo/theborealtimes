## How to play

The Gísls þáttr Illugasonar tells how a young Icelander avenges his father in Niðarós and escapes death before the king. Take each fact to the right stage of the tale. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Gísls þáttr Illugasonar, an Icelandic tale, Gísl, aged seventeen, comes to Norway to avenge his father and wounds Gjafvaldr in Niðarós. Imprisoned, he is saved by the bell of the ninth hour, by the Icelanders of the town, by Father Jón Ögmundarson and by a poem recited to the king, who spares him in exchange for service and sixteen marks of gold. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
