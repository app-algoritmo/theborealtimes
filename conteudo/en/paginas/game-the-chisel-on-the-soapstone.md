## How to play

The cathedral of Nidaros, seat of Archbishop Eystein Erlendsson, is built of soapstone, a soft rock. Here you keep the rhythm of the chisel on the cathedral blocks, in the right beat. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to carve the soapstone of Nidaros. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Eystein Erlendsson was archbishop of Nidaros from 1161 to 1188. He studied at Saint-Victor in Paris, and crowned the boy Magnus Erlingsson in 1163, after a new law of succession. He is credited with the Passio et Miracula beati Olavi. The archbishop's seat was the church of the Holy Trinity in Nidaros, over the grave of Saint Olaf, today built of soapstone; the oldest parts of the present building are the octagon and its ambulatory. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
