## How to play

Tap in the direction you want to walk, or use the arrow keys, to take a step. Reach the castle, in the opposite corner, before time runs out. There are three forests, each larger than the last, and the mazes change every game.

## The story behind it

Soria Moria is the castle in a tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe. The mazes in the game are random and made up. Read the story in [Soria Moria: the castle every Norwegian seeks without ever having seen it](/en/2026/11/15/soria-moria-castelo/).
