## How to play

Gunnhild, the Mother of Kings, appears in sources that contradict each other. A passage appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the source that holds it, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is Heimskringla, the second is Egil's saga and the third is the other sources. A wrong box, or a passage left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 passages before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, Heimskringla makes Gunnhild a daughter of Ozur Toti and ties her to two Finnish wizards. In Egil's saga, her brothers try to kill the poet, who raises a horse's head on a pole. Historia Norwegiae, Laxdæla saga and Jómsvíkinga saga bring different origin, love and end. The queue of passages, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
