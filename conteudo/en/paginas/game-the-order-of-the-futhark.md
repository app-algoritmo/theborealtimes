## How to play

A slab of limestone carries twelve runes. Look at the row of runes at the bottom of the screen and tap the stones in the same order. The first round has the first six runes of the futhark, f, u, þ, a, r, k; the second has the next six. A rune out of order is a mistake. Land all 12 runes before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Kylver Stone from Gotland, from about the year 400, carries the whole Elder Futhark in order, the oldest such sequence known. The game with twelve runes is invented. Read the story in [The Kylver Stone: the whole futhark carved on Gotland around 400](/en/2027/03/14/pedra-de-kylver-o-futhark-inteiro-gravado-em-gotland-por-volta-de-400/).
