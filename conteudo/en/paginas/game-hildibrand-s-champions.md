## How to play

In the Ásmundar saga kappabana, Ásmundr beats one of Hildibrand's champions after another. Here you block with the shield every blow from the champions. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Ásmundar saga kappabana, an Icelandic legendary saga, Ásmundr recovers a sword in Lake Loginn, beats Hildibrand's champions in duels and faces Hildibrand himself on the Rhine. The saga adapts the story of the Hildebrandslied, a poem in Old High German. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
