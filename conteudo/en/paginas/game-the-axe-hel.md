## How to play

Defend the battle line. Wendish warriors advance along three lanes. Tap the left side, the centre or the right side of the screen to strike with the axe Hel when a warrior gets close. A swing at nothing and a warrior who gets past are mistakes. Fell 14 warriors before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In 1043, at Lyrskov Heath, Magnus the Good defeated an army of Wends, and the sources speak of Saint Olaf's axe Hel. The strikes in a row are invented. Read the story in [Magnus the Good and the battle of Lyrskov Heath, in 1043, against the Wends](/en/2027/03/07/magnus-o-bom-e-a-batalha-de-lyrskov-hede-em-1043-contra-os-wendos/).
