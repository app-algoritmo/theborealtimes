## How to play

In the text Hversu Noregr byggðist, the giant Fornjótr has sons tied to sea, fire and wind, and his descendant Snær is the snow. Here you tap the snowflakes and do not tap the embers of Logi. Tap the snowflakes as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap Logi's embers: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Hversu Noregr byggðist, of the Flateyjarbók, Fornjótr has the sons Hlér (sea), Logi (fire) and Kári (winds); from Kári descend Jökull, Snær ("snow") and King Þorri, who gives his name to a winter month. No one knows whether there was a King Þorri. The flakes and the embers in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
