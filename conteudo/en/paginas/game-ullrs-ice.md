## How to play

Swipe (or use the arrow keys) to make the skier slide on the ice: it only stops when it hits a stone or the edge, and it stops by itself at the marker. Reach the marker within the move limit and 75 seconds. A tap on the board also gives the direction, starting from the skier.

## The story behind it

Ullr has a hall of yews and many place names, but almost no myth. The ice and the marker are invented. Read the story in [Ullr: the god of skis and the bow, of whom almost no stories remain](/en/2026/12/27/ullr-deus-dos-esquis-e-do-arco/).
