## How to play

At Lithend, Gunnar fights with his bow and his halberd, and the hound Sam was already killed before the attack. The request appears at the top of the screen. Tap the object asked for before the time for the request runs out: the bow, the halberd or the hound Sam. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. After each request, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a request left unanswered, is a mistake. Answer 10 requests before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Njáls saga (chapters 75 and 76), the hound Sam is killed with an axe, Gunnar pierces Thorgrim with the halberd and shoots arrows until Thorbrand cuts his bowstring. The order of the requests, the timing and the swaps of place are invention.
