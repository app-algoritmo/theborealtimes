## How to play

In 1361, the farmers of Gotland faced the army of Valdemar in the open field before Visby. Here you defend the field with the shield against every blow. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

On 27 July 1361, before Visby, King Valdemar IV of Denmark defeated the farmers of Gotland in the battle of Korsbetningen. Excavations from 1905 found mass graves outside the walls. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
