## How to play

On Frösön, the stone says that Ásbjörn built the bridge. Twelve planks form the crossing, and a marker runs around them without stopping. One of the planks glows. Tap the screen, or use Space, when the marker is over the plank that glows, to set it in place. Tapping off the plank is a mistake. Set 10 planks before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Frösö stone, from the eleventh century, says that Austmaðr Guðfastsson had it raised and had a bridge made, which Ásbjörn built. The twelve planks, the marker and the rhythm of the game are invention.
