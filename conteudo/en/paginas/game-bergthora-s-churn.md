## How to play

Bergþórshvoll, the farm of Njáls saga, means "Bergþóra's knoll". This game is a calm household task, without the burning. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to churn the butter. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Dasent's translation of Njáls saga, Bergþóra tells Flosi she promised Njáll, when young, to share the same fate. The game shows an invented household task and not the burning scene. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
