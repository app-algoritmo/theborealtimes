## How to play

According to the Landnámabók, the Irish slaves fled by boat to the islands in the south. Here you run along the beach in three lanes, pick up the water skins and dodge the lava rocks. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the water skins and dodge the lava rocks: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Landnámabók, written centuries after the settlement, Irish slaves killed Hjörleifr and fled to the islands in the south, where Ingólfr Arnarson caught up with them; that is why the islands are called Vestmannaeyjar, "islands of the men of the west". The run and the objects in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
