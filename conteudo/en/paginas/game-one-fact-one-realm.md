## How to play

Margaret ruled Denmark, was queen of Norway and won in Sweden. Take each fact to the right realm. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Margaret I was regent of Denmark, queen consort of Norway and defeated King Albert at Åsle, in Sweden, in 1389. Erik of Pomerania was crowned king of the three realms on 17 June 1397. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Margaret I of Denmark: the "Lady King" who won at Åsle in 1389 and built the Kalmar Union](/margaret-i-of-denmark-the-lady-king-who-won-at-asle-in-1389/).
