The Boreal Saga Play VR cinema runs right in your phone's browser, with no app to install, no sign-up and no cables. All you need is a phone headset, the kind where the phone slots in front of the lenses.

## What we tested

We tested the cinema with a **Shinecon** phone headset and an **iPhone 17 Pro** (Safari). The list of tested models will grow; if you use another one, tell us.

## What to look for in a phone headset

- **Adjustable lenses**, with control over lens distance and the distance between your eyes. This makes the biggest difference to comfort.
- **A holder that fits your phone.** Check the manufacturer's description for the screen sizes it accepts.
- **A comfortable strap and padding**, for sessions of a few minutes without pressure on the face.
- **Phone in landscape**, with the whole screen in view of the lenses.

You don't need a controller, an app, an account or a computer. You choose with your gaze.

## Step by step

1. Open the VR cinema on your phone, with the button above or the QR code on the [VR page](/vr/).
2. Tap "Headset mode (VR)" and allow motion access if the phone asks.
3. Turn the phone to landscape, slot it into the headset and look straight ahead.
4. Adjust the headset's lenses and, if needed, the cinema's "Lens adjustment" settings.

## Quick questions

**Does it work with Meta Quest or Apple Vision Pro?** We haven't tested it. The cinema was built for phones in phone headsets.

**Do I need an internet connection?** Yes, to open the cinema and load the stories.

**Do you sell headsets?** No. Any shop will do; use the criteria above to choose.

**Is it safe?** The cinema uses only the phone's motion sensor, never the camera or the microphone. As a precaution, we don't recommend it for young children and suggest short breaks.
