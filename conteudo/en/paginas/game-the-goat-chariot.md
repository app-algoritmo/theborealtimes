## How to play

Drive Thor's chariot along the rocky road. Tap the screen, or press Space, to jump the rocks. Do not jump under the beams: stay on the ground. Gather bones, the ones on the ground and the ones up high, without breaking any. Collect 16 bones before time runs out. Three crashes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Thor eats the goats at night and brings them back at dawn, and no bone may be broken. The chariot race is invented. Read the story in [Tanngrisnir and Tanngnjóstr: the goats that pull Thor's chariot](/en/2027/03/07/tanngrisnir-e-tanngnjostr-os-bodes-que-puxam-o-carro-de-thor/).
