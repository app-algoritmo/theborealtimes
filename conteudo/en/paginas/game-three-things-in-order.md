## How to play

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the boy wears the blue belt, sees a giant egg and lifts the trolls' giant sword. Watch the order in which the three objects rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the objects in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong object is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Dasent's translation, the boy finds a blue belt that gives him strength, the sailors split a giant egg and he lifts the trolls' giant sword. The shapes, the order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [The Blue Belt: the Norwegian tale of the poor boy who found a belt, beat a troll and danced as a bear to find the princess](/the-blue-belt-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-poor-boy-who-found/).
