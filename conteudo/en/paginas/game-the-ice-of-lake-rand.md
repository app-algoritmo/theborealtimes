## How to play

According to the Heimskringla, Halfdan the Black died when the ice of a lake gave way under his sleigh. Here you run across the ice in three lanes, collect the ears of grain and dodge the cracks. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the ears of grain and dodge the cracks in the ice: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Heimskringla, Snorri Sturluson tells that Halfdan the Black died at forty, when the ice of Lake Rand gave way in spring, and that his body was divided into four parts, since it was believed to bring good harvests. The run, the ears of grain and the cracks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
