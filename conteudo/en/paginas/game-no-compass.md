## How to play

Bjarni sails for Greenland with no map or compass, and the wind pushes the ship sideways. The arrow at the top shows where the wind blows. Tap the left side of the screen, or press the left arrow, to turn the helm left, and the right side, or the right arrow, to turn right. Keep the ship inside the green band. Leaving it is a mistake, and the ship returns to the centre. Sail 14 stretches before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Greenlanders' Saga, Bjarni missed his course to Greenland, with no map or compass, and sighted three lands without landing. The winds and the stretches of the game are invention. Read the story in [Bjarni Herjólfsson: the captain who saw lands in the west and did not land](/bjarni-herjolfsson-the-captain-who-saw-lands-in-the-west/).
