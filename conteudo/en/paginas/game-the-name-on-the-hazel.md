## How to play

In the Geitarlauf, Tristram cuts a hazel branch, carves his name on it and leaves it by the road along which the queen will pass. Here you mark the time of each letter of the name Tristram. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to carve the eight letters of Tristram. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 8 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Geitarlauf, from the Strengleikar and the Norwegian version of Marie de France's Chevrefoil, Tristram, banished from Cornwall, returns in secret, carves his name on a hazel branch and leaves it by the queen's road. The honeysuckle winding around the hazel is the image of the two: if they are parted, both die. The title means "goat's leaf," like the French chèvrefeuille. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
