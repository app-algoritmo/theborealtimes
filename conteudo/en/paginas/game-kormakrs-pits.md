## How to play

Six pits are yours, in the bottom row, and six are your rival's, in the top row; each pit starts with 4 seeds. Tap one of your pits to pick up its seeds and sow them one by one counter-clockwise (to the right along the bottom and to the left along the top), passing through your store, on the right, and never your rival's. If the last seed lands in your store, you play again. If it lands in an empty pit of yours, you take that seed and the seeds in the opposite pit. The game ends when a row is empty, and whoever has more seeds in the store wins. (Keyboard: keys 1 to 6.)

## The story behind it

The Kormáks saga tells how Kormákr, an Icelandic poet of the 10th century, loves Steingerðr; because of a curse he arrives late for the wedding and she marries another man. The seed game with the rival is invented. Read the story in [Kormáks saga: the poet Kormákr, Steingerðr and a wedding that comes too late](/en/2027/01/24/kormaks-saga-kormakr-steingerdr-poeta-islandia/).
