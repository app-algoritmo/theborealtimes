## How to play

Pesta, the old woman of the plague, travels from farm to farm with a broom and a rake. The girl has to escape and gather bread. Loaves and tools fall from above. Tap the left or right side of the screen to change lane, or use the arrow keys. Catch the golden loaves and dodge the brooms and rakes. Catching a tool is a mistake. Gather 14 loaves before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Norwegian legends, Pesta travels from farm to farm with a broom and a rake, and in several legends a girl escapes, as in Jostedalen. The falling loaves, the lanes and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Pesta: the old woman of the Black Death in Norwegian folklore, with rake and broom](/en/2027/04/04/pesta-a-velha-da-peste-negra-no-folclore-noruegues-com-rastelo-e-vassoura/).
