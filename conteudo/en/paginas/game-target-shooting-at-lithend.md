## How to play

In Njáls saga, Gunnar shoots arrows from the hall at Lithend until Thorbrand cuts his bowstring. Tap a target to loose an arrow at it before it crosses the screen. Hit the wooden targets and do not tap the white peace shields: that is a mistake. A target that escapes costs you nothing. Hit 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Njáls saga (chapter 76), Gunnar shoots arrows and withstands several attacks until Thorbrand cuts his bowstring. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Gunnar of Hlíðarendi: the man who turned back home and the bowstring that was never mended](/gunnar-of-hlidarendi-the-man-who-turned-back-home/).
