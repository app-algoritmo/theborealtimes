## How to play

At Harald's court, insults fly on two levels, the upper and the lower, toward Halli. Tap the level where the insult arrives, or use the up and down arrows, when it enters the glowing zone, to send it back. Returning with no insult in the zone is a mistake. An insult that gets past is a mistake. Return 14 insults before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Sneglu-Halla þáttr tells that the Icelander Halli faces King Harald and the poet Thjodolf with cutting replies, in a senna. The insults that fly and the return zone are invention. Read the story in [Sneglu-Halli: the sharp-tongued Icelander at the court of Harald Hardrada](/en/2027/03/21/sneglu-halli-o-islandes-de-lingua-afiada-na-corte-de-harald-hardrada/).
