## How to play

In the tale, the smith welds the steel purse with the devil inside and beats it on the anvil until the devil begs for mercy. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to strike the purse on the anvil. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 10 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the tale collected by Asbjørnsen and Moe, the smith puts the devil in the steel purse, welds it shut and beats it on the anvil until the devil begs and promises never to return. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [The Master-Smith: the Norwegian tale of the smith who trapped the devil in a pear-tree, a chair and a steel purse](/the-master-smith-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-smith/).
