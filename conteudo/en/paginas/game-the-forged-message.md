## How to play

A message was swapped for a secret sequence of coloured seals, and the same colour may repeat. Tap the colours to build a guess. When it is complete, each dark dot shows a right seal in the right place, and each light dot a right colour in the wrong place. Work out the sequence within a limited number of guesses. Tapping the guess being built removes the last seal. Time is limited. There are 3 messages.

## The story behind it

In Bjarnar saga Hítdœlakappa, Þórðr falsifies the message in which Björn asked for Oddný’s hand, spreads word that he has died and marries her. Deciphering seals is invented. Read the story in [Bjarnar saga Hítdœlakappa: two poets, one woman and a fatal feud](/en/2027/02/14/bjarnar-saga-hitdaelakappa-bjorn-thordr-oddny/).
