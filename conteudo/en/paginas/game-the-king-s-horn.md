## How to play

In the Helga þáttr Þórissonar, Helgi receives two chests, one of gold and one of silver, from an otherworldly princess, and later two drinking horns arrive at King Óláfr's court and are blessed. Here you move the king's horn to catch the gold from the chests and avoid the spilled ale. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the king's horn and catch the gold from the chests. Avoid the spilled ale: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Helga þáttr Þórissonar, a tale in the Flateyjarbók, Helgi gets lost in the fog, spends three nights with Ingibjörg, daughter of King Guðmundr, and receives two chests, one of gold and one of silver. At a Christmas, two drinking horns sent by Guðmundr appear at the court of King Óláfr Tryggvason. A bishop blesses them, the visitors spill the ale, put out the lights and vanish. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
