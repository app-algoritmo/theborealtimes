## How to play

The Háttatal gathers Snorri's verses as examples of each form. Here you stack the stanzas one on top of the other. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the poet's table. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Háttatal, the last part of Snorri's Prose Edda, has about 20,000 words of verse illustrating the forms of skaldic poetry. The stack of stanzas in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Háttatal: Snorri's "Tally of Metres" and the forms of skaldic verse](/hattatal-snorri-s-tally-of-metres-and-the-forms-of-skaldic/).
