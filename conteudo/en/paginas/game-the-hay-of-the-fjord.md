## How to play

Brattahlíð still has some of Greenland's best farmland. Here you move the basket to gather the hay that falls. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the basket and catch the bales of hay. Avoid the stones of the slope: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Brattahlíð, today Qassiarsuk, was Erik the Red's farm in Greenland, and there the small church of Þjóðhildr was found in 1961. The basket, the hay and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Brattahlíð: Erik the Red's farm in Greenland, and the small church of Þjóðhildr found in 1961](/brattahlid-erik-the-red-s-farm-in-greenland-and-the-small/).
