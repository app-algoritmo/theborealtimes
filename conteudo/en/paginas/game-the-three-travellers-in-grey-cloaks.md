## How to play

King Olaf travels with Finnr Árnason and Þormóðr Kolbrúnarskáld, all three in grey cloaks and under the false name Grímr. Watch the order in which the three travellers rise and glow. Then they swap places. When the arrow appears, tap the travellers in the same order in which they glowed, or use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. A wrong traveller is a mistake, and the order is shown again. Get 8 orders right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, in Völsa þáttr King Olaf II travels with only Finnr Árnason and Þormóðr Kolbrúnarskáld, all three in grey cloaks to hide who they are, and all three say their name is Grímr, "hooded". The heights, the order you repeat and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [Völsa þáttr: King Olaf and the household ritual of the völsi in the Flateyjarbók](/volsa-thattr-king-olaf-and-the-household-ritual/).
