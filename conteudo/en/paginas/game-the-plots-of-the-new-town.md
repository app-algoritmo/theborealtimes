## How to play

In Lund, the 65–80 Viking farms became 600 to 700 house plots. Twelve plots form the new town, and a marker sweeps across them without stopping. One of the plots glows. Tap the screen, or press Space, when the marker is over the plot that glows, to mark it. Tapping off the plot is a mistake. Mark 10 plots before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Medieval Histories article, the original 65–80 Viking farms of Lund became 600 to 700 medieval plots, and plots along Stora Torget were about 2000 to 2500 m². The twelve plots, the marker and the pace of the game are invention. Read the story in [Lund: the city believed founded in 990, from 65 farms to 700 plots](/lund-the-city-believed-founded-in-990-from-65-farms-to-700/).
