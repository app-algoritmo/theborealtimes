## How to play

Buttercup put a root and a stone in the sack, in his place, and fooled the hag. Round grey stones pass before you. Tap the stones to put them in the sack. Do not tap Buttercup, round and yellow: he is the boy, and must not go in the sack. Tapping Buttercup is a mistake. Put 14 stones in the sack before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, in Dasent's translation, Buttercup cuts a hole in the hag's sack and leaves a fir-tree root in his place. The second time, he puts in a great stone, which punches a hole in the pot. The passing stones, the yellow boy and the pace of the game are invention.
