## How to play

Four stones echo at the cave mouth, each with a sound and a colour. Watch the sequence and repeat it by tapping the stones in the same order. Each round the sequence grows by one howl. Reach 8 howls with at most 2 mistakes; the third mistake ends the game. (Keyboard: keys 1 to 4.)

## The story behind it

In the Völuspá, Garmr's howl before Gnipahellir returns like a refrain and announces Ragnarök. The echo game is invented. Read the story in [Garmr, the hound that howls before Gnipahellir: what the Norse sources tell](/en/2027/01/17/garmr-cao-hel-gnipahellir-ragnarok/).
