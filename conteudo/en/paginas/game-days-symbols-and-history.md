## How to play

The primstav marks days with notches, feasts with symbols and has a history of its own. Take each phrase to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The primstav is a Norwegian wooden calendar, used from the 14th to the 19th century, with notches for the days and about 55 feast symbols. The oldest dated example is from 1457. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
