## How to play

Every square will receive a number in sequence. You start at 1: tap a neighbouring square, diagonals included, to place the 2, and so on. The golden numbers are already in place and only fit their own squares. Tap the last square placed to undo it. Time is limited. There are 3 grids.

## The story behind it

Harald Klak was a Danish king baptised in 826. Linking neighbouring numbers up to a river mouth is invented. Read the story in [Harald Klak: the Danish king baptized in 826, ally of Louis the Pious](/en/2027/02/28/harald-klak-o-rei-dinamarques-batizado-em-826-aliado-de-luis-o-piedoso/).
