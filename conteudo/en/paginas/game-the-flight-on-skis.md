## How to play

According to legend, Gustav Vasa fled on skis, and men from Mora caught up with him at Sälen. Here you glide in three lanes, pick up provisions and dodge the spears. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the parcels of provisions from Mora and dodge the spears of the king's soldiers: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to legend, Gustav Vasa fled on skis, and the 90 km Vasaloppet, from Berga to Mora, was born in 1922. The episode is disputed, and the parcels and spears in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
