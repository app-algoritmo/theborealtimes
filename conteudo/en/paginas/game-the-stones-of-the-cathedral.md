## How to play

According to the Lund legend, the giant Finn built the cathedral stone by stone. Here you stack the cathedral's stones with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the hill of Helgonabacken. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Lund legend, the giant Finn built the cathedral in exchange for the sun and the moon, or the eyes of Saint Lars, until his name was revealed by a lullaby; in the crypt there is a stone figure hugging a column. The first known mention is from 1593. The stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
