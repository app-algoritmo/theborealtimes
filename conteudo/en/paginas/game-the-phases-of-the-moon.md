## How to play

Máni steers the Moon, and Hjúki and Bil follow it. Five moons stand in a row, and the last one is a question mark. The phases always follow the same order: the Moon grows from the right, becomes full and wanes. Tap the moon below that comes next in the row, or use keys 1, 2 and 3. A wrong moon is a mistake. Get 10 phases right before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Snorri says Hjúki and Bil follow the Moon and that Máni steers its waxing and waning. The row of phases in the game is invention. Read the story in [Hjúki and Bil: the children who follow the Moon carrying a pail in the Edda](/en/2027/03/28/hjuki-e-bil-as-criancas-que-seguem-a-lua-carregando-um-balde-na-edda/).
