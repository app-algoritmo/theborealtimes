## How to play

In Ögmundar þáttr dytts, Gunnarr flees to Sweden and crosses a blizzard in the mountains. Here you run across the snow in three lanes, collect the silver gifts and dodge the snowdrifts. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the silver gifts and dodge the snowdrifts: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Ögmundar þáttr dytts, Gunnarr helmingr flees to Sweden, destroys the idol of Freyr that goes out in a wagon procession and takes its place, asking the devotees for valuable gifts. The run, the gifts and the snowdrifts in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
