## How to play

The patch starts at the top-left corner. Tap a colour to paint the patch with it: it swallows neighbouring cells of the same colour. Paint the whole picture one colour in 18 moves or fewer.

## The story behind it

The picture stones of Gotland, in Sweden, show sailing ships from the 5th to the 12th century. The colour game is made up. Read the story in [The picture stones of Gotland: carved sailing ships and colours that hardly survive](/en/2026/12/06/gotland-pedras-pintadas-barcos-velas/).
