## How to play

Gullveig's name has to do with gold, and the Völuspá puts her under the spears, in Hárr's hall. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the gold coins and dodge the spears: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 14 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, in the Völuspá Gullveig is pierced by spears and burned three times in Hárr's hall, and is reborn, coming to be called Heiðr. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
