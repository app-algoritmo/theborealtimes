## How to play

Odin, Vili and Vé killed Ymir and made the world from his body. A part of the body appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the place in the world it became, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the earth, the second is the sea and the third is the sky. A wrong box, or a part left unanswered, is a mistake. Return 10 parts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Gylfaginning and the Grímnismál, the flesh became the earth, the bones became mountains, the teeth became stones, the hair became trees, the blood became the sea, the skull became the sky, the brains became clouds and the sparks of Muspell were set in the sky. The row of parts, the three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Ymir: the giant born of ice and venom whose body the gods made into the world](/ymir-the-giant-born-of-ice-and-venom-whose-body-the-gods/).
