## How to play

Fifteen rivets hold the hull planks, and one of them is loose. Tap a rivet and then the empty hole on the other side of a neighbour: the neighbour comes out. Remove them all until only one is left. You can undo 3 moves, with the button or the U key.

## The story behind it

The Ladby ship in Denmark was a Viking chieftain's grave, its hull held by rivets. The jumping game is made up. Read the story in [Ladby: the Danish ship grave that left only marks and rivets in the earth](/en/2026/11/21/ladby-navio-funerario-dinamarca/).
