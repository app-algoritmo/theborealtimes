## How to play

Hreiðarr, taken for a fool, wins the friendship of King Magnús and draws the wrath of Haraldr. Here the cups of the royal feast arrive at the table among the courtiers' knives. Tap the mead cups of the feast as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the courtiers' knives: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Hreiðars þáttr heimska, the Icelander Hreiðarr wins the friendship of Magnús, kills one of Haraldr's courtiers and draws his wrath. The cups and knives in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Hreiðars þáttr heimska: the Icelander taken for a fool at the court of Magnús and Haraldr](/hreidars-thattr-heimska-the-icelander-taken-for-a-fool/).
