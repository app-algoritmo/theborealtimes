## How to play

Ivar Aasen travelled across Norway studying the dialects to write his grammar and his dictionary. Here you tap the notebooks of words and do not tap the ink blots. Tap the notebooks of words as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the ink blots: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Aasen published his grammar in 1848, his dictionary in 1850 and, in 1873, the *Norsk Ordbog*, based on his travels researching the dialects. The notebooks and the blots in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Ivar Aasen: the farmers' son who travelled Norway gathering dialects and built Landsmål, today's Nynorsk](/ivar-aasen-the-farmers-son-who-travelled-norway-gathering/).
