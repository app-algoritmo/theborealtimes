## How to play

One rock keeps the law, and it has a number. Tap a number to try. The game says whether the right rock lies at a higher or lower number, and the numbers that no longer fit are dimmed. Tapping a dimmed number counts as a mistake. Make few mistakes. There are 3 rounds.

## The story behind it

The lawspeaker, the lagman, was an office in Scandinavia. Guessing a number among rocks is invented. Read the story in [The lawspeaker: the lawman in Scandinavia, from Iceland to Sweden](/en/2027/02/28/o-recitador-da-lei-o-lagman-na-escandinavia-da-islandia-a-suecia/).
