## How to play

In the Hyndluljóð, Freyja forces the seeress Hyndla to recite Óttar's ancestors. Here you write them down. Tap the stones with an ancestor's name, in gold runes as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the cracked stones with no name: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Hyndluljóð, Freyja rides the boar Hildisvíni, who is Óttar in disguise, to the seeress Hyndla and forces her to recite his lineage, generation by generation. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Hildisvíni, Freyja's boar, and the man who travelled disguised as a pig to the seeress Hyndla](/hildisvini-freyja-s-boar-and-the-man-who-travelled/).
