## How to play

Eir has few lines, and each one is in a different source. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “Fjölsvinnsmál”, the second is “Gylfaginning” and the third is “Skáldskaparmál”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, Eir is one of Menglöð's maidens in Fjölsvinnsmál, the third of the ásynjur and “an extremely good physician” in the Gylfaginning, and a valkyrie name in the Skáldskaparmál. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
