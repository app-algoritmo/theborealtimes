## How to play

In the fragment, Hnæf and his men hold the doors of the hall for five days. Here you defend the door with the shield against every blow of the attackers. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Finnsburg Fragment, of about 50 lines, copied by George Hickes and published in 1705, tells that Hnæf and his men held the doors for five days, with none of them falling. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [The Finnsburg Fragment: Hnæf and the sixty warriors who held the doors of the hall for five days](/the-finnsburg-fragment-hnaef-and-the-sixty-warriors/).
