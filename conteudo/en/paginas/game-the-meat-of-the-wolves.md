## How to play

Odin gives the two wolves everything he is served at the table and lives on wine alone. Here you move Geri and Freki to catch the meat and avoid the cups of wine. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the two wolves and catch the pieces of meat. Avoid the cups of wine: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Grímnismál, Odin says he gives food to Geri and Freki and himself lives on wine alone, and Snorri repeats the detail in the Gylfaginning. The meat and the cups in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
