## How to play

Hrafnagaldr Óðins means "the raven-chant of Odin". Here you keep the rhythm of the chant, beat by beat, beside the raven. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to chant with the raven. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Hrafnagaldr Óðins, a poem of 26 stanzas that survives only in paper copies from the 17th century on, is seen by nearly all scholars as post-medieval. The raven and the rhythm in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
