## How to play

A knot of threads joins the points on the board. Tap a point to start and then, one by one, the neighbouring points to run along the threads: each thread must be run exactly once, no more and no less, without lifting your finger from the line. Tapping a point with no free thread leading to it, or getting stuck at a point with no free thread, is a mistake and the knot is undone. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Borre mound cemetery, in Vestfold, Norway, has seven large mounds and 21 smaller ones. The Borre style, tied to the place, is known for animal and knot ornaments, often used on harnesses. The one-stroke knot is invented. Read the story in [Borre, in Vestfold: seven great mounds, a buried ship and the style of knots and animals](/en/2027/01/24/borre-cemiterio-montes-vestfold-estilo-borre/).
