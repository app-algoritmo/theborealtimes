## How to play

Karlsefni and his men traded goods with the natives of Vinland. Sixteen cargoes appear open for a few seconds and then turn over. Tap two cargoes to open them: if they match, the pair stays open. If they differ, both close again and that is a mistake. Find the 8 pairs before time runs out. Nine mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Eiríks saga rauða, Karlsefni's settlers trade goods with the natives, the skrælings, until a bull breaks loose and frightens them. The symbols on the cargoes and the pairs are invention.
