## How to play

Sleipnir, Odin's eight-legged horse, runs along a road between the worlds. Golden sheaves of oats and grey stones from the wall fall from above. Tap the left or right side of the screen to change lanes, or use the arrow keys. Catch the golden sheaves and dodge the stones. Hitting a stone is a mistake. Gather 14 sheaves before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Gylfaginning, Sleipnir was born of Loki, in the shape of a mare, and of the stallion Svaðilfari, who hauled the stones of the wall of Asgard. The Grímnismál calls him the best of horses. The road, the sheaves of oats and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Sleipnir: Odin's eight-legged horse and his strange origin](/sleipnir-odin-s-eight-legged-horse-and-his-strange-origin/).
