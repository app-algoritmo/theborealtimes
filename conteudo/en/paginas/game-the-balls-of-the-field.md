## How to play

In knattleikr, players contested a ball with wooden bats in front of a crowd. Here you tap the balls that jump on the field and do not tap the stones. Tap the balls as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the stones: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Knattleikr, a ball game played with a wooden bat, appears in several Icelandic sagas, among them Egils saga; the sagas give no rulebook, so what is known of the rules is incomplete. The balls and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
