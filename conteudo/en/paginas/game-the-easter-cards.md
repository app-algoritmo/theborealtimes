## How to play

The children dressed as Easter witches carry cards to trade for sweets. Sixteen cards show face up for a few seconds and then turn over. Tap two cards to open them: if they match, the pair stays open. If they are different, both close again and that is a mistake. Find the 8 pairs before time runs out. Nine mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Nordiska museet, Swedish children dress up as Easter witches and wish a happy Easter with cards, hoping for sweets or a coin. The cards in the game and the pairs are invention. Read the story in [Easter witches in Sweden: from Blåkulla to children with Easter cards](/easter-witches-in-sweden-from-blakulla-to-children/).
