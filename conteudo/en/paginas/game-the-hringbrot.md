## How to play

The Jarlmanns saga ok Hermanns contains the first written mention of the hringbrot, an Icelandic circle dance, and Iceland's first dance of elves. Here you mark the rhythm of the circle dance. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to dance the elves' hringbrot. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Jarlmanns saga ok Hermanns is an Icelandic chivalric saga that survives in 76 manuscripts, by a 2020 count. Hermann, son of the king of France, sends his foster-brother Jarlmann to Miklagarðr to woo Ríkilát. The old king Rudent abducts the bride, and Jarlmann kills the giantess Þorbjörg. The saga contains the first written evidence of the hringbrot and the first Icelandic attestation of elves dancing. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
