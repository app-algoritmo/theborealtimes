## How to play

At Lejre, the Grydehøj burial mound held gold threads and bronze pieces. Nine mounds of earth stand in front of you. A glint appears on one mound at a time, gold or bronze. Tap the mound that glints before the glint fades, or use keys 1 to 9. Tapping a mound with no glint, or letting a glint fade, is a mistake. Collect 12 finds before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the UCLA page on Lejre, the Grydehøj burial mound, of about 550, held gold threads and bronze pieces. The mounds with glints and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Lejre: the great royal hall in Denmark and the sacrifices every nine years](/en/2027/03/28/lejre-o-grande-salao-do-rei-na-dinamarca-e-os-sacrificios-de-nove-em-nove-anos/).
