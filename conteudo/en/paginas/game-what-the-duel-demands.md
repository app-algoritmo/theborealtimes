## How to play

In the hólmganga there was a fighting ground, stakes at the corners and shields. The request appears at the top of the screen. Tap the object asked for before the time for the request runs out: the hide, the hazel stave or the shield. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. After each request, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a request left unanswered, is a mistake. Answer 10 requests before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the hólmganga was fought on a hide about 3 metres on a side, with hazel stakes at the corners and, in general, three shields for each fighter. The order of the requests, the timing and the swaps of place are invention. Read the story in [The hólmganga: the duel on the ox hide, the three shields and the ban of 1006](/the-holmganga-the-duel-on-the-ox-hide-the-three-shields/).
