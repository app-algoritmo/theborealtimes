## How to play

In Njáls saga, the life of Kári runs through the sea, the fire and the vengeance. Take each moment to the right chapter. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Njáls saga, Kári Sölmundarson is the one who escapes the burning of Bergþórshvoll, around 1010, and later pursues the Burners. His sword was called Fjörsváfnir. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
