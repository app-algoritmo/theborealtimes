Every game here comes from a story published on the site. They work on phones, tablets and computers, need no sign-up and collect no data.

<ul class="jogos-lista">
<li><a href="/en/game-thors-fishing-trip/">Thor's fishing trip</a><br>Hold the line, let go at the right moment and bring the Midgard Serpent to the surface. Based on the story of Thor and the Midgard Serpent (Portuguese edition).</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-leifs-voyage/">Leif's voyage</a><br>Dodge ice and rocks until you reach Vinland. Based on the story of Leif Eriksson and Vinland (Portuguese edition).</li>
</ul>

The games are entertainment inspired by the stories. Where a game changes something the sources tell, the story text explains what is actually recorded.
