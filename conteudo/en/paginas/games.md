---
titulo: Games
resumo: Short browser games, played fullscreen, inspired by the stories of The Boreal Times, for all ages.
olho: Play the stories
atualizado: November 2026
---

Every game here comes from a story published on the site. They open fullscreen on phones, tablets and computers, need no sign-up and collect no data.

<ul class="jogos-lista">
<li><a href="/en/game-thors-fishing-trip/">Thor's fishing trip</a><br>hold the line, let go when the serpent lunges, and bring Jörmungandr to the surface, as in the story told by Snorri.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-leifs-voyage/">Leif's voyage</a><br>steer Leif Eriksson's ship through ice and rocks, from Helluland to Markland and on to Vinland, as the sagas tell it.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-idun-and-the-apples/">Idun and the apples</a><br>catch the golden apples of youth before they fall and dodge the eagle, in an adventure inspired by the myth of Idun.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-three-billy-goats/">The Three Billy Goats Bruse</a><br>run across the bridge with the three goats, jump the gaps and the troll's hands, and reach the pasture.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-barn-nisse/">The barn nisse</a><br>be the barn nisse and look after the animals through the winter night, until the girl brings the porridge at dawn.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-flight-of-huginn/">The flight of Huginn</a><br>flap with the raven Huginn, fly between the branches of the World Tree and bring the news to Odin.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-gleipnir-binds-the-wolf/">Gleipnir, the impossible ribbon</a><br>gather the six things that no longer exist so the dwarfs can make Gleipnir, then hold the ribbon at the right tension to bind the wolf Fenrir.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-rognvalds-comb/">Rögnvald’s comb</a><br>untangle Harald’s hair by dragging over the knots, as in the scene where Rögnvald combs it after Hafrsfjord.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-egils-night/">Egil’s night</a><br>tap the words that start with the verse’s letter and compose six verses before dawn, as Egil did to save his head.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-digging-at-gjermundbu/">Digging at Gjermundbu</a><br>clear the soil from a Viking grave and uncover the iron Gjermundbu helmet, with no horns.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-oseberg-hull/">The Oseberg hull</a><br>drop oak planks at the right moment and build a Viking ship hull, as in the Oseberg and Gokstad ships.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-freyjas-forge/">The dwarfs’ forge</a><br>watch the order of the anvils and repeat the strikes of the four dwarfs forging Freyja’s necklace.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-ulfberht-blade/">The Ulfberht blade</a><br>strike the hot iron at the right moment and forge a sword bearing the inscription VLFBERHT.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-askeladden-and-the-troll/">Askeladden and the troll</a><br>hide porridge in a bag without the troll noticing, like Askeladden in the eating contest.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-wall-of-asgard/">The wall of Asgard</a><br>be Loki disguised as a mare and distract the builder’s horse so the wall of Asgard is not finished before summer.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-hnefatafl/">Hnefatafl in miniature</a><br>take the king to one of the board’s corners before the attackers surround him, in a small version of the Viking game.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-birkebeiner-crossing/">The Birkebeiner crossing</a><br>ski down the mountain with little Prince Haakon, dodging trees and rocks, until you reach safety.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-salt-mill/">The mill that grinds salt</a><br>turn the magic mill to fill the ship with salt, but let go in time: it is slow to stop and the ship can sink.</li>
<li><a href="/en/game-carve-the-runes/">Carve the runes</a><br>carve five runes by connecting the dots, as if chiseling the Jelling stone.</li>
</ul>

The games are entertainment inspired by the stories. Where a game changes something the sources tell, the game page explains what is actually recorded.
