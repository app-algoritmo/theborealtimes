## How to play

Áskell goes to Leyningsbakki, the "bank of hiding", beside the river Laxá. Here you run along the riverbank in three lanes. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the gravel stones of the bank and dodge the waves of the river: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Reykdæla saga, Áskell, chieftain of Hvammr, chooses Leyningsbakki to be buried, and the place still exists, downstream of a hydroelectric power station. The run and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Reykdæla saga: Áskell goði, the gravel bank of Leyningsbakki and the farm that now holds a power station](/reykdaela-saga-askell-godi-the-gravel-bank-of-leyningsbakki/).
