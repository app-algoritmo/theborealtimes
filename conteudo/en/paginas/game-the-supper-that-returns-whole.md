## How to play

In Valhalla, the cook Andhrímnir boils Sæhrímnir every day. Four tables stand around the cauldron, and meat piles up at one of them at a time. Tap the table to serve when its bar passes the mark, or use the arrow keys. Tapping a table with no meat, or serving too early, is a mistake. If the meat passes the full bar, it goes cold and is lost, and that is also a mistake. Serve 12 times before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Grímnismál, Andhrímnir cooks Sæhrímnir in the cauldron Eldhrímnir, and the Gylfaginning says the animal is a boar, whole again every night. The tables, the meat that piles up and the rhythm of the game are invention.
