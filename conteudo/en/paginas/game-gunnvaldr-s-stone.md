## How to play

The Snoldelev stone is 1.25 metres tall and says it is Gunnvaldr's, a þulr of Salhaugar. Here you raise it with blocks. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the field. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Snoldelev stone, from the 9th century, is 1.25 metres tall and says: "Gunnvaldr's stone, Hróaldr's son, þulr of Salhaugar". It bears three interlaced horns, whose meaning scholars still debate. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
