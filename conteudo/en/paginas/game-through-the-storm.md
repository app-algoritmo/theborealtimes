## How to play

The caption of the Vegvísir promises that whoever carries it will not get lost in the storm. Here you run through the snow in three lanes. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the staves that mark the way and dodge the snow whirls: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Vegvísir, "way-finder", appears in the Huld manuscript, of 1860, and promises that whoever carries it will not get lost in storms. It has no clear link to the Viking Age. The run and the staves in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
