## How to play

In the Atlamál, Guðrún sends a warning in runes and the messenger Vingi corrupts it. Kostbera still reads enough to sense the danger. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Kostbera's pouch and catch Guðrún's runes. Avoid the runes swapped by Vingi: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Atlamál, Guðrún sent a warning in runes, Vingi corrupted it and Kostbera read enough to sense the danger. The sticks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Atlamál: the Greenlandic poem of Atli, with the women's dreams and the swapped message of runes](/atlamal-the-greenlandic-poem-of-atli-with-the-women-s/).
