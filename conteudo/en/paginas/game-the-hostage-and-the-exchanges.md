## How to play

Like Hœnir among the Vanir, you trade with a partner for 10 rounds. In each one, choose Share (split the offering) or Keep (hold on to it). Both share: 3 points each. You keep and they share: 5 for you and 0 for them. You share and they keep: 0 for you and 5 for them. Both keep: 1 point each. The partner starts by sharing and then repeats your choice from the previous round. Reach 28 points. (Keyboard: 1 shares, 2 keeps.)

## The story behind it

In the Ynglinga saga, after the war between the Æsir and the Vanir, Hœnir and Mímir are sent to the Vanir as hostages; Hœnir is soon made chief but depends on Mímir to decide. The rounds of exchange are invented. Read the story in [Hœnir, the god who helps make humans and relies on Mímir: what the sources say](/en/2027/01/24/hoenir-deus-aesir-criacao-refem-lokka-tattur/).
