## How to play

Pieces come down the belt: round coins go left and bone combs go right. Tap the left or right side of the screen (or use ← →) to send the lowest piece. Sort 30 pieces correctly. Sending one the wrong way or letting one pass counts as a mistake: on the third mistake, the workshop stops.

## The story behind it

Founded around 980, Sigtuna minted the first Swedish coins. The sorting of coins and combs is invented. Read the story in [Sigtuna: Sweden's first real town, with coins, aligned plots and more than 40 runestones](/en/2026/12/27/sigtuna-primeira-cidade-sueca-moedas-runas/).
