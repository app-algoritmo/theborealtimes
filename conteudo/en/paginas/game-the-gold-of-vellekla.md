## How to play

Vellekla means "lack of gold", and the poem praises Jarl Hákon. Here a ship follows the Sunnmøre coast after the gold. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the poet's gold and dodge the rocks of the coast: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to tradition, the battle of Hjörungavágr took place in 986 off the Sunnmøre coast, and the Vellekla is one of the sources. The ship and the gold in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Vellekla: the poem Einarr skálaglamm made for Jarl Hákon, and the battle of Hjörungavágr](/vellekla-the-poem-einarr-skalaglamm-made-for-jarl-hakon/).
