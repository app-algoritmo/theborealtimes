## How to play

In the tale, the shepherds, bribed with silver objects, tell the king that everything belongs to Lord Peter. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the sack and catch the silver objects. Avoid the troll's stones: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Dasent's translation, the shepherds, bribed with silver objects, tell the king that everything belongs to Lord Peter, and the cat leads the king to a silver castle. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Lord Peter: the Norwegian tale of the cat who made the youngest son pass for a lord and tricked a troll with a story about rye](/lord-peter-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-cat-who-made/).
