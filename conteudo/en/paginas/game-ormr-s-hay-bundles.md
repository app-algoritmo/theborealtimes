## How to play

In the Orms þáttr Stórólfssonar, Ormr carries two bundles of hay on his back all the way home in a hard winter. Here you stack the bundles with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it next to the barn at Hváll. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Orms þáttr Stórólfssonar, a tale in the Flateyjarbók, Ormr, son of Stórólfr, is the strongest man in Iceland: in a hard winter, he takes two bundles of hay from his neighbour Dufþakr and carries them on his back, and this starts a feud between the two families. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
