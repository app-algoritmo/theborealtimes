## How to play

Tap one of your pits, at the bottom, to sow counter-clockwise. If the last seed lands in your store, on the right, you play again. If it lands in an empty pit of yours, you also take whatever is in the opposite pit. It ends when one side runs empty. Beat the draugr with more seeds in your store.

## The story behind it

In Grettis saga, Kárr the Old is called a draugr and lies in a mound that reeks horribly. The seed game is invented. Read the story in [The draugr, the dead who do not stay in the mound: what the Icelandic sagas tell](/en/2027/01/10/draugr-morto-vivo-sagas-islandesas-monte/).
