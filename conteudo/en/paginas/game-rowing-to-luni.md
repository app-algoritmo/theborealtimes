## How to play

The fleet of Hastein and Björn went down the Iberian coast and entered the Mediterranean. Here you row in time with the ship. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to row to Luni. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Sources from several peoples record a great Viking expedition into the Mediterranean in 859–861, led by Hastein and Björn. Luni is the place of the fake funeral trick, told by William of Jumièges. The rowing rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Björn Ironside and Hastein: the Viking expedition to the Mediterranean of 859 to 862 and the fake funeral trick at Luni](/bjorn-ironside-and-hastein-the-viking-expedition/).
