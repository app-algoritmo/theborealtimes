## How to play

The castle gate has three posts and a stack of rings of different sizes on the left post. Carry the whole stack to the right post. Tap a post to pick up its top ring and tap another to drop it. Never put a larger ring on a smaller one. The number of moves and the time are limited. There are 3 rounds, with 3, 4 and 5 rings.

## The story behind it

In Fjölsvinnsmál, the watchman Fjölsviðr asks Svipdagr’s name, and Svipdagr hides it until the end; when he gives it, the gates open and Menglöð greets him. Moving rings between posts is invented.
