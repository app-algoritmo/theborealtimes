## How to play

In the Fimbulvetr the snow comes from every side. Four drifts grow around the house, one at a time. Tap a drift to sweep it once its bar passes the mark, or use the arrow keys. Tapping a side with no snow, or sweeping too early, is a mistake. If the snow passes the full bar, it floods the house and that is also a mistake. Sweep 12 drifts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Snorri says the Fimbulvetr brings three winters in a row with no summer, and that snow comes from every quarter. The drifts to sweep and the rhythm of the game are invention.
