## How to play

After Sverre's death in 1202, Haakon III reigned for two years, and his death left suspicions about Queen Margareta and the succession of the boy Guttorm. Here you take each phrase to the person it is about. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Haakon Sverresson, Haakon III, was born around 1183 and was king of Norway from 1202 to 1204. He died on 1 January 1204, after a bloodletting at Christmas. Poisoning was suspected and the suspicion fell on Margareta, Sverre's queen, who fled to Sweden after an ordeal. The poisoning is a suspicion, not an established fact. The successor was Guttorm, Haakon's nephew, about four years old. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
