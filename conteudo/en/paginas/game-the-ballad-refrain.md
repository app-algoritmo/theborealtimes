## How to play

In the Swedish ballad Herr Mannelig, a mountain creature asks the knight to marry her. Here you mark the rhythm of the refrain, line by line. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to sing the ballad's refrain. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Herr Mannelig is a Swedish ballad (SMB 26) in which a mountain creature offers gifts to a knight, who refuses. The oldest version with a named source was published in 1877, in Södermanland, and there are variants such as Skogsjungfruns frieri and Herr Magnus; groups such as Garmarna recorded it in the twentieth century. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
