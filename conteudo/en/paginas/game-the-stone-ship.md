## How to play

At Ale's Stones, 59 blocks draw a ship 67 metres long. Twelve places form the outline of the ship, and a golden marker runs around it without stopping. One of the places glows. Tap the screen, or press Space, when the marker is over the place that glows, to raise a block there. Tapping off the place is a mistake. Raise 10 blocks before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Kåseberga, in Scania, 59 blocks of up to 1,800 kilograms draw a ship 67 metres long, which radiocarbon dates to about 1,400 years ago. The twelve places in the game, the marker and the rhythm are invention. Read the story in [Ale's Stones: the stone ship of Sweden and the debate over its age](/en/2027/03/28/ales-stenar-o-navio-de-pedra-da-suecia-e-o-debate-sobre-a-sua-idade/).
