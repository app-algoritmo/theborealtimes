## How to play

The ground of the fort is divided into blocks of earth. Tap a block to dig. Each dig shows, as a number, how many hidden finds lie in the neighbouring blocks. Find all the hidden objects before you run out of digs. Complete 3 sites before time runs out. Three lost sites end the game.

## The story behind it

Fyrkat was excavated from 1950 to 1958 and has 16 longhouses and about 30 graves. The hidden finds in the game are invented. Read the story in [Fyrkat: the Viking ring fortress near Hobro, in Denmark, around 980](/en/2027/03/07/fyrkat-o-forte-circular-viking-perto-de-hobro-na-dinamarca-por-volta-de-980/).
