## How to play

The Hálfs saga ok Hálfsrekka speaks of King Hálfr's companions, of the feast where the hall was burned and of the survivors' revenge. Take each fact to the right box. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Hálfs saga ok Hálfsrekka, an Icelandic legendary saga, King Hálfr and half his men die in a burning hall in Hörðaland, and the survivors take revenge. Many scholars place it in the early fourteenth century. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Hálfs saga ok Hálfsrekka: King Hálfr, Ásmundr's feast, the hall burned in Hörðaland and the survivors' revenge](/halfs-saga-ok-halfsrekka-king-halfr-asmundr-s-feast/).
