## How to play

In Laxdæla saga, Kjartan, Bolli and Guðrún live a triangle of love and revenge. An act appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the one who did it, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is Kjartan, the second is Bolli and the third is Guðrún. A wrong box, or an act left unanswered, is a mistake. Link 10 acts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Laxdæla saga, Kjartan accepts baptism in Nidaros, gives Ingibjörg's headdress to Hrefna and besieges Bolli's farm. Bolli marries Guðrún and kills Kjartan. Guðrún urges her brothers to ambush Kjartan and, in the end, says she was worst to the one she loved most. The row of acts, the three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention.
