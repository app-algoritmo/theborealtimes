## How to play

Helgi, the poet, travels to Greenland and becomes a prominent figure at Brattahlíð. Here you move the boat across the fjord and catch the fish that rise. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move Helgi's boat and catch the fish of the fjord. Avoid the blocks of ice: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Skáld-Helga saga, known through the rímur of the 14th century, Helgi Þórðarson travels to Greenland and becomes a prominent figure at Brattahlíð. The boat, fish and ice in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Skáld-Helga saga: the poet Helgi who went to Greenland and the rímur that kept the story](/skald-helga-saga-the-poet-helgi-who-went-to-greenland/).
