## How to play

After his death, coins bearing Edmund's name were struck for decades. Here you move the king to catch the coins and avoid the arrows. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the king and catch the memorial coins. Avoid the arrows: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Edmund, king of East Anglia, died in 869 or 870 before the Great Heathen Army. Coins with the legend SCE EADMVND REX were struck by more than 70 moneyers, and the Cuerdale Hoard held more than 1,800. The arrows and the collecting in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
