## How to play

In the saga, Eirek reaches a land of streams of honey, before a wall of fire. Here you gather the drops of honey in the cup and dodge the sparks. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the cup and catch the drops of honey. Avoid the sparks of the wall of fire: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Eiríks saga víðförla, a late saga of around 1300, Eirek reaches, past a dragon, a land of streams of honey and a wall of fire before Paradise. The drops and sparks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Eiríks saga víðförla: the traveller who leapt into a dragon's mouth in search of the Land of the Undying](/eiriks-saga-vidforla-the-traveller-who-leapt-into-a-dragon/).
