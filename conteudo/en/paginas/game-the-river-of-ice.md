## How to play

Cross the river by hopping from block to block. Tap the screen, or press Space, to jump to the next block. You can only land on it, so wait until the block passes right in front of you. Reach the far bank 4 times before time runs out. Three falls into the water end the game.

## The story behind it

In the myth, Thor waded the Élivágar rivers with Aurvandil in a basket, and a frozen toe became a star. The crossing by hopping is invented. Read the story in [Aurvandil: the toe that became a star in the myth of Thor](/en/2027/03/07/aurvandil-o-dedo-do-pe-que-virou-estrela-no-mito-de-thor/).
