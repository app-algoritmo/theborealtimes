## How to play

Thor asked that the bones of the goats be laid on the hides. The bones pass along the table. Tap the short white bones to lay them on the hide. The thigh bone is the long one with red ends: do not touch it, because Thjálfi broke it. Tapping the thigh bone is a mistake. Lay 14 bones on the hide before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Prose Edda, Thjálfi breaks the thigh bone of one of Thor's goats, and for that Thor takes Thjálfi and Röskva as servants. The bones passing along the table and the rhythm of the game are invention. Read the story in [Thjálfi and Röskva: the broken bone and the two servants of Thor](/en/2027/03/28/thjalfi-e-roskva-o-osso-quebrado-e-os-dois-servos-de-thor/).
