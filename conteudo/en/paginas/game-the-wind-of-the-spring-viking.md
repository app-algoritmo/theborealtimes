## How to play

Svein sowed his fields and then set out on his spring viking. Gusts of wind cross the sea of Orkney. The blue ones point to the front of the ship and fill the sail: tap them to catch them. The red ones point backward, against the sail: let them pass. Tapping a red gust is a mistake. Catch 14 blue gusts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Orkneyinga saga, Svein spent the winter at home, sowed in spring and set out on his "spring viking", returning after midsummer; after the harvest he went out on the "autumn viking". The gusts of wind in the game and the rhythm are invention. Read the story in [Svein Asleifarson: the Orkney raider who went "viking" twice a year](/svein-asleifarson-the-orkney-raider-who-went-viking-twice/).
