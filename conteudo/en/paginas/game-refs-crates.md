## How to play

Tap a square next to your character to walk to it. If there is a crate on that square, you push it one square ahead, and it will not move if there is a wall or another crate behind it. Bring all the crates to the golden marks. Tapping your own character restarts the map. Time is limited. There are 3 maps.

## The story behind it

In the Króka-Refs saga, Ref builds a notable ship and is given the nickname "Ref the Sly" by King Harald Hardrada. Pushing crates onto marks is invented. Read the story in [The saga of Króka-Ref: the sly Icelander who went through Greenland, Norway and Denmark](/en/2027/02/21/saga-de-kroka-ref-o-astuto-groenlandia-noruega-danimarca/).
