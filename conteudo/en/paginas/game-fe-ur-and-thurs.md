## How to play

The Icelandic rune poem gives each rune a short image. Here you take each reading to the right rune. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Icelandic rune poem, Fé is wealth and a source of discord among kinsmen, Úr is the bad drizzle, the lament of the clouds, and Þurs is the giant, the troll or the demon, the torment of women. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
