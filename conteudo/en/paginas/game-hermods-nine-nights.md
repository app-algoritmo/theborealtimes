## How to play

Hermóðr rides Sleipnir towards Hel. Each night a marker runs back and forth along the track. Tap (or press Space) when the marker is inside the gold band. Each night the band narrows and the marker speeds up. Get through the nine nights with at most 2 mistakes; the third mistake ends the ride.

## The story behind it

In the Gylfaginning, Hermóðr rides Odin's Sleipnir for nine nights, passes the Gjöll bridge and leaps Hel's gate. The track of markers is invented. Read the story in [Hermóðr, the son of Odin who rode nine nights to Hel in search of Baldr](/en/2027/01/17/hermodr-cavalgada-nove-noites-hel-baldr/).
