## How to play

On the night of 3 February 1161, outside Oslo, Inge was defeated and killed. Here you tap the torches that light up in the dark and avoid the sentinels. Tap the torches as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the sentinels: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The battle of Oslo was fought on the night of 3 February 1161, outside the city. According to Heimskringla, Inge had 4,800 men, but many of them, led by Guðrøðr Óláfsson, went over to Haakon Herdebrei. Inge was defeated and killed, and buried in the church of Saint Hallvard. Haakon reigned only one more year: he was killed at Sekken, on 7 July 1162, by Erling Skakke. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Oslo, 1161: the night battle in which King Inge fell and young Haakon Broad-Shoulders came to the throne, until Sekken in 1162](/oslo-1161-the-night-battle-in-which-king-inge-fell/).
