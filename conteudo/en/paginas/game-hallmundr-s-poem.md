## How to play

In the Bergbúa þáttr, Þórðr and his servant hear in the cave the voice of a giant reciting a poem of twelve stanzas. Here you mark the time of each stanza. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to recite Hallmundr's stanzas. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Bergbúa þáttr, an Icelandic tale of the thirteenth century, Þórðr and a servant, lost in a snowstorm, shelter in a cave, hear three times a poem of twelve stanzas recited by a giant called Hallmundr and, in the morning, no longer find the cave. Þórðr keeps the whole poem, and the servant remembers nothing. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
