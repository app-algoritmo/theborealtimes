## How to play

You play the light discs. Tap a marked square to place a disc: the rival discs between it and another of yours turn to yours. Whoever has more discs when no moves are left wins; a tie counts as a loss. You have 150 seconds. (Keyboard: arrows move, Space plays.)

## The story behind it

Haakon Sigurdsson ruled northern Norway as Earl of Lade and died around 995. The board is invented. Read the story in [Haakon Sigurdsson, Earl of Lade: the lord of Norway who died in a pigsty](/en/2027/01/03/haakon-sigurdsson-jarl-de-lade/).
