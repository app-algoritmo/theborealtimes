## How to play

Fulla keeps Frigg's ash-wood box, her shoes and her secrets. The queen's request appears at the top of the screen. Tap the object asked for before the time for the request runs out: the ash-wood box, the shoes or the gold ring. You can also use keys 1, 2 and 3 for the places from left to right. After each request, the objects swap places. A wrong object, or a request left unanswered, is a mistake. Answer 10 requests before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Gylfaginning, Fulla keeps Frigg's ash-wood box (eski), looks after her shoes and knows her secrets. After Baldr's death, he sends a ring to Fulla. The order of the requests, the timing and the swaps of place are invention.
