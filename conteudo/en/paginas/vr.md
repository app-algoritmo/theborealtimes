Boreal VR turns the illustrations of our stories into a giant curved screen that fills your view, with the story read aloud by your phone's own voice. The pictures have depth: in a phone headset, the figures in the front stand out from the background.

## How to start

1. On your phone, open the VR cinema with the button above, or scan the code below.
2. For a headset, tap "Headset mode (VR)", allow motion access if the phone asks, turn the phone to landscape, slot it into the headset and look straight ahead.
3. Without a headset, tap "Explore on screen" and drag to look around.

<p><img src="/estatico/img/qr-vr-en.svg" width="168" height="168" alt="QR code that opens the Boreal VR cinema on your phone"></p>

## Choosing with your gaze

Aim the dot in the centre of the view at a realm or a button and wait about a second. Look at the arrows on the sides to change story, and look down for "Pause" and "Realms". When a story ends, the next one starts by itself.

You can also open a story straight in VR: in the Gallery, open a sticker and tap "View in VR".

## What you need

- A phone with a motion sensor: an iPhone with Safari or an Android phone with Chrome.
- Optionally, a phone headset in which the phone sits in landscape.
- Sound on. The voice depends on the voices installed on your phone.

## Good to know

- The screen stays on while you watch. With Low Power Mode on, some phones turn it off sooner.
- Lens distortion, eye distance and 3D depth can be adjusted in "Lens settings" on the start screen.
- The cinema uses only the motion sensor of your phone, never the camera or the microphone.
- As a precaution, we do not recommend it for young children, and we suggest short breaks.
