grupos:
- letra: A
  termos:
  - id: aesir
    termo: Æsir
    def: 'The main group of Norse gods, who live in Asgard: Odin, Thor, Frigg, Baldr, Týr and others.'
  - id: alfheim
    termo: Alfheim
    def: The world of the light elves in the medieval sources. In Grímnismál it is the home of the god Freyr.
  - id: asgard
    termo: Asgard
    def: The realm of the Æsir gods, high above the world, linked to the earth by the bridge Bifröst.
- letra: B
  termos:
  - id: berserker
    termo: Berserker
    def: A saga warrior who fought in a frenzy, sometimes described as wearing a bear skin. From berserkr, usually explained as “bear-shirt”.
  - id: bifrost
    termo: Bifröst
    def: The rainbow bridge between Midgard and Asgard, guarded by Heimdall.
  - id: blot
    termo: Blót
    def: A ceremony of sacrifice and feasting in the old Norse religion, in honor of gods or spirits.
- letra: D
  termos:
  - id: drakkar
    termo: Drakkar
    def: The modern name for the Viking longships, with a narrow hull and a dragon-shaped prow. The word comes from drakkr, “dragon”.
  - id: draugr
    termo: Draugr
    def: An undead creature of the Icelandic sagas, strong and heavy, who guards its own grave.
- letra: E
  termos:
  - id: edda
    termo: Edda
    def: 'The name of two medieval Icelandic works on Norse mythology: the Poetic Edda, in verse, and the Prose Edda, by Snorri Sturluson.'
  - id: einherjar
    termo: Einherjar
    def: The warriors killed in battle whom the valkyries take to Valhalla to fight beside Odin in Ragnarök.
- letra: F
  termos:
  - id: fimbulvetr
    termo: Fimbulvetr
    def: The “great winter” of three winters in a row with no summer, which comes before Ragnarök.
  - id: futhark
    termo: Futhark
    def: 'The oldest runic alphabet. The name comes from its first six letters: f, u, þ, a, r, k.'
- letra: G
  termos:
  - id: ginnungagap
    termo: Ginnungagap
    def: The great void between the ice of Niflheim and the fire of Muspelheim, where Ymir was born.
- letra: H
  termos:
  - id: hel
    termo: Hel
    def: Both the goddess of the dead, daughter of Loki, and the place where she rules, in Niflheim.
  - id: hnefatafl
    termo: Hnefatafl
    def: A Viking board game in which a king in the center is defended against attackers who want to surround him.
  - id: huldra
    termo: Huldra
    def: A forest woman of Scandinavian folklore, beautiful, with a cow’s tail, who lures wanderers.
- letra: J
  termos:
  - id: jarl
    termo: Jarl
    def: A high-ranking noble in medieval Scandinavia, below the king, who ruled a region.
  - id: jotunheim
    termo: Jotunheim
    def: The realm of the giants, of mountains, forests and ice, east of the world of men.
  - id: jotunn
    termo: Jötunn
    def: 'A giant of Norse mythology (plural: jötnar). The jötnar live in Jotunheim and are often enemies of the gods.'
- letra: K
  termos:
  - id: kenning
    termo: Kenning
    def: 'A compound metaphor of skaldic poetry: “wave-steed” means ship, “sword-sweat” means blood.'
- letra: M
  termos:
  - id: midgard
    termo: Midgard
    def: The world of humans, in the center, surrounded by the ocean where the serpent Jörmungandr lives.
  - id: mjolnir
    termo: Mjölnir
    def: Thor’s hammer, forged by the dwarves Sindri and Brokkr. It is the weapon that protects the gods from the giants.
  - id: muspelheim
    termo: Muspelheim
    def: The realm of fire, in the south, guarded by Surtr.
- letra: N
  termos:
  - id: niflheim
    termo: Niflheim
    def: The realm of ice and mist, in the north, home of the spring Hvergelmir and of the realm of Hel.
  - id: nisse
    termo: Nisse
    def: A small guardian spirit of the farm in Scandinavian folklore, who likes porridge with butter and gets angry if forgotten.
  - id: norns
    termo: Norns
    def: Three women of fate, Urðr, Verðandi and Skuld, who sit by the well of Urðr and water Yggdrasil.
- letra: R
  termos:
  - id: ragnarok
    termo: Ragnarök
    def: 'The “fate of the gods”: the final battle in which many gods and monsters die and the world is burned, to be reborn afterwards.'
  - id: runes
    termo: Runes
    def: Letters of the old Germanic alphabet, carved in stone, bone and wood. They were also believed to hold magic power.
- letra: S
  termos:
  - id: saga
    termo: Saga
    def: A prose narrative from medieval Iceland about kings, families and heroes. The word comes from segja, “to say”.
  - id: seidr
    termo: Seiðr
    def: A magical practice tied to divination and fate, associated mainly with women, such as the völur, and with the goddess Freyja.
  - id: skald
    termo: Skald
    def: A medieval Scandinavian court poet, who composed verses of praise in very complex forms.
- letra: T
  termos:
  - id: thing
    termo: Thing
    def: An assembly of free men that made laws and judged disputes. The Icelandic Althing, from 930, is the most famous.
  - id: troll
    termo: Troll
    def: In the medieval sources, a vague word for giants and monsters. In folklore, a big creature of the mountains that turns to stone in sunlight.
- letra: V
  termos:
  - id: valhalla
    termo: Valhalla
    def: Odin’s hall in Asgard, where the chosen warriors go. In Old Norse, Valhöll, “hall of the slain”.
  - id: valkyrie
    termo: Valkyrie
    def: A woman who chooses who dies in battle and takes the warriors to Valhalla. In Old Norse, valkyrja.
  - id: vanir
    termo: Vanir
    def: 'The second group of gods, tied to fertility and wealth: Njörðr, Freyr and Freyja. They fought the Æsir before joining them.'
  - id: vinland
    termo: Vinland
    def: The land the Norse found in North America around the year 1000, after the voyages of Leif Eriksson.
  - id: volva
    termo: Völva
    def: A seeress who traveled from place to place foretelling the future. The Völuspá is the prophecy of a völva.
- letra: Y
  termos:
  - id: yggdrasil
    termo: Yggdrasil
    def: The great tree, an ash, that holds up the nine worlds, with roots in Asgard, Jotunheim and Niflheim.
