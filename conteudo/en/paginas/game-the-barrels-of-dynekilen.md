## How to play

At Dynekilen in 1716, Tordenskiold attacked a Swedish fleet carrying ammunition. Here you catch the barrels of ammunition in the ship's hold and avoid the cannonballs. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the ship's hold and catch the barrels of ammunition. Avoid the cannonballs: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

On 8 July 1716, Tordenskiold attacked the Swedish transport fleet, carrying ammunition and supplies, at Dynekilen and captured or destroyed about 30 ships. The barrels and the cannonballs in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
