## How to play

Saxo and Sven Aggesen link Þyri to the Danevirke wall, which protected Denmark from southern invaders. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it onto the wall. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Saxo and Sven Aggesen, Þyri ordered the Danevirke wall built, but archaeology shows that the first phase uses trees felled in 737 AD and that the defences were extended until the twelfth century. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Þyri Danebod: the queen of the Jelling stone and the legend of the Danevirke wall](/thyri-danebod-the-queen-of-the-jelling-stone-and-the-legend/).
