## How to play

In the Bisclaretz ljóð, the wife has the baron's clothes stolen, and without them he cannot return to human form. Here you run through the forest in three lanes, recover the clothes and dodge the king's hounds. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the baron's clothes and dodge the king's hounds: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Bisclaretz ljóð, from the Strengleikar and the Norwegian version of Marie de France's Bisclavret, a baron becomes a wolf three days a week and keeps his clothes beside a stone. His wife has them stolen and marries another knight. When the king's hounds corner the wolf, he kneels before the king, who takes him to court. There he attacks the new husband and the wife, she confesses, and the clothes are returned. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
