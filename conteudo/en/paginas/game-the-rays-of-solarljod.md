## How to play

Sólarljóð means "Song of the Sun". A dead father speaks to his son, and stanzas 39 to 45 give the poem its name. Drag your finger or mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the silver mirror and catch the rays of the sun. Avoid the dark clouds: each one that lands there is a mistake. Whatever hits the ground costs you nothing. Catch 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Sólarljóð was written in Iceland around 1200, in the ljóðaháttr metre, but has a Christian outlook. The rays and clouds in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
