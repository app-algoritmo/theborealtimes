## How to play

Around 1070, King Olaf Kyrre began a great stone church in Nidaros, with the altar over the grave of Saint Olaf. Here you stack the stones of the church with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it over the grave of Saint Olaf. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Olaf III Haraldsson, the Kyrre, "the Peaceful", reigned from 1067 to 1093 and did not organise great expeditions of conquest. According to the tradition going back to Snorri, he founded Bergen around 1070. In Nidaros he began, around 1070, a great stone church dedicated to the Holy Trinity, about fifty metres long, the largest in Norway at the time. Bishops came to have fixed seats in Oslo, Nidaros and Bergen. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
