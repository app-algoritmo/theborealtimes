## How to play

According to the sources, Niels Ebbesen entered Randers at night with 47 men. Here you block with the shield every blow from the count's mercenaries. Blows come from the left, the centre or the right. Tap the third of the screen the blow comes from, or use the arrow keys or keys 1, 2 and 3, to block. Blocking the wrong side, or too late, is a mistake. Block 14 blows before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the sources, on the night of 1 April 1340 the squire Niels Ebbesen entered Randers with 47 men and killed Count Gerhard III of Holstein, who ruled Jutland; he died in November that year. The blows in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Niels Ebbesen and the death of Count Gerhard in Randers in 1340: the forty-seven men, the bridge over the Guden river and the Danish hero](/niels-ebbesen-and-the-death-of-count-gerhard-in-randers/).
