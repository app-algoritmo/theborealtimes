## How to play

Inside Vipunen, Väinämöinen set up a forge: the armour is the workshop, the sleeves are the bellows, the knees are the anvil and the forearm is the hammer. Four stations stand before you, and heat builds up in one at a time. Tap the station to strike when its bar passes the mark, or use the arrow keys. Tapping a station with no heat, or striking too early, is a mistake. If the heat passes the full bar, it is lost, and that is also a mistake. Strike 12 times before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In Kalevala rune 17, in Crawford's translation, Väinämöinen, swallowed by Vipunen, sets up a forge inside the giant: the armour is the workshop, the sleeves are the bellows, the knees are the anvil and the forearm is the hammer. He forges for three days. The four stations, the heat that builds up and the pace of the game are invention.
