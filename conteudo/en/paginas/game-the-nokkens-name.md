## How to play

The nøkken rises from the lake toward the child. Tap the letters N, Ø, K, K, in that order, while they float on the water. A wrong letter makes it advance faster. Drive it off in three rounds; in each one, the letters move faster.

## The story behind it

In Norwegian folklore, the nøkken is a water spirit that lures people to drown. In some accounts, knowing its name or using iron drives it off. The letter game is made up.
