## How to play

Tap a square to dig. Each number tells how many of the neighbouring squares hide a trap. Use the numbers to find the safe squares and dig them all. Two traps stepped on or 180 seconds end the game. Use the button to switch between digging and marking a square. (Keyboard: arrows move, Space digs, F marks.)

## The story behind it

In the Norse sources, dwarfs are smiths of the gods' jewels and weapons. The mine and the traps are invented. Read the story in [The dwarfs of the Edda: maggots in Ymir's flesh, smiths of the gods and stones at sunrise](/en/2027/01/03/anoes-dvergar-edda-ymir-ferreiros/).
