## How to play

The Gutasaga tells of the enchanted island, the three sons of Hafþi and the departure of those chosen by lot. Take each passage to the right part. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

The Gutasaga, from the 13th century, survives in Codex Holm. B 64, from about 1350, beside the Gutalag, the law code of Gotland. The boxes in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
