## How to play

In Fólkvangr, Freyja chooses half of the slain warriors, and Odin keeps the other half. Nine grassy mounds stand before you in a field. A golden glow appears on one mound at a time, marking one of her choices. Tap the mound that glows before the glow fades, or use keys 1 to 9. Tapping a mound with no glow, or letting a glow fade, is a mistake. Follow 12 choices before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the Grímnismál and the Gylfaginning (ch. 24), Freyja chooses half of those who die in battle, who go to Fólkvangr, and Odin takes the other half. No passage describes the field. The nine mounds, the glows and the pace of the game are invention.
