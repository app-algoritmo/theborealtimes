## How to play

In the Gylfaginning, Hel's servants are called Ganglati and Ganglöt, names that suggest slow walking. Here you mark their slow step in the hall of Éljúðnir. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to walk with Ganglati. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In chapter 34 of the Gylfaginning, Snorri calls Hel's hall Éljúðnir, its threshold Fallanda forað, the male servant Ganglati and the maidservant Ganglöt. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
