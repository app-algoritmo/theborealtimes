## How to play

In the tale, Boots runs after the seven foals over hills and valleys, holding on to the tail of one of them. Run along three lanes. Tap the left or right half of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lane. Collect the flasks of wine and dodge the rocks: hitting them is a mistake. Collect 14 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to the tale by Asbjørnsen and Moe, Boots runs after the foals over hills and valleys, holding on to the tail of one of them, and brings the king the wine and the wafer from the church. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [The Seven Foals: the Norwegian tale of the lad in the ashes who ran after seven foals and broke a troll's spell](/the-seven-foals-the-norwegian-tale-of-the-lad-in-the-ashes/).
