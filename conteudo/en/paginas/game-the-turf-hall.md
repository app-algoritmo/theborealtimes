## How to play

In the sagas, Ketill Flatnose rules the Hebrides and is the father of Auðr the Deep-Minded. Here you stack the turf blocks of a hall on the islands with precision. A block slides from side to side. Tap the screen, or press Space, to drop it on the foundation of the hall. Whatever overhangs falls off and the stack gets narrower, but never below a minimum. Missing the stack completely is a mistake. Stack 10 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the sagas, Ketill Flatnose (Ketill flatnefr) is lord of the Hebrides in the 9th century and father of Auðr the Deep-Minded; the sources tell his conflict with Harald Fairhair in different ways. The turf blocks in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
