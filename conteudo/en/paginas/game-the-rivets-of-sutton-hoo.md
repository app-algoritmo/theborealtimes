## How to play

In 1939, on the third day of digging Mound 1, Basil Brown found the first ship rivet. Here you tap the iron rivets and do not tap the stones. Tap the rivets as soon as it appears, or use keys 1 to 6. Do not tap the stones: that is a mistake. Whatever disappears by itself costs you nothing. Get 12 before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In May 1939, on the third day of digging Mound 1 at Sutton Hoo, Basil Brown's team found the first rivet of a ship about 27 m long. The rivets and the stones in the game are invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention. Read the story in [Sutton Hoo: the 27-metre ship buried in Suffolk, the helmet like those of Vendel and Valsgärde, 37 gold coins and the occupant whose body was never found](/sutton-hoo-the-27-metre-ship-buried-in-suffolk-the-helmet/).
