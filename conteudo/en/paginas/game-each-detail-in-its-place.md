## How to play

Four bronze dies from Öland have a find, scenes and readings that scholars debate. A sentence appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box it belongs to, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is “The find”, the second is “What they show” and the third is “Readings and doubts”. A wrong box, or a sentence left unanswered, is a mistake. Match 10 sentences before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

According to Wikipedia, the four Torslunda dies were found in 1870 and were used to make decorative foils for helmets. The link with Odin and wolf warriors is an interpretation, not a certainty. The queue of sentences, the three boxes and the pace of the game are invention.
