## How to play

Snorri goði appears in three Icelandic sagas. An act of his appears at the top of the screen. Tap the box of the saga that tells it, or use keys 1, 2 and 3: the first is the Eyrbyggja saga, the second is the Laxdæla saga and the third is Njáls saga. A wrong box, or an act left unanswered, is a mistake. Link 10 acts before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Eyrbyggja saga, Snorri buys Helgafell, has the church built and swaps properties with Guðrún. In the Laxdæla saga, he helps to make peace between the Ólafssons and Guðrún's sons. In Njáls saga, he backs Njáll's kinsmen at the Althing and is called the wisest man in Iceland. The row of acts, the three boxes and the rhythm of the game are invention.
