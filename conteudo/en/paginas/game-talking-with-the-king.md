## How to play

In the Ívars þáttr Ingimundarsonar, King Eysteinn proposes to Ívarr, downcast over his brother's marriage to the woman he loved, that they talk about her every day. Here you mark the rhythm of the conversation, day after day. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to talk with the king, day after day. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Ívars þáttr Ingimundarsonar, a tale in the Morkinskinna, the Icelander Ívarr, at the court of King Eysteinn of Norway, asks his brother Þorfinnr to tell the woman he loves to wait, but Þorfinnr does not and marries her. Downcast, Ívarr receives the king's proposal that they talk about her every day, and slowly recovers. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
