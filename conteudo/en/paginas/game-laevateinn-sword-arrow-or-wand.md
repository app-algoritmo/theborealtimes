## How to play

Nobody knows what Lævateinn, the weapon of the Fjölsvinnsmál, is. Scholars have read it as a sword, an arrow or a wand. Each phrase falls from above. Guide it to the right box: tap the box, or use the arrow keys and keys 1, 2 and 3 (Space drops it faster). The wrong box is a mistake. Get 7 right before time runs out. 3 mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Fjölsvinnsmál, only Lævateinn kills the rooster Viðófnir, and the weapon is kept with nine locks. The poem does not say what it is, and so scholars have read it as a sword, an arrow or a wand.
