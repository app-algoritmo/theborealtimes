## How to play

Each ring of the fort has an opening. Tap a ring on its left side to turn it one step to the left, or on its right side to turn it to the right. Line up the openings of all the rings in a single path to the centre, without going over the number of turns. Time is limited. There are 3 forts.

## The story behind it

Eketorp is a circular fort on Öland, in Sweden, used from the Iron Age to the 13th century. Turning rings is invented. Read the story in [Eketorp: the ring fort of Öland, in Sweden, from the Iron Age to the 13th century](/en/2027/02/28/eketorp-o-forte-circular-de-oland-na-suecia-da-idade-do-ferro-ao-seculo-xiii/).
