## How to play

Enemy ships sail up the Arousa estuary. A power gauge swings back and forth at the bottom of the screen, and the green mark shows the right spot for the ship. Tap the screen, or press Space, when the gauge is over the mark, to launch a stone from the tower. A stone too short or too long is a mistake, and so is a ship reaching the shore. Drive off 8 ships before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

At Catoira in Galicia, the Western Towers guarded the entrance to the Arousa estuary against attacks from the sea, and every August the Viking Pilgrimage reenacts the attack and the defence. The stone throwing is invented. Read the story in [Catoira: the Torres de Oeste and the Viking Pilgrimage of Galicia](/catoira-the-torres-de-oeste-and-the-viking-pilgrimage/).
