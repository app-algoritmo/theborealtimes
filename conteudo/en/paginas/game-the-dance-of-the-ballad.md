## How to play

Faroese kvæði were sung to accompany dancing. Here you mark the dance step in time with the ballad. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to mark the step of the ballad dance. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

Ormurin Langi is a Faroese ballad about Olaf Tryggvason and the battle of Svolder, composed around 1830 by Jens Christian Djurhuus and standardised by Hammershaimb in 1891. Kvæði were sung to accompany dancing. The step in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
