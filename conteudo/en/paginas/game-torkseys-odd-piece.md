## How to play

Each round shows almost identical gaming pieces: one of them has a slightly different shade. Tap it before the round time runs out. Every round brings more pieces and a smaller difference. Complete 10 rounds with at most 2 mistakes. A wrong tap or running out of time counts as a mistake. (Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space picks.)

## The story behind it

In the winter of 872–873, the Great Army camped at Torksey, Lincolnshire, and left more than 300 lead gaming pieces. The rounds are invented. Read the story in [Torksey, 872–873: the winter camp of the Great Army that looked like a town on the move](/en/2026/12/27/torksey-acampamento-de-inverno-grande-exercito-872/).
