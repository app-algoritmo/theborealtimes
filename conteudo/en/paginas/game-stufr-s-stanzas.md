## How to play

In the Morkinskinna, the blind poet Stúfr recites stanza after stanza before King Haraldr. Here you mark the rhythm of the poem, one stanza at a time. A marker runs back and forth along the bar. Tap the screen, or press Space, when it is in the green zone to recite the stanzas to the king. Hitting the very middle is worth two points. Outside the zone is a mistake. The zone shrinks and the marker speeds up. Score 12 points before time runs out. Three mistakes end the game.

## The story behind it

In the Stúfs þáttr, of the Morkinskinna, the blind Icelandic poet Stúfr recites stanzas to King Haraldr Sigurðarson, who arrives unannounced at the house where he is lodging, and ends up accepted at court, in Nidaros. The Morkinskinna was composed around 1220. The rhythm in the game is invented. The game itself, with its objects and rules, is invention.
